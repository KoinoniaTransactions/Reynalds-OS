# OBJ-00000024 — Transaction Management Pricing Rule

Applies to: OBJ-00000019 — Transaction Management  
Status: Pre-Launch Working Pricing Rule  
Effective Date: 2026-09-03

## Standard Price

**$450 per successful closing**

## Billing

Billed/collected at successful closing under the approved payment workflow.

## No-Close Rule

If the transaction does not successfully close, no standard Transaction Management coordination fee is due under this launch rule.

## Scope

The standard price covers normal Koinonia contract-to-close coordination for one standard transaction lifecycle as defined in the applicable service/product scope. Routine emails, reminders, participant follow-up, deadline tracking, and standard file touches are not separately micro-billed.

## Exceptions

A materially nonstandard transaction may be scoped separately before work expands when complexity, multiple related properties/contracts, institutional/commercial/new-construction requirements, or extraordinary dispute administration materially changes the delivery economics.

Do not use exceptions to create routine add-on charges.

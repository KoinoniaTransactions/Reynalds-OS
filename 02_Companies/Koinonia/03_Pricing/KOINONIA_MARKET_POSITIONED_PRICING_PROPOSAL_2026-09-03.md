# Koinonia Market-Positioned Pricing Proposal — 2026-09-03

Status: Working Recommendation / Owner Review Required  
Owner: Koinonia  
Purpose: Recommend simple market-competitive pricing for the five core Koinonia products while preserving white-glove positioning and enough margin to deliver the promised service standard.

This proposal does not replace canonical pricing rules until approved and reconciled.

---

# 1. Pricing Position

Koinonia should not compete as the cheapest provider.

The target position is:

> **Competitive with established real-estate support providers, priced modestly above commodity/task-market options, and clearly below the cost and complexity of assembling multiple specialists or hiring dedicated staff.**

White-glove should be expressed through:

- breadth of support;
- continuity across the Realtor lifecycle;
- proactive communication;
- quality control;
- one accountable relationship;
- integration between listings, transactions, field coverage, marketing, CRM, and operations;
- fewer handoffs and less vendor management for the Realtor.

White-glove should **not** mean arbitrarily charging 30–50% above market for the same commodity service.

---

# 2. Market Benchmarks Reviewed — September 2026

## Transaction coordination

Public examples reviewed:

- Colorado Transaction Coordinator: $400 per transaction.
- TransActive Colorado: $425 per transaction, no closing/no fee.
- Midnight Orchid Colorado: $400 per file.
- Key Support Solutions: $450 per side, paid at closing, zero fee if the deal falls through.
- NCTLC: $449 standard TC.
- Agent Ally Group: $395 transaction-to-close.

Observed competitive center:

> **Approximately $400–$450 per standard file.**

A broader industry 2026 range is commonly around $350–$500 for standard contract-to-close work.

## Listing coordination

Public examples reviewed:

- Key Support Solutions: $150 listing coordination.
- Midnight Orchid: $200 listing coordination.
- NCTLC: $195 Lite / $299 Standard / $379 Rush.
- Smooth Closing Solutions: $199 pre-listing.
- Agent Ally Group: broader listing/transaction offers up to $795 for premium end-to-end work.

Observed basic listing-launch range:

> **Approximately $150–$300.**

Broader listing-to-close / premium coordination reaches roughly:

> **$550–$795.**

## Licensed field coverage

Public examples reviewed:

- Showami reports a national average around $55 per showing, with variable pricing based on notice/distance/time.
- MyShowAgent publishes $75 for a single showing and $115/hour for open-house hosting.
- ShowingMarket provides examples such as an $85 buyer showing and $220 hosted open house.
- Lower-cost marketplace products exist around $35 but represent a more commodity/on-demand model.

Market interpretation:

> **$55–$75 is a defensible range for a standard showing, with managed/white-glove service supporting the upper end.**

## Marketing management

Public examples reviewed:

- Coffee & Contracts: $74/month — templates/content platform, not managed execution.
- Restate Social: $197/month Pro and $467/month Elite for done-for-you marketing/content execution.
- Curaytor: $1,300/month for platform plus ongoing marketing support/team execution.
- Broader 2026 real-estate social/marketing services range from low-cost template tools to $1,000+ managed services.

Market interpretation:

> **A Koinonia managed marketing offer around $700–$800/month fits between lower-cost content services and higher-cost full marketing platforms/agencies.**

## Business / operational support

Public examples reviewed:

- Managed real-estate VA providers commonly publish roughly $900–$1,700/month and higher depending on hours, staffing, language, lead generation, CRM, and support level.
- Generic VA economics are not a direct comparison to Koinonia because Koinonia is intended to combine real-estate operations, marketing, CRM, and continuity with its other services.

Market interpretation:

> **A $1,200–$1,300/month integrated Koinonia Partnership can be competitive if it includes meaningful recurring marketing + CRM/business operations rather than merely a small block of assistant hours.**

# 3. Recommended Public Pricing

Use simple round professional-service pricing rather than excessive charm pricing.

## Transaction Management

### Recommended public price

# **$450 per transaction**

Recommended billing rule:

- billed at successful closing;
- no coordination fee if the transaction fails to close under the standard no-close/no-fee rule;
- card/payment authorization on file during onboarding;
- exceptional/custom transaction types can be scoped separately where materially different.

### Why $450

- directly inside the current competitive market;
- matches a published market price from another provider;
- only modestly above $400–$425 Colorado competitors;
- simple to remember and explain;
- removes the current $389 versus $599 front-end complexity;
- avoids a $599 pay-at-closing price that is difficult to defend when several competitors already provide no-close/no-fee around $425–$450.

### Recommended change from current model

Retire the two-price public presentation:

- $389 prepaid;
- $599 pay at closing.

Replace with one standard public price:

> **$450 — paid at closing. No close, no coordination fee.**

If Koinonia later wants a volume/prepayment incentive, keep it as a private/client-specific commercial tool rather than a second primary website price.

# 4. Hand Us the Listing

### Recommended public price

# **$350 per listing**

Billing rule:

- charged when Koinonia begins the listing engagement / approved launch work;
- fee is for listing-side operational management through accepted-offer handoff;
- once the listing goes under contract, standard Transaction Management begins at $450 and is billed at successful closing;
- approved outside property/media expenses are additional.

### Total listing-to-close Koinonia fees when property closes

- Hand Us the Listing: $350
- Transaction Management: $450

# **Total Koinonia service fees: $800**

Outside expenses remain separate.

### Why $350

Basic listing coordination is commonly $150–$300, but Koinonia's proposed scope is intentionally broader:

- intake and documents;
- launch planning;
- vendor/media coordination;
- MLS/setup support;
- base listing marketing;
- active listing administration;
- feedback/seller update support;
- accepted-offer handoff.

At $350, Koinonia sits above basic launch coordinators but still lands at roughly $800 listing-to-close, essentially at the upper edge of current premium/full-service competitive offers rather than far beyond them.

That is appropriate for white-glove positioning.

### Outside expenses

Additional only when required and approved:

- photography;
- drone/video;
- floor plans / 3D;
- staging;
- cleaning/repairs/landscaping;
- printing/postage;
- ad spend;
- other specialty property services.

No separate Koinonia photographer-coordination fee, sign-coordination fee, etc. when normal coordination is already part of Hand Us the Listing.

# 5. Licensed Field Coverage

## Standard showing / access assignment

### Recommended starting price

# **$75 per standard assignment**

Standard assignment concept:

- one property;
- normal local travel within base service area;
- ordinary appointment duration;
- adequate notice;
- standard access/feedback requirements.

Final price is confirmed before booking if timing, distance, duration, or complexity materially changes the economics.

### Why $75

- Showami reports an average around $55;
- managed competitors publish $75 for single showings;
- Koinonia's proposition includes coordination, instructions, accountability, feedback, and continuity with the Realtor relationship;
- $75 is a defensible premium over a marketplace while still very accessible compared with the value of preserving a client appointment.

## Open House Coverage

### Recommended public price

# **$200 standard open house**

Working standard scope:

- up to 3 hours of hosting;
- standard setup/teardown;
- approved property information/materials;
- sign-in/lead capture workflow;
- completion/lead handoff summary;
- normal local service-area travel.

Extended/custom events quoted before booking.

### Why retain $200

- existing Koinonia price is already easy to understand;
- marketplace examples around $220 exist;
- some managed services publish substantially higher hourly rates;
- $200 remains competitive while still supporting a white-glove presentation.

## Other field assignments

Inspection/appraisal/vendor access, property checks, lockbox/key tasks, multi-property tours, and extended assignments should use the same **Licensed Field Coverage** product with the price confirmed before booking.

Do not publish a giant task-specific matrix at launch.

# 6. Marketing Management

### Recommended recurring price

# **$750 per month**

This should be one primary managed-marketing plan at launch rather than three artificial tiers.

## Working standard recurring scope to support this price

The exact production counts should remain contractual/operational, but the customer experience should include:

- monthly marketing priorities/content plan;
- approximately 8–12 core content pieces/month using efficient reusable production systems;
- captions/copy;
- branded graphics/carousels;
- faceless or templated short-form content where appropriate;
- publishing/scheduling to agreed channels;
- one recurring email/newsletter rhythm;
- basic database/review/referral campaign support;
- listing/open-house lifecycle integration using information already available through Koinonia;
- concise monthly performance summary;
- ongoing coordination/approval workflow.

## Why $750

It places Koinonia:

- clearly above template/content-library services;
- above lightweight done-for-you plans around $197–$467;
- materially below a $1,300 managed platform/team product like Curaytor;
- in a range that supports meaningful human management rather than commodity content delivery.

### Outside expenses

Additional when used and approved:

- ad spend;
- printing/postage;
- professional photo/video/drone;
- premium production;
- specialty software/client-specific platform costs;
- other specialist execution.

## One-time marketing projects

Use quoted project pricing.

Do not publish a large project price menu until actual demand shows repeatable project types worth standardizing.

# 7. Koinonia Partnership

### Recommended recurring price

# **$1,250 per month**

## Recommended positioning

> **The managed support relationship for Realtors who want both recurring marketing and the business operations behind it handled through Koinonia.**

## Recommended standard foundation

Include the standard Marketing Management relationship plus a defined recurring CRM/business-operations layer such as:

- CRM/pipeline maintenance;
- next-action/follow-up tracking;
- past-client/review/referral workflows;
- database segmentation;
- task/calendar/workflow organization;
- recurring communication/admin support;
- process/checklist/SOP maintenance;
- vendor coordination;
- recurring business/marketing review;
- priority service relationship.

## What remains separately priced by default

- Transaction Management — $450 per successful closing;
- Hand Us the Listing — $350 per listing;
- Licensed Field Coverage — standard rates;
- large custom projects outside partnership scope;
- approved outside expenses.

Do not include unlimited transactions/listings/field labor in the $1,250 base plan.

## Why $1,250

- similar to the price of some generic real-estate VA services;
- below or around higher-end managed marketing platforms that may provide marketing alone;
- provides a materially different value proposition because Koinonia combines marketing + CRM + recurring business operations + continuity with its transaction/listing/field services;
- enough room to deliver a true managed relationship instead of selling a token 3-hour/month support package.

## Website presentation

Do not lead with hours.

Internally, Koinonia should still set a monthly capacity target and review sustained overuse.

# 9. Unit-Economics Guardrails

These are management targets, not client-facing promises.

## Transaction Management — $450

Target direct Koinonia delivery cost:

> **$180 or less on a normal completed file**

This supports roughly a 60% gross contribution before broader overhead on completed files.

No-close risk must be monitored at portfolio level.

At a 10% failed-file rate, $450 no-close/no-fee produces approximately $405 average collected revenue per opened file before considering that failed files typically consume less than full closing-cycle labor.

If the failure rate or delivery time materially exceeds assumptions, adjust operations before immediately increasing public price.

## Hand Us the Listing — $350

Target direct Koinonia delivery cost:

> **Approximately $140–$160 or less on a standard listing**

The biggest economic risk is active-listing duration and repeated relaunch/custom seller concierge work.

Use scope-change rules for materially different engagements rather than charging routine micro-fees.

## Licensed Field Coverage — $75

Field coverage naturally carries lower percentage margin because a licensed provider must be paid for physical labor/travel.

Target standard assignment provider/direct cost:

> **Ideally $45–$50 or less where market conditions allow**

This leaves roughly $25–$30 gross contribution for Koinonia coordination/payment/coverage infrastructure.

Do not force coverage at a loss simply to preserve the advertised starting price; quote the actual assignment before booking when local supply/distance/timing requires more.

## Open House — $200

Target total direct field-provider/assignment cost:

> **Approximately $130–$150 or less for a standard event**

This produces a lower but acceptable strategic gross contribution while helping Koinonia own more of the Realtor relationship.

## Marketing Management — $750/month

Target direct monthly delivery cost:

> **Approximately $300–$340 or less**

This requires templates, reusable workflows, event-driven content, efficient approvals, and disciplined scope.

Do not promise high-touch custom agency production at this price without separate project scope.

## Koinonia Partnership — $1,250/month

Target direct monthly delivery cost:

> **Approximately $500–$575 or less**

The plan should be designed so the combined marketing + CRM/business operations workload remains within that direct-cost envelope on average.

High-variable products remain separately priced to protect the economics.

# 10. White-Glove Without Overpricing

Koinonia should deliberately avoid two bad positions:

## Too cheap

Examples:

- $250 TC;
- $35 field coverage;
- $197/month managed marketing;
- $299/month broad partnership.

This would force Koinonia into volume/automation economics inconsistent with the promised service experience.

## Too expensive before proof

Examples:

- $599 standard TC when strong competitors offer $400–$450;
- $500+ listing-side fee before contract for standard residential listings;
- $1,500+ marketing plan at launch without mature case studies;
- $2,000+ partnership before Koinonia demonstrates sufficient recurring value.

Koinonia can raise prices later when demand, outcomes, capacity constraints, client retention, and brand proof support it.

# 11. Recommended Launch Strategy

Treat these as **standard launch prices**, not permanent ceilings.

Recommended review triggers:

- first 20–30 completed transactions;
- first 10–15 Hand Us the Listing engagements;
- first 50 field assignments;
- first 5 recurring Marketing Management clients with at least 90 days of delivery data;
- first 5 Koinonia Partnership clients with at least 90 days of delivery data.

At review, measure:

- average labor/coordination time;
- provider costs;
- failed/cancelled-file rate;
- client acquisition/conversion rate;
- retention;
- scope creep;
- client satisfaction;
- gross contribution;
- competitor movement;
- demand/capacity pressure.

Raise prices because the data supports it, not merely because Koinonia uses the phrase white-glove.

# OBJ-00000028 — Koinonia Partnership Pricing Rule

Applies to: OBJ-00000023 — Koinonia Partnership  
Status: Pre-Launch Working Pricing Rule  
Effective Date: 2026-09-03

## Standard Price

**$1,250 per month**

## Billing

Recurring monthly billing under the approved client agreement/payment workflow.

## Standard Economic Scope

The fee includes the standard Marketing Management relationship plus an agreed recurring CRM/business-operations layer within the defined partnership capacity.

The product is sold as managed recurring support and continuity, not as unlimited assistant access or an unlimited hourly bank.

## Separately Priced by Default

Unless a future written package explicitly changes the rule:

- Transaction Management remains separately priced;
- Hand Us the Listing remains separately priced;
- Licensed Field Coverage remains separately priced;
- Professional Open House Coverage remains separately priced;
- large custom projects remain separately scoped;
- approved outside expenses remain additional.

## Capacity Rule

Internal/contractual capacity boundaries are required. If usage consistently and materially exceeds the agreed support level, Koinonia should review the client's plan/scope rather than create numerous surprise micro-fees.

## Outside Expenses

Variable approved media, advertising, print/postage, specialty provider, software, and other third-party costs remain additional unless specifically included in writing.

# OBJ-00000016 Contract & Document Support Pricing Rule

Class: Pricing Rule
Type: Service Pricing Rule
Module: Koinonia
Status: Active
Version: 1.0
Owner: Koinonia
Effective Date: 2026-08-15

Applies to:
- OBJ-00000003 Contract Preparation & Writing Service

Pricing:
- Single-document support starts at $75.
- Multi-document, offer-package, urgent, or materially expanded requests are scope-based.

Billing Rule:
The agreed price and included document scope must be confirmed before work begins.

Scope Rule:
Pricing reflects document-preparation workflow based on Realtor instructions. The Realtor remains responsible for terms, advice, negotiation decisions, brokerage compliance, and final approval.

Governance:
Do not publish or quote an automatic discount, package discount, or recurring-client discount unless it is separately documented in an approved pricing rule or custom support variation.

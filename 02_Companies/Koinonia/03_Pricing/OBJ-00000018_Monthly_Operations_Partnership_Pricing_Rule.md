# OBJ-00000018 Monthly Operations Partnership Pricing Rule

Class: Pricing Rule
Type: Service Pricing Rule
Module: Koinonia
Status: Active
Version: 1.0
Owner: Koinonia
Effective Date: 2026-08-15

Applies to:
- OBJ-00000005 Business Operations Support Service

Approved Monthly Tiers:
- Starter: $299/month, up to 3 hours.
- Growth: $599/month, up to 7 hours.
- Partner: $999/month, up to 12 hours.

Scope Rule:
Monthly Operations Partnership is capacity-based and scope-based. It is not unlimited assistant access.

Billing Rule:
The selected tier, included operational scope, exclusions, and review cadence must be documented in the client engagement.

Add-On Governance:
No automatic or unpublished discount applies to Contract & Document Support or other Koinonia services merely because a client is on a monthly tier. Any discounted add-on must be documented in a separately approved pricing rule or custom support variation before it is marketed or quoted.

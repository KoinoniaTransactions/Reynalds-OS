# OBJ-00000029 — Custom Project & Outside Expense Pricing Rule

Applies to: Approved Koinonia custom projects and variable third-party expenses  
Status: Pre-Launch Working Pricing Rule  
Effective Date: 2026-09-03

## Custom Project Rule

When a legitimate Realtor need does not fit the standard scope of a named product, Koinonia may define one project outcome and quote one clear Koinonia fee before work begins.

Do not expose or rely on a customer-facing task-by-task micro-price sheet unless a repeatable project type later justifies standardization.

## Outside Expense Rule

Koinonia service fees pay for Koinonia's approved coordination, management, production, follow-through, and accountability.

Variable third-party costs remain additional when required, including professional media, advertising spend, printing/postage, staging, repairs, specialty software, premium production, and other approved outside services.

## Approval

Material outside expenses must be confirmed with the client before purchase unless the client has provided a standing written authorization/budget covering the expense.

## Margin / Markup Principle

Default commercial direction is to earn Koinonia's margin through the visible Koinonia service/management fee rather than hidden vendor markups. Any exception must be explicitly documented in the applicable written package/engagement.

## Client-Facing Language

Preferred concise explanation:

> **Outside expenses are additional only when needed. Koinonia coordinates the work and confirms the applicable cost before purchase.**

Do not frame the white-glove experience as a directory of vendors the Realtor must source or manage.

# OBJ-00000017 Licensed Showing Coverage Pricing Rule

Class: Pricing Rule
Type: Service Pricing Rule
Module: Koinonia
Status: Active
Version: 1.0
Owner: Koinonia
Effective Date: 2026-08-15

Applies to:
- OBJ-00000004 Licensed Showing Coverage Service

Pricing:
- Local Licensed Showing Coverage starts at $50.
- Distance, access complexity, urgent timing, extended showing time, multiple-property coverage, or materially expanded scope may change pricing.

Billing Rule:
The requesting Realtor must receive and accept the quoted price/scope before the showing is accepted when the request falls outside the standard local starting scope.

Governance:
Do not advertise every mileage, rush, or complexity variation as a fixed public fee unless a separate approved pricing rule is created. Public materials should use the approved local starting point and state that nonstandard scope may affect pricing.

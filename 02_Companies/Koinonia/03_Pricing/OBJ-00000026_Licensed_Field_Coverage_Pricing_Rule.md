# OBJ-00000026 — Licensed Field Coverage Pricing Rule

Applies to: OBJ-00000021 — Licensed Field Coverage  
Status: Pre-Launch Working Pricing Rule  
Effective Date: 2026-09-03

## Standard Starting Price

**From $75 per standard assignment**

## Professional Open House

**$200 per standard event**

Working standard event scope: up to approximately three hours of hosting with standard setup/teardown, approved property materials/information, lead-capture/handoff process, and normal local travel within the approved service-area assumptions.

## Assignment Pricing Principle

Field economics legitimately vary. The final assignment price must be confirmed before booking when any of these materially change the standard scope:

- distance/travel;
- short notice/rush;
- evening/weekend/holiday staffing;
- multiple properties;
- extended duration;
- unusual access/logistics;
- assignment complexity.

Do not publish or maintain a large customer-facing mileage/time matrix unless usage data proves it improves the buying experience.

## Included in Confirmed Assignment Fee

Normal request intake, access/appointment confirmation, Realtor instruction review, assignment/provider coordination, standard task performance, completion feedback/documentation, and normal local travel within the base pricing assumptions.

## Outside Expenses

Unusual parking, tolls, externally purchased materials, or other direct assignment-specific costs may be additional when disclosed and approved. Routine local travel should be embedded in normal pricing rather than itemized whenever practical.

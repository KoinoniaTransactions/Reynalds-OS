# OBJ-00000010 Pricing Rules Catalog

Class: Master Object
Type: Pricing Rules Catalog
Module: Koinonia
Status: Active
Version: 1.0
Owner: Koinonia
Memory Level: Foundational

Purpose:
The Pricing Rules Catalog is the single source of truth for how Koinonia pricing is defined, presented, billed, discounted, customized, and reported.

Approved pricing rules:
- OBJ-00000011 Prepaid Pricing Rule
- OBJ-00000012 Pay-at-Closing Pricing Rule
- OBJ-00000013 Custom Support Pricing Rule

Governance:
No service or package may be quoted without a pricing rule. Pricing changes require versioning and change log entry.

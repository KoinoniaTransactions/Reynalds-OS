## Purpose

The Pricing Rules Catalog is the single source of truth for how Koinonia commercial products are priced, presented, billed, scoped, and paired with approved outside expenses.

The current pricing philosophy is intentionally simple:

> **Simple Koinonia service fee. Variable outside costs only when the work requires them. No task-by-task nickel-and-diming.**

## Current Working Launch Pricing Rules

- **OBJ-00000024 — Transaction Management Pricing Rule**  
  Working launch price: **$450 per successful closing**. No successful closing, no standard Transaction Management coordination fee.

- **OBJ-00000025 — Hand Us the Listing Pricing Rule**  
  Working launch price: **$350 per listing** through accepted-offer handoff. Transaction Management begins separately when the listing enters contract.

- **OBJ-00000026 — Licensed Field Coverage Pricing Rule**  
  Working launch pricing: **from $75 per standard assignment**; **$200 per standard professional open-house event**. Material distance/timing/duration/complexity differences are confirmed before booking.

- **OBJ-00000027 — Marketing Management Pricing Rule**  
  Working launch price: **$750 per month** for the defined recurring managed-marketing scope.

- **OBJ-00000028 — Koinonia Partnership Pricing Rule**  
  Working launch price: **$1,250 per month** for the standard managed marketing + agreed CRM/business-operations relationship.

- **OBJ-00000029 — Custom Project & Outside Expense Pricing Rule**  
  Defined custom projects are quoted before work begins. Variable approved third-party costs are additional only when required and are confirmed before purchase unless standing written authorization applies.

## Outside Expense Principles

1. Koinonia earns its service/management margin through the visible Koinonia fee by default.
2. Photography, video/drone, staging, printing/postage, ad spend, repairs, specialty software, and similar variable third-party costs are separate unless a written product expressly includes them.
3. Do not make the Realtor source/manage those providers merely because the cost is separate; Koinonia may coordinate approved outside services within product scope.
4. Material outside spend requires client approval unless standing written authorization/budget exists.
5. Avoid hidden vendor markups by default. Any different written commercial arrangement must be explicit.

## Governance

1. No product may be publicly quoted without an applicable current pricing rule or approved project quote.
2. Pre-launch prices are not automatically public until the related product's fulfillment/compliance readiness is approved.
3. Pricing changes require versioning/change history.
4. Routine work already included in a product should not be broken into micro-fees.
5. Material scope changes should be confirmed before expanded work proceeds.
6. Sustained recurring over-capacity should trigger a plan/scope review rather than surprise micro-billing.

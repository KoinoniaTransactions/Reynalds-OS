# OBJ-00000025 — Hand Us the Listing Pricing Rule

Applies to: OBJ-00000020 — Hand Us the Listing  
Status: Pre-Launch Working Pricing Rule  
Effective Date: 2026-09-03

## Standard Price

**$350 per listing**

## Billing

The Koinonia listing-management fee is earned according to the approved engagement/payment terms once Koinonia begins the defined listing-side work. Final payment timing must be reflected in the client agreement/onboarding workflow before launch.

## Included Economic Scope

The fee covers normal Koinonia coordination/execution for one standard listing engagement through accepted-offer handoff, including the defined base listing-marketing layer.

Do not separately micro-bill normal photographer scheduling, sign/lockbox coordination, basic listing marketing setup, routine status administration, or other coordination already included in the product.

## Outside Expenses

Property-specific third-party costs are additional only when required and approved, including professional media, staging, repairs, printing/postage, advertising spend, and specialty services.

## Accepted-Offer Rule

When the listing enters contract, Transaction Management begins separately under OBJ-00000024. Do not charge a duplicate Koinonia intake/setup fee for information already captured during the listing engagement.

## Scope Change

Materially expanded seller-concierge/remodel work, unusually extensive custom marketing, multiple-property engagements, or major relaunches caused by changed direction may require a revised scope confirmed before the expanded work proceeds.

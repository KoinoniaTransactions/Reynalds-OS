# OBJ-00000015 Professional Open House Pricing Rule

Class: Pricing Rule
Type: Service Pricing Rule
Module: Koinonia
Status: Active
Version: 1.0
Owner: Koinonia
Effective Date: 2026-08-15

Applies to:
- OBJ-00000014 Professional Open House Coverage Service
- OBJ-00000009 Pay-at-Closing Coordination when the engagement involves an applicable listing transaction

Pricing:
- Standalone Professional Open House Coverage: $200 for up to 3 hours.
- One Professional Open House Coverage session is included with an applicable $599 pay-at-successful-close Transaction Support engagement.
- Additional Professional Open House Coverage sessions under the same qualifying $599 engagement: $100 each for up to 3 hours.
- The $389 prepaid Transaction Support option does not include an open house.

Billing Rules:
- Standalone sessions are billed according to the agreed service request/payment terms before or at booking as established by Koinonia.
- The included session under the $599 pay-at-successful-close package does not create a separate open-house charge.
- Additional $100 qualifying sessions are separate service charges and must be documented on the client engagement/invoice.

Scope Rule:
Pricing above assumes a standard session of up to 3 hours with the approved service scope. Materially expanded scope, unusual travel, special access requirements, or other nonstandard work requires documented custom pricing before acceptance.

Governance:
No unpublished discount or alternate Open House price may be quoted without an approved pricing-rule change or documented custom support variation.

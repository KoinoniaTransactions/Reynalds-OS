# OBJ-00000027 — Marketing Management Pricing Rule

Applies to: OBJ-00000022 — Marketing Management  
Status: Pre-Launch Working Pricing Rule  
Effective Date: 2026-09-03

## Standard Price

**$750 per month**

## Billing

Recurring monthly billing under the approved client agreement/payment workflow.

## Standard Economic Scope

The fee covers the agreed recurring managed-marketing workload defined in OBJ-00000022, including planning, normal content production, publishing/coordination, recurring email/database marketing support, listing/event integration, routine online-presence support, and monthly reporting within the agreed capacity.

The product is not marketed as an hourly bank and does not imply unlimited custom production.

## Outside Expenses

Additional only when used and approved:

- advertising/media spend;
- printing/postage;
- professional photography/video/drone;
- premium specialty production;
- client-specific specialty software/services;
- other approved third-party marketing costs.

## Scope Change

Large custom campaigns, substantial websites/software projects, extensive on-location production, repeated strategic redesign after approval, or materially larger paid-media scope may be quoted separately before expanded work proceeds.

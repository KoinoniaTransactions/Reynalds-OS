## 2. Maintenance plans, responsibilities and safeguards

**All resident maintenance requests first enter Koinonia's managed request/triage process** for tracking, urgency classification, communication and documented follow-through.

### 10% fully managed
Koinonia coordinates routine contractor selection, scheduling, resident communications, follow-up and completion documentation within agreed authority. The 10% contractor markup still applies when Koinonia coordinates qualifying vendor work; it is **not included inside** the 10% management fee.

### 8% owner-involved
The owner meaningfully participates in eligible nonemergency maintenance, including owner-selected qualified contractors, approved minor self-performed work where permissible, or agreed coordination responsibilities. Mere invoice approval alone should not be portrayed as equivalent owner participation that reduces Koinonia's workload. Koinonia retains request intake, triage, documentation, resident communication and oversight duties as allocated in the signed agreement.

### Agreed operational defaults
- Routine nonemergency repair spending threshold: **$250** per relevant authorized item, subject to precise contractual definition.
- Owner response expectation for eligible nonurgent repair decisions: **24 hours**. This is a coordination expectation, not a substitute for statutory response or repair deadlines.
- Owner may choose vetted/qualified vendors or perform **approved minor nonurgent work**, where lawful, appropriately insured/qualified and compatible with tenant access and safety.
- If owner fails to respond or follow through, Koinonia may take over as contractually authorized and as necessary to meet legal obligations; qualifying Koinonia-coordinated contractor work carries the **10% markup** under either plan.
- **Do not automatically change** an 8% owner to 10% after one lapse. Repeated failures trigger a review and separately agreed plan adjustment or other contractual remedy.
- No owner preference, approval threshold, or waiting period may delay emergency, safety or statutory habitability responses. Brokerage and property-owner responsibility, response authority, required tenant notice and vendor dispatch must be verified against Colorado law.

### Markup calculation and disclosure
For an illustrative **$400** qualifying contractor invoice, the separately itemized Koinonia 10% markup is **$40**, giving a **$440** owner charge before other disclosed charges. Both management plans pay this markup when Koinonia coordinates and invoices the qualifying vendor. No such markup applies to qualifying owner-directed vendor work **that the owner arranges and pays independently without Koinonia contractor coordination**. Define whether a vendor is considered coordinated, treatment of subcontractor/material charges, credits, taxes, and any affiliate/referral compensation in the management agreement. Do not double-charge a separately quoted project-management fee for the same scope without explicit agreement.

## 3. Leasing and early departure

Half-month tenant placement fee should include the agreed scope of listing/advertising, showing coordination, application/screening administration, lease documentation, initial collection through authorized accounts, and move-in coordination. Services remain conditional on brokerage authority, fair-housing compliance, and approved screening/privacy workflows.

**Success-fee rule approved:** No additional placement fee is due because an applicant withdraws, a first marketing round fails, or additional showings are needed **before a successful placement**. The placement fee is earned upon an approved tenant signing the lease and satisfying the contractually defined move-in conditions. Document exact timing, cancellations and deposits in the agreement.

**No complimentary tenant replacement or 90-day guarantee.** If a tenant leaves early and Koinonia subsequently performs a new successful placement, a new **50% of one month's rent** placement fee applies. The lease should define only lawful, enforceable early-termination obligations, which when owed and actually collected are accounted to the owner as required. Do not promise every early departure will incur a collectible penalty; account for legal termination rights, mitigation requirements, security-deposit law, and rules against double recovery.

**Lease renewal:** $199 only for a completed renewal. Define included rent review, communications, lease preparation, signatures and records; no fee for an unsuccessful attempted renewal unless separately agreed.

## 4. Inspection standard

- Initial property assessment included as part of approved onboarding.
- Move-in and move-out visual condition inspections included as above.
- **Mid-lease visual inspection at lease midpoint: $99**; adjust timing to actual lease length rather than presuming every lease is twelve months.
- Additional owner-requested visual inspections / visits: **$99 per visit**.
- Provide photos, condition observations and an owner report through an approved secure process when implemented. Visual management inspections are not certified building or home inspections. State entry-notice, access, privacy and tenant rights must be observed.
- Billing triggers, included travel radius and what qualifies as a separate visit require final agreement language.

## 5. Future digital capabilities — specified but not yet built

### Instant rental analysis
Owners should see a **data-backed rental estimate directly on the website** after entering and confirming a property address/details, rather than waiting for a callback merely to receive an initial number. Consider licensed **RentCast** API as a candidate primary provider, with Rentometer or commercially licensed Zillow rental data as potential alternatives. None is selected or connected yet.

Requirements before implementation: validate actual API licensing, data rights, current pricing/quotas, geographic coverage, comparable quality, address normalization, backend key secrecy, costs/abuse controls, uncertainty ranges, attribution and disclaimer requirements. The model/AI may summarize verified data but must not manufacture rents, comparables or confidence. Do not require contact information before showing a basic estimate unless a later explicit decision changes that.

### Owner Maintenance Center
Provide a future authenticated owner portal experience with:
- preferred/vetted contractor directory, specialties, actual qualifications and service areas;
- **verified** relationship pricing, service calls, negotiated rates and effective dates (no invented rates);
- repair request history, photos, status, vendor quotes, approval workflow, invoices and completion;
- owner-selected maintenance participation plan, spending threshold and qualified vendor preferences;
- practical checklists, seasonal guidance, and maintenance spending history.

Contractor relationships, prepricing and discounts must be actually negotiated and documented before publication. Disclose referral/affiliate compensation and conflicts as applicable. This portal is a **planned feature**, not currently implemented; do not imply a private platform or contractor-price catalog is live.

## Customer Promise

**You win the listing. Koinonia manages the operational work around it through accepted-offer handoff.**

## Primary Service Objects

- OBJ-00000015 — Listing & Seller Support Service
- OBJ-00000017 — Marketing & Growth Service, for the defined base listing-marketing layer
- OBJ-00000016 — Licensed Field Coverage Service, when separate field labor is requested
- OBJ-00000002 — Transaction Management Service, beginning separately at accepted offer unless a future written bundle says otherwise

## Standard Scope

For one standard listing engagement:

- seller/listing intake and launch planning;
- listing-document/disclosure organization;
- media, sign, lockbox, staging, repair, cleaning, landscaping, and other approved vendor coordination;
- MLS/setup and listing-live support within approved authority;
- defined base listing marketing;
- showing-feedback/seller-update support;
- active-listing administrative changes from Realtor direction;
- relaunch/withdrawal/expiration administration within normal scope;
- accepted-offer operational handoff.

## Base Listing Marketing

The listing-management fee includes the defined Koinonia coordination/production layer for standard listing marketing. Do not separately charge Koinonia coordination fees for normal photographer scheduling, sign coordination, basic listing marketing setup, or similar work already included in this product.

## Outside Expenses

Property-specific third-party costs are additional only when needed and approved, including professional media, drone/video, floor plans/3D, staging, cleaning/repairs/landscaping, printing/postage, advertising spend, and specialty services.

## Licensed Field Labor

Open-house hosting and other licensed field labor are separately priced under Licensed Field Coverage unless a future written package explicitly includes an allowance. Normal coordination of the field need is included in Hand Us the Listing.

## Accepted-Offer Rule

At accepted offer:

- listing information carries forward without a duplicate Koinonia intake/setup charge;
- Transaction Management begins under its applicable pricing rule;
- the Realtor experiences a seamless operational handoff even though the lifecycle has moved into a separately priced product.

The Realtor/brokerage retains seller advice, pricing strategy, CMA conclusions, negotiation, legal/compliance decisions, professional judgment, and required approvals.

## Customer Promise

**Your marketing, consistently handled.**

Koinonia manages an agreed recurring real-estate marketing workload so the Realtor does not personally have to plan, create, coordinate, publish, and chase every recurring marketing item.

## Primary Service Object

- OBJ-00000017 — Marketing & Growth Service

## Standard Recurring Scope

Working launch scope includes:

- monthly marketing/content priorities;
- approximately 8–12 core content pieces/month as an internal production target;
- copy/captions and branded graphics/carousels;
- efficient faceless/templated short-form content where appropriate;
- scheduling/publishing to agreed channels;
- recurring email/newsletter rhythm;
- basic database/review/referral marketing support;
- listing/open-house lifecycle integration using approved information;
- routine online-presence support;
- concise monthly performance summary.

The product is sold as managed marketing, not as a public hourly bank. Internal/contractual capacity boundaries still apply.

## Controlled-Launch Standard

`02_Companies/Koinonia/04_Departments/Operations/MARKETING_MANAGEMENT_PUBLIC_CLAIM_AND_FULFILLMENT_READINESS_2026-09-03.md`

Key launch controls:

- social/content and standard publishing are approved with authorized account access and approval workflows;
- recurring email/newsletter marketing requires compliant sender identity, physical address, unsubscribe, and suppression processes;
- paid advertising remains campaign-specific and requires account, budget, claim, property-fact, and platform-policy controls;
- SMS/text marketing is outside the standard launch promise until a separate consent/compliance workflow is approved;
- no guaranteed leads, rankings, reach, appointments, sales, or closings;
- client/brokerage retains responsibility for professional claims, material property facts, required advertising identity, and regulatory approvals.

## Outside Expenses

Advertising spend, printing/postage, professional photography/video/drone, premium specialty production, and client-specific specialty software/services are additional only when used and approved.

## Boundaries

Not included by default:

- guaranteed leads/appointments/closings;
- unlimited custom creative or revisions;
- general CRM pipeline ownership unrelated to marketing;
- daily administrative/VA support;
- unlimited on-location production;
- large custom websites/software builds;
- arbitrary paid-media scope;
- promotional SMS without an approved consent/compliance workflow;
- professional claims or property facts not approved by the Realtor/brokerage.

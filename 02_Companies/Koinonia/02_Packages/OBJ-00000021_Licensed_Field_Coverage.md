## Customer Promise

**When you cannot physically be there, Koinonia helps keep the approved property/client assignment covered.**

## Primary Service Object

- OBJ-00000016 — Licensed Field Coverage Service

## Public Launch Assignment Types

- buyer showing;
- approved multi-property tour;
- professional open-house hosting;
- inspection/appraisal access;
- photographer/stager/vendor/contractor access;
- vacation/overflow field coverage using approved assignment types.

## Conditional Assignment Types

Subject to property, Brokerage Firm, MLS/lockbox, insurer, and access authority:

- property documentation/status visit;
- approved lockbox/key/sign task;
- repair-completion visual documentation;
- vacancy/securement observation;
- unusual access assignments.

## Standard Assignment

A standard field assignment includes normal intake, access/appointment confirmation, Realtor instruction review, assignment coordination, normal local travel within the pricing-rule base scope, performance of the approved task, completion feedback/documentation, and escalation when needed.

Consumer-facing showings/open houses use active Colorado licensed providers under the controlled launch standard. The provider does not become the consumer's independent broker merely by performing the coverage assignment.

## Billing Relationship

Current pricing rule:

- OBJ-00000026 — Licensed Field Coverage Pricing Rule

Launch pricing:

- **from $75 per standard assignment**;
- **$200 per standard professional open-house event**.

Final price is confirmed before booking when distance, timing, duration, number of properties, or complexity materially changes the assignment.

## Professional Open House

Standard working event scope:

- up to approximately three hours;
- basic setup/teardown;
- approved objective property information/materials;
- visitor registration/lead capture;
- event completion summary and lead handoff;
- normal local travel within accepted assignment economics.

## Ministerial Coverage Boundary

Koinonia Field Coverage is sold as an approved administrative/REAP coverage assignment, not as substitute brokerage representation.

Advice, negotiation, offer/contract preparation, representation decisions, legal/title/loan advice, and property-condition conclusions remain outside the standard assignment.

If requested work becomes Real Estate Brokerage Services, the standard Field Coverage assignment stops and the applicable Brokerage Relationship, supervision, compensation, and licensing rules control.

## Not a Standard Public Product

- final-walk-through representation;
- closing representation/document review;
- negotiation;
- inspection-resolution negotiation;
- possession/property-condition disputes;
- repair-quality certification;
- independent Brokerage Relationship creation.

## Consumer-Facing Disclosure

The provider should clearly identify the limited coverage role and direct property/price/strategy/representation questions back to the requesting Realtor.

## Outside Expenses

Special parking, tolls, externally purchased materials, or other unusual direct expenses may be additional when approved. Routine local travel should be built into the standard base economics rather than itemized when possible.

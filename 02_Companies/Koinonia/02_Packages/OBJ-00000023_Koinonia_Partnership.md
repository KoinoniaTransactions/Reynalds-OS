## Customer Promise

**More than marketing. Help running the business behind your clients.**

Koinonia becomes a recurring real-estate operations and marketing support relationship, owning an agreed layer of recurring work so fewer important details depend on the Realtor personally pushing every next step.

## Primary Service Objects

- OBJ-00000017 — Marketing & Growth Service

- OBJ-00000014 — Contract & Document Support Service only where lawfully/operationally approved within the partnership scope

## Standard Foundation

The Partnership includes the core Marketing Management relationship plus an agreed recurring operations layer that may include:

- CRM/pipeline maintenance;
- next-action/follow-up tracking;
- task/calendar administration;
- past-client/review/referral workflows;
- vendor/business coordination;
- checklist/template/SOP upkeep;
- recurring communications/admin support;
- recurring operations brief and review;
- priority relationship/continuity.

## Capacity Principle

This is not unlimited assistant access and is not marketed primarily as hours.

Working internal delivery target is approximately 13–17 efficient equivalent hours/month across the combined marketing and operations workload, with exact capacity controlled contractually and operationally.

If recurring usage materially and consistently exceeds the agreed level, Koinonia should review scope/capacity rather than generate numerous micro-fees.

## Separately Priced by Default

Unless a future written package expressly includes an allowance or discount:

- Transaction Management remains separately priced;
- Hand Us the Listing remains separately priced;
- Licensed Field Coverage remains separately priced;
- Professional Open House Coverage remains separately priced;
- large custom projects remain separately scoped;
- approved outside expenses remain additional.

Separate pricing does not mean a fragmented client experience: Koinonia should preserve context, continuity, data flow, and coordination across these services.

## Boundaries

The Partnership does not include legal/tax/financial advice, brokerage supervision, unapproved licensed activity, personal errands, unlimited emergency/on-call access, unlimited custom projects, or uncapped use of every Koinonia product.

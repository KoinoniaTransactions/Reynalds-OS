## Customer Promise

**Hand off the contract-to-close coordination.**

Koinonia keeps the transaction organized, tracked, coordinated, and moving while the Realtor retains client advice, negotiation, professional judgment, and brokerage responsibility.

## Primary Service Objects

- OBJ-00000002 — Transaction Management Service
- OBJ-00000014 — Contract & Document Support Service, only where the approved compensation/operating model permits inclusion

## Standard Scope

Includes normal coordination for one standard transaction lifecycle:

- transaction intake/file setup;
- critical-date/deadline tracking;
- document/signature follow-through;
- lender/title/inspection/appraisal coordination;
- approved repair/vendor coordination;
- routine milestone communication;
- closing preparation;
- post-close file completion.

Do not micro-charge for normal reminders, participant follow-up, routine emails/calls, or standard file touches.

## Billing Relationship

Current working pricing rule:

- OBJ-00000024 — Transaction Management Pricing Rule

Working launch price: **$450 per successful closing**.

No successful closing means no standard Transaction Management coordination fee under the working launch rule.

## Boundaries

Not included:

- legal advice;
- negotiation strategy/decisions;
- brokerage supervision;
- unapproved professional judgment;
- property field labor;
- extraordinary litigation/dispute work;
- unrelated custom projects;
- outside costs.

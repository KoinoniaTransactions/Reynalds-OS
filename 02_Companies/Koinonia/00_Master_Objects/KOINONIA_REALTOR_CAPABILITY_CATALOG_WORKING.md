# Koinonia Realtor Capability Catalog — Working Master

Status: Working Master / Pre-Certification  
Owner: Koinonia  
Date: 2026-09-02

> This is the comprehensive working inventory of what a Realtor may be able to hand to Koinonia. It is broader than the current production-certified Service Catalog. Inclusion here means **evaluate / build / operationalize**, not automatically "approved for public sale today."

## Strategic Intent

Koinonia is the white-glove, one-stop Realtor support relationship.

The client should experience:

> **Bring Koinonia the need. We help get it handled.**

The public service architecture should stay simple while the underlying capability depth is extensive.

## Fulfillment Tags

- **KD — Koinonia Direct:** Koinonia operations/admin/creative resources can perform the work.
- **LC — Licensed Coverage:** requires or may require an appropriately licensed/authorized real-estate professional.
- **MS — Managed Specialist:** Koinonia coordinates an outside specialist/vendor while owning the client experience.
- **AG — Agent/Broker Gate:** professional judgment, negotiation, brokerage authority, consumer representation, legal/compliance decision, or similar responsibility must remain with or be specifically delegated/approved by the responsible Realtor/brokerage.

## Maturity Tags

- **CERTIFIED** — already supported by canonical production service object.
- **PUBLIC** — already being publicly presented or priced somewhere in current production materials.
- **BUILD** — strong next capability to operationalize.
- **LATER** — strategically compatible but lower priority.
- **GATED** — must not be broadly promised until legal, licensing, brokerage, insurance, authority, vendor, or workflow controls are resolved.

# What Koinonia Should Still Not Claim

One-stop support does **not** mean unrestricted authority.

Koinonia should not independently provide or claim:

- legal advice;
- tax advice;
- financial advice;
- property valuation or pricing judgment presented as Koinonia's independent opinion unless the appropriate licensed relationship/authority exists;
- negotiation on behalf of a consumer unless specifically lawful and authorized;
- brokerage supervision unless Koinonia is operating through the appropriate licensed brokerage structure;
- inspection, appraisal, title, lending, legal, tax, photography, staging, construction, or other specialist credentials that Koinonia does not itself hold;
- guaranteed lead, sales, SEO, ad, or closing results;
- a false implication that third-party providers are Koinonia employees or licensees.

The white-glove promise is **coordination, ownership of the support experience, and getting the need handled appropriately** — not pretending every specialty is performed in-house.

The website and sales material should not dump this entire catalog onto a Realtor as a wall of 150 checkboxes.

Instead, use simple umbrella messages such as:

## Transactions & Contracts
From contract prep through closing, Koinonia keeps the details organized and moving.

From getting the home ready to launch through active-listing follow-through, Koinonia handles the operational work behind the listing.

## Licensed Field Coverage
When you cannot physically be there, Koinonia helps arrange licensed coverage for showings, open houses, inspections, access needs, and other approved field work.

## Marketing & Growth
Koinonia helps build and execute the marketing that keeps your listings, brand, database, and business visible.

## CRM, Client & Business Operations
Koinonia helps keep the business behind the business organized — CRM, follow-up, workflows, tasks, client touches, vendors, reporting, and recurring operations.

Then use a prominent discovery mechanism:

> **Need something else? Ask Koinonia.**

or

> **Not sure where it fits? Send us the need.**

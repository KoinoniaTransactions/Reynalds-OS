## Mission

Provide listing Realtors with a professionally prepared, promoted, and hosted open house when they want the event handled without personally giving up the full preparation and hosting block.

## Service Position

**Your listing. Professionally prepared, promoted, and hosted.**

This is a Koinonia Transactions operations-support service. It supports the listing Realtor's client relationship; it does not replace the Realtor's representation, strategy, pricing decisions, negotiation role, or brokerage responsibilities.

## Included Scope

A standard Professional Open House Coverage session may include:

- confirmation of listing details, scheduling, property information, and Realtor instructions
- custom property flyer using listing information supplied or approved by the Realtor
- sign setup and takedown using available approved signage
- approved digital promotion through agreed channels
- up to 3 hours of professional licensed hosting
- visitor notes and a post-event summary

## Pre-Event Confirmation Rule

Before promotion begins, Koinonia and the listing Realtor confirm:

- property and listing information
- open-house date and time
- access instructions
- available signage
- marketing content and approved channels
- any property-specific instructions or limitations

## Professional Boundaries

The listing Realtor retains responsibility for:

- client relationship and representation
- listing strategy and pricing decisions
- negotiations and offers
- brokerage compliance and supervision
- legal questions and professional judgment
- final approval of listing information and marketing content

Koinonia does not make independent changes to listing terms, property representations, or brokerage strategy.

## Pricing Summary

Pricing is governed by OBJ-00000015.

- Standalone Professional Open House Coverage: $200 for up to 3 hours.
- One session is included with an applicable $599 pay-at-successful-close Transaction Support engagement for a qualifying listing transaction.
- Additional qualifying sessions under that same engagement: $100 each for up to 3 hours.
- The $389 prepaid Transaction Support option does not include an open house.

## Marketing Rule

This service may be marketed as a standalone capacity solution and as a natural low-friction entry point into a broader Koinonia relationship.

Do not present it as generic event staffing. The value proposition is professional preparation, promotion, licensed hosting, and organized follow-through behind the listing Realtor's client relationship.

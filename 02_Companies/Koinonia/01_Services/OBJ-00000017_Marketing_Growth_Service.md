## Mission

Turn the activity already happening in a Realtor's business into consistent, professional marketing execution without requiring the Realtor to personally plan, create, coordinate, publish, and chase every campaign and asset.

## Service Promise

Koinonia provides managed real-estate marketing execution tied to the Realtor's brand, listings, database, events, and business priorities, while keeping approval, claims, budgets, and professional responsibility clear.

## Capability Scope

### Strategy and brand presence

- monthly/engagement marketing priorities;
- content and campaign planning;
- brand messaging support;
- Realtor bio/about/value-proposition support;
- profile and online-presence updates;
- Google Business Profile content/support where eligible and authorized;
- review-generation workflow support.

### Social / content

- recurring social content planning;
- captions and copy;
- branded static/carousel assets;
- efficient/faceless/templated short-form video where appropriate;
- listing/open-house/under-contract/sold lifecycle content;
- scheduling/publishing to agreed channels;
- basic performance reporting.

### Listing / event marketing

- property messaging/copy from approved facts;
- listing announcement assets;
- open-house promotion;
- flyers/e-flyers;
- tracked/QR links;
- property page/landing-page support where included;
- direct-mail campaign coordination;
- paid listing promotion coordination.

### Email / database / referral marketing

- newsletter/market-update campaigns;
- sphere/past-client campaigns;
- buyer/seller nurture marketing;
- listing announcements;
- review/referral campaigns;
- database reactivation marketing;
- segmentation and campaign reporting.

### Paid / specialty marketing coordination

- Meta or other approved campaign coordination;
- landing-page/source/UTM support;
- print/mail coordination;
- photography/video/drone/floor-plan/3D/virtual-staging coordination;
- specialty production/vendor coordination.

## Outside Expenses

Koinonia marketing fees cover the Koinonia management/production scope defined in the applicable product or project. Variable third-party costs remain separate when used, including:

- advertising spend;
- printing and postage;
- professional photography/video/drone;
- specialty production;
- specialty client-specific software or web costs;
- other approved outside services.

Material outside costs must be approved before commitment unless a standing written budget/authorization applies.

## Boundaries

Koinonia does not guarantee leads, appointments, sales, closings, ranking, reach, or advertising results.

The responsible Realtor/brokerage retains approval and responsibility for material property facts, advertising claims, required disclosures, brokerage advertising compliance, and professional representations.

Unlimited custom creative, unlimited revisions, on-location production, large website/software builds, or unusually extensive paid-media management are not implied by the standard recurring service.

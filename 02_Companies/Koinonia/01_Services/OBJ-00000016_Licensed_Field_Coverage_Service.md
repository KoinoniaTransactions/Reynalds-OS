## Mission

Provide trusted licensed/authorized in-person coverage for approved property and client assignments when the requesting Realtor cannot physically be present.

## Service Promise

Koinonia coordinates and performs or assigns approved field work with clear instructions, confirmed access, professional conduct, completion documentation, and escalation when a Realtor decision is required.

## Capability Scope

### Buyer / showing coverage

- single-property showings;
- approved multi-property tours;
- overlapping-client coverage;
- distance-conflict coverage;
- evening/weekend coverage subject to availability;
- vacation/overflow showing coverage.

### Open-house coverage

- licensed hosting where required/appropriate;
- standard setup and teardown;
- approved property information/materials;
- visitor registration/lead-capture process;
- completion summary and lead handoff;
- property securement after the event.

### Property / access coverage

- inspection access;
- appraisal access;
- photography/media access;
- staging/vendor/contractor access;
- repair-completion access;
- measurement/floor-plan access.

### Approved property tasks

- visual property documentation visit;
- vacancy/property check under written instruction;
- lockbox/key pickup or drop-off;
- lockbox install/removal where permitted;
- sign/property task where permitted;
- approved visual securement/status check.

## Standard Assignment Model

One field engagement is a confirmed assignment with a defined property/location, time or window, expected duration, access instructions, and approved task.

Normal assignment coordination includes:

- request intake/review;
- appointment/access confirmation;
- Realtor instruction review;
- assignment/provider coordination;
- performance of the defined task;
- standard completion documentation/feedback;
- issue escalation when needed.

## Professional / Regulatory Boundaries

Koinonia does not use this service as authority to provide unapproved negotiation, legal advice, property-condition warranties, technical inspection opinions, brokerage supervision, or representation decisions outside the established brokerage/licensing relationship.

The following remain gated unless and until the applicable brokerage relationship, authority, licensing, and operating process are specifically approved:

- final-walkthrough representation/coverage beyond scheduling or narrowly approved attendance;
- physical closing attendance as a representation service;
- closing-document review responsibility;
- possession/property-condition disputes;
- any activity outside delegated or licensed authority.

Unsafe or unauthorized access is never included.

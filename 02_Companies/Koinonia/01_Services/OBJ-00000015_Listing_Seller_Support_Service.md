## Mission

Manage the approved operational work around a Realtor's listing from seller/listing intake through launch, active-listing follow-through, and accepted-offer handoff so the Realtor can remain focused on seller strategy, client advice, negotiation, and winning business.

## Service Promise

Koinonia provides one accountable operational relationship for the normal administrative, coordination, vendor, launch, marketing, and active-listing work defined by the applicable product scope.

## Capability Scope

### Seller / listing intake

- seller and property intake organization;
- listing-document and disclosure organization;
- missing-item follow-up;
- launch timeline/checklist;
- approved brokerage requirement tracking;
- Realtor approval/decision points surfaced clearly.

### Property preparation coordination

- photography/media scheduling;
- staging consultation scheduling;
- cleaning, repair, handyman, landscaping, and other approved provider coordination;
- sign coordination;
- lockbox coordination;
- floor-plan, 3D, drone, and video coordination.

### MLS / launch support

Within approved brokerage/MLS authority and Realtor direction:

- listing-data organization;
- MLS draft/input support;
- property-description drafting from approved facts;
- media and document upload coordination;
- virtual-tour/link setup;
- showing-service setup;
- showing instructions;
- syndication/listing-live verification;
- launch QA and confirmation.

### Listing marketing integration

- approved property messaging/copy;
- standard Just Listed content;
- standard email/e-flyer listing announcement;
- standard flyer/open-house asset where applicable;
- tracked/QR property link where useful;
- open-house promotional asset when applicable;
- lifecycle status content according to the applicable product/marketing scope.

### Active listing administration

- showing-feedback collection/organization;
- feedback summary support;
- routine seller-update preparation from approved information;
- price/status/media/showing-instruction changes from Realtor direction;
- listing refresh and back-on-market administration;
- withdrawal/expiration closeout;
- sign/lockbox removal coordination;
- accepted-offer operational handoff.

## Outside Expenses

The Koinonia service fee covers normal Koinonia coordination/execution within the applicable product. Third-party property-specific costs remain separate when required, including photography, drone/video, floor plans/3D, staging, cleaning, repairs, landscaping, printing/postage, paid advertising, and specialty services.

Koinonia coordinates approved outside needs and confirms material outside costs before commitment unless standing written authorization applies.

## Boundaries

Not included as Koinonia-owned professional judgment:

- seller pricing strategy;
- CMA conclusions and list-price decisions;
- negotiation;
- legal advice;
- unapproved material property representations;
- brokerage supervision;
- work outside delegated authority.

Licensed field labor is not automatically included merely because Koinonia coordinates the assignment. Transaction Management begins separately at accepted offer unless a future written package expressly bundles it.

## Mission

Support approved real-estate contract and document workflow from Realtor-provided instructions while preserving the Realtor's professional judgment, brokerage authority, and applicable compensation/compliance requirements.

## Capability Scope

Koinonia may support approved activities such as:

- offer/document preparation from Realtor-supplied terms;
- counterproposal preparation;
- amendments and addenda;
- notices and extensions;
- inspection/repair documentation from Realtor instruction;
- signature-package preparation;
- missing-information review;
- version/document organization;
- executed-document follow-up.

## Professional Boundaries

Koinonia does not independently choose material terms, provide legal advice, negotiate without appropriate authority, invent client facts, or replace Realtor/brokerage approval.

## Compensation Gate

This capability is intentionally **not assigned a standalone public price in the current commercial architecture**.

Before Koinonia publishes or charges a separate fee for legal-document preparation performed in connection with brokerage services, the compensation structure must be reviewed against current Colorado brokerage rules and the applicable brokerage relationship.

Approved commercial paths may include:

- inclusion within an active managed transaction where lawfully structured;
- inclusion within another approved engagement where lawfully structured;
- inclusion within Koinonia Partnership according to approved scope;
- a separately scoped request only after the compensation/compliance gate is satisfied.

## Commercial Relationship

This is a **capability service**, not a required standalone product card.

Current public products should reference this capability only where the approved operating and compensation model permits it.

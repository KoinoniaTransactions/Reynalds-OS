## Mission

Provide structured business operations support for real estate professionals and small teams so routine administrative, organizational, and process work is handled consistently, visibly, and professionally.

The purpose is not to become a vague assistant service. The purpose is to provide defined operational support that improves clarity, organization, follow-through, client experience, and business capacity.

## Service Promise

When a client requests Business Operations Support, they should expect:

- Clear scope definition.
- Organized intake.
- Task tracking.
- Communication support.
- Administrative follow-through.
- Process improvement support.
- Visible status updates.
- Reliable completion or escalation.

## Ideal Client Profile

Best fit:

- Realtors needing recurring operational support.
- Small teams with inconsistent administrative follow-through.
- Agents who need help organizing business processes.
- Clients who value structure, visibility, and accountability.

Not ideal fit:

- Requests without defined outcomes.
- Personal assistant tasks outside business support.
- Legal, financial, or brokerage supervisory duties.
- Open-ended support without agreed boundaries.
- Emergency-only requests with no operational process.

## Service Boundaries

### Included

- Administrative task coordination.
- CRM cleanup and follow-up tracking.
- Basic client communication drafting.
- Checklist and process management.
- Business file organization.
- Template management.
- Calendar and task support.
- Reporting support.
- Workflow documentation.
- Operational improvement recommendations.

### Not Included

- Legal advice.
- Financial advice.
- Tax advice.
- Brokerage supervision.
- Contract negotiation.
- Personal errands.
- Unlicensed activity beyond approved support boundaries.
- Work without client-provided direction when required.

## Deliverables

Every support request should include:

- Business Support Request Object.
- Scope definition.
- Required inputs.
- Assigned owner.
- Due date or cadence.
- Completion criteria.
- Status updates.
- Final deliverable or completed task record.
- Timeline event.
- Archive or follow-up path.

## Success Criteria

A successful Business Operations Support request demonstrates:

- Scope was clear.
- Required inputs were collected.
- Work was completed or escalated.
- Client was updated.
- Related objects were updated.
- Timeline event was created.
- Follow-up was assigned if needed.
- Outcome improved organization, clarity, or execution.

## Operational Workflow

Request Received → Scope Confirmed → Inputs Collected → Task Created → Work Executed → QA / Review → Client Update → Completion → Archive / Follow-Up

## Stage 1 — Request Intake

Trigger:

Client requests business or administrative support.

Required inputs:

- Client.
- Business context.
- Requested outcome.
- Due date.
- Required materials.
- Communication expectations.
- Approval requirements.
- Related transaction, client, campaign, or business object.

Output:

Business Support Request Object created.

## Stage 2 — Scope Confirmation

Confirm:

- What is being requested?
- What is the expected deliverable?
- What is the deadline?
- What information is required?
- Who approves the final output?
- Is the request inside Koinonia service boundaries?

If unclear, pause until clarified.

## Stage 3 — Work Planning

Create:

- Task list.
- Owner assignment.
- Due date.
- Priority.
- Related object links.
- Completion criteria.

## Stage 4 — Execution

Work may include:

- Updating CRM records.
- Drafting communication.
- Organizing documents.
- Creating checklists.
- Updating trackers.
- Preparing operational summaries.
- Following up with clients or vendors as directed.
- Supporting business processes.

## Stage 5 — Review

Before completion, verify:

- Scope completed.
- Required fields updated.
- Communication is professional.
- Client approval obtained if required.
- Related records updated.
- Follow-up tasks created if needed.

## Stage 6 — Completion

Close request when:

- Deliverable completed.
- Client notified.
- Related object updated.
- Timeline event created.
- Archive or follow-up path recorded.

## Decision Playbooks

### Playbook 001 — Scope Unclear

Situation: Client request is vague or lacks a clear deliverable.

Immediate actions:

1. Identify missing scope details.
2. Ask clarifying questions.
3. Do not begin work based on assumptions.
4. Mark request as Waiting on Client.
5. Set follow-up reminder.

### Playbook 002 — Request Outside Service Boundary

Situation: Request includes legal, financial, brokerage, personal, or unlicensed work outside scope.

Immediate actions:

1. Clarify boundary.
2. Offer approved operational alternative if available.
3. Document limitation.
4. Proceed only with allowable work.

### Playbook 003 — Missing Required Materials

Situation: Work cannot be completed because inputs are missing.

Immediate actions:

1. List missing materials.
2. Request missing items.
3. Set request status to Waiting on Client.
4. Monitor due date.

### Playbook 004 — Recurring Support Needed

Situation: Request is repeated or should become a recurring process.

Immediate actions:

1. Identify cadence.
2. Create recurring task or workflow recommendation.
3. Document process.
4. Recommend template or SOP creation.

### Playbook 005 — Operational Bottleneck Identified

Situation: Multiple support requests point to a repeat business bottleneck.

Immediate actions:

1. Identify pattern.
2. Document cause.
3. Recommend process improvement.
4. Create Knowledge or Workflow update if approved.

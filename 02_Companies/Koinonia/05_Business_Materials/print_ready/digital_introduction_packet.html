Koinonia helps Realtors protect client service by organizing the operational work that competes for their time.

The service is the product. Systems, checklists, and the portal are tools used to communicate, provide visibility, and complete the work.

Five Practical Starting Points

1 · Transaction Support

“I can handle the client. I need help carrying the file.”

Contract-to-close operational coordination including timelines, deadlines, outstanding items, party coordination, closing preparation, and file completion.

2 · Contract & Document Support

“I know the decision. I need the document workflow handled.”

Licensed preparation based on Realtor instructions for offers, counters, amendments, addenda, notices, revisions, and approved documents.

3 · Licensed Showing Coverage

“My client needs access, but I cannot be in two places at once.”

Professional licensed backup when a client needs property access and the Realtor cannot personally cover the showing.

4 · Professional Open House Coverage

“My listing deserves a professional open house, but my weekend is already spoken for.”

Your listing. Professionally prepared, promoted, and hosted—with visitor notes and post-event follow-up.

5 · Monthly Operations Partnership

“I can handle my clients. I need the business behind them to stay organized.”

Recurring CRM, pipeline, follow-up, tasks, checklists, calendar, workflow, templates, and business-operations support without hiring a full-time assistant.

Current Pricing Snapshot

Transaction Support

Same operational coordination. Two payment structures.

  • $389 prepaid per transaction.
  • $599 pay at successful close.
  • No coordination fee if the $599 transaction does not successfully close under the approved structure.
  • One Open House session included with an applicable $599 listing engagement.

Contract, Showing & Open House

  • Contract & Document: single-document support starts at $75.
  • Licensed Showing: local coverage starts at $50.
  • Open House: $200 standalone, up to 3 hours.
  • Additional qualifying Open House sessions: $100 each under the applicable $599 engagement.

Monthly Operations Partnership

Starter $299/month · up to 3 hours   |   Growth $599/month · up to 7 hours   |   Partner $999/month · up to 12 hours

Monthly support is capacity- and scope-based, not unlimited assistant access. No automatic service discount is included unless specifically documented.

Keep the Client

We'll help you carry the operation.

Koinonia's five core services are designed for Realtors who want to preserve the client relationship while receiving dependable operational support.

Separate Brokerage Referral Path

Can't take the client? Keep the opportunity.

40% Referral Partner Option — a qualifying successfully closed referred opportunity may earn a 40% referral fee.

This is not a sixth Koinonia Transactions service. Complete brokerage, compensation, transaction, and referral terms are provided before client handoff. A Koinonia support relationship never becomes a brokerage referral unless the Realtor explicitly chooses that path.

Professional Boundary

Koinonia provides operational support. Realtors remain responsible for client advice, negotiation decisions, brokerage compliance, legal questions, professional judgment, representation strategy, and final approval.

Koinonia is not unlimited assistant access, brokerage supervision, legal/tax/financial advice, or a replacement for the Realtor-client relationship.

The Operational Pressure

Serving clients requires more than client-facing work.

Realtors balance clients, contracts, deadlines, showings, listings, open houses, communication, follow-up, and business development. Koinonia provides dependable support behind the client relationship so important work remains visible, organized, and moving forward.

How Koinonia Supports Realtors

01

Transaction Support

Contract-to-close coordination, deadline visibility, document checklists, communication coordination, closing preparation, and file completion.

02

Contract & Document Support

Licensed document preparation based on the Realtor's instructions, including offers, amendments, addenda, notices, revisions, and review-ready organization.

03

Licensed Showing Coverage

Licensed property access and showing support when schedules, distance, timing, or conflicts make personal coverage difficult.

04

Professional Open House Coverage

Professional preparation, approved digital promotion, available signage, custom property flyer, up to three hours of licensed hosting, and a post-event summary.

05

Monthly Operations Partnership

Recurring CRM, pipeline, task, follow-up, workflow, checklist, calendar, and business operations support without hiring a full-time assistant.

A Separate Option When You Don't Want to Take the Client

40% Referral Partner Option

Want to keep the client? Koinonia can help you carry the operation.

Don't want or can't take the client? You may be able to refer the opportunity instead and receive a 40% referral fee on qualifying successfully closed referred business.

Complete brokerage, compensation, transaction, and referral terms are provided in the formal referral documents before client handoff. A Koinonia Transactions support relationship never becomes a brokerage referral unless the Realtor explicitly chooses to refer the client.

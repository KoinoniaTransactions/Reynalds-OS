1 · Transaction Support

Contract-to-close coordination without carrying every next step yourself.

Once a transaction is under contract, Koinonia organizes the operational side of the file.

  • File intake, timeline setup, deadlines, and milestones.
  • Document and outstanding-item tracking.
  • Lender, title, client, and party coordination.
  • Inspection, appraisal, closing preparation, and file completion.
$389 prepaid · $599 pay at successful close

The $599 option reflects Koinonia assuming closing/payment risk. Under the approved structure, no coordination fee is due if the transaction does not successfully close. Client relationship, advice, negotiation, brokerage compliance, professional judgment, and final decisions remain with the Realtor.

2 · Contract & Document Support

You make the decision. We organize the document workflow.

Licensed preparation support based on the Realtor's instructions.

  • Offers, counters, amendments, addenda, notices, and approved documents.
  • Missing-information follow-up and revisions.
  • Organized, review-ready draft delivery.
Single-document support starts at $75

The Realtor provides the terms, business decisions, instructions, and final approval.

3 · Licensed Showing Coverage

Professional licensed backup when you cannot be in two places at once.

Koinonia helps protect the client experience when schedule conflicts make personal coverage impossible.

  • Property and appointment confirmation.
  • Licensed property access and agreed showing coverage.
  • Access coordination, completion confirmation, and feedback.
Local coverage starts at $50

Distance, urgency, extended coverage, or access complexity may affect pricing. Representation, advice, negotiation, strategy, and the ongoing client relationship remain with the Realtor.

Koinonia provides operational support. Realtors remain responsible for client advice, negotiation decisions, brokerage compliance, legal questions, professional judgment, and final approval.

4 · Professional Open House Coverage

Your listing. Professionally prepared, promoted, and hosted.

A prepared, promoted, professionally hosted open house without giving up a full block of your own weekend.

  • Custom property flyer using Realtor-supplied listing information.
  • Approved signage setup/takedown and digital promotion.
  • Up to 3 hours of licensed hosting.
  • Visitor notes and post-event summary.
$200 standalone · up to 3 hours

One Professional Open House is included with an applicable $599 pay-at-successful-close Transaction Support engagement. Additional qualifying sessions under that engagement are $100 each, up to 3 hours. The $389 prepaid option does not include an open house.

5 · Monthly Operations Partnership

Recurring structure and follow-through behind the client work.

For Realtors who need consistent operational support without hiring a full-time assistant.

  • CRM organization and pipeline visibility.
  • Follow-up tracking, recurring tasks, and checklists.
  • Client communication, calendar, and task coordination.
  • Templates, workflow documents, and operational cleanup.
$299 · $599 · $999 monthly tiers

Starter includes up to 3 hours, Growth up to 7 hours, and Partner up to 12 hours. Monthly support is capacity-based and scope-based, not unlimited assistant access.

A Separate Path

40% Referral Partner Option

Want to keep the client? We'll help you carry the operation.

Don't want to take the client? You may be able to refer the opportunity instead and receive a 40% referral fee on qualifying successfully closed referred business.

Complete brokerage, compensation, transaction, and referral terms are provided in formal referral documents before client handoff. A Koinonia Transactions support relationship never becomes a brokerage referral unless the Realtor explicitly chooses to refer the client.

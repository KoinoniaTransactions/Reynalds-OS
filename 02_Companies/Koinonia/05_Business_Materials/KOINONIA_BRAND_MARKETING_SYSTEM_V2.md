# Koinonia Brand & Marketing System v2

Status: Canonical Marketing Direction  
Business: Koinonia Transactions  
Primary Audience: Colorado Realtors, brokerage leaders, office managers, and real estate teams

---

## Purpose

Create a cohesive sales and marketing system for introducing Koinonia to brokerages and Realtors.

The system must feel:

- Professional
- Organized
- Calm
- Dependable
- Premium without being flashy
- Service-centered
- Faith-rooted without overpowering the practical business message

Koinonia should be presented as a real estate operations partner, not a generic virtual assistant, discount paperwork service, replacement brokerage, or software company.

---

## Core Positioning

Koinonia helps Realtors protect client service by organizing the operational work that competes for their time.

Primary promise:

**You focus on your clients. We'll keep the business running behind you.**

Primary descriptor:

**Real Estate Operations Support for Colorado Realtors**

Primary tagline:

**Real Estate Operations. Elevated.**

Canonical product principle:

**Koinonia sells dependable real estate operations support. The portal is a delivery, communication, and completion tool—not the product.**

Marketing hierarchy:

1. Outcome first.
2. Mechanism second.
3. Boundaries third.

The public story should lead with the work Koinonia takes responsibility for and the operational relief the Realtor receives. Technology should support that story rather than become the story.

---

## Current Service Architecture

Koinonia Transactions has five core service lanes:

1. Transaction Support / Contract-to-Close Coordination
2. Contract & Document Support
3. Licensed Showing Coverage
4. Professional Open House Coverage
5. Monthly Operations Partnership

The 40% Referral Partner Option is a separate secondary brokerage referral path, not a sixth Koinonia Transactions service.

Support ladder:

- Keep the client and need file support -> Transaction Support.
- Keep the client and need documents prepared -> Contract & Document Support.
- Keep the client and cannot make a showing -> Licensed Showing Coverage.
- Keep the listing and cannot prepare/promote/host the open house -> Professional Open House Coverage.
- Keep the clients but need recurring business organization -> Monthly Operations Partnership.
- Do not want or cannot take the client -> separate 40% Referral Partner Option.

Canonical sales principle:

**The first engagement should earn the second engagement.**

---

## Referral Partner Public-Detail Boundary

The public website may clearly communicate that a qualifying successful referred closing can earn a 40% referral fee.

Do not publicly expose detailed referral settlement mechanics, internal Koinonia coordination deductions, or brokerage-specific compensation mechanics before the Realtor agrees to proceed with a referral.

Public-facing language should direct the Realtor to the formal referral documents for complete brokerage, compensation, transaction, and referral terms before client handoff.

Trust invariant:

**A Koinonia Transactions support relationship never becomes a brokerage referral unless the Realtor explicitly chooses to refer the client.**

## Brand Mark

The canonical print mark is the gold K inside a thin gold circle.

Rules:

- Use the circled K as a deliberate brand mark, not a background watermark.
- Do not place text over the K.
- Maintain generous clear space around it.
- Use the same mark direction across business cards, service sheets, brokerage materials, folders, and digital PDFs.

## Primary Visual System

Colors:

- Near-black / charcoal
- Warm white / paper
- Koinonia gold
- Soft neutral beige

Visual characteristics:

- Clean typography
- Strong hierarchy
- Generous white space
- Thin gold rules used sparingly
- Minimal decorative elements
- Clear service structure
- No unnecessary stock photography
- No crowded flyer-style layouts

Website hero direction:

- Bright, light, airy professional workspace imagery.
- Cream or warm-white environment.
- Soft natural light.
- Warm wood.
- Restrained black/gold branded details.
- Organized physical work cues such as documents, folders, checklists, property materials, or professional workspace objects.
- Marketing copy remains live HTML/CSS; do not bake page headlines or body copy into hero imagery.
- Leave purposeful negative space when the layout needs live copy beside the image.

### Hero Screen-Visibility Rule

Brand consistency comes from the shared visual system, not identical computer orientation.

Most Koinonia heroes should emphasize the physical professional workspace and may show the back or side of a laptop or other technology.

A front-facing computer or device screen is appropriate when seeing the workflow materially strengthens the service story, such as referral handoff, operational visibility, or another process where the organized workflow itself is part of what reassures the Realtor.

Front-facing screens must remain occasional rather than becoming the default. Koinonia should never begin to look primarily like a software company.

The approved Referral Partner hero is the canonical precedent for this controlled exception. Its visible workflow screen supports the story of an opportunity entering an organized handoff process; it does not redefine the broader hero system.

Do not create additional front-facing-screen heroes merely to make the Referral Partner hero look consistent. Use them only when the service story calls for them.

## Canonical Marketing Pieces

### 1. Business Card

Status: Approved

Purpose:

- Personal introduction
- Direct contact
- Brand reinforcement

### 2. Brokerage Introduction Sheet

Status: Current priority

Purpose:

- Primary brokerage leave-behind
- Explain the problem Koinonia solves
- Introduce the five service categories
- Establish trust, positioning, and next step
- Avoid detailed pricing so the piece remains evergreen

### 3. Service Guide

Purpose:

- Explain service scope in greater detail
- Include starting prices
- Include professional boundaries
- Support follow-up conversations

### 4. Pricing Insert

Purpose:

- Keep pricing separate and easy to update
- Prevent reprinting the primary introduction sheet when prices change

### 5. Tri-Fold Brochure

Status: Later phase

Purpose:

- Condensed networking and event piece
- Derived from approved Introduction Sheet and Service Guide content

### 6. Digital Introduction Packet

Purpose:

- Email follow-up
- Brokerage presentation
- Digital sharing and consultation preparation

### 7. Presentation Folder

Status: Later phase

Purpose:

- Hold introduction sheet, service guide, pricing insert, and business card
- Used for intentional brokerage meetings

## Sales Flow

The printed materials should support this conversation:

1. Introduce Koinonia and Jeremiah.
2. Identify the operational pressure Realtors experience.
3. Explain how Koinonia supports the work behind the client relationship.
4. Introduce the five core service lanes.
5. Clarify professional boundaries.
6. Make the separate Referral Partner Option visible without presenting it as a competing service lane.
7. Offer a consultation or follow-up conversation.

---

### First

What the Realtor gains:

- More time for clients

- Stronger follow-through

### Second

How Koinonia helps:

- Transaction coordination
- Contract and document support

## Print Direction

Recommended paper:

- 16pt or 18pt premium matte for business cards
- Heavy premium matte cover stock for one-page materials when appropriate
- High-quality uncoated or silk/matte text stock for packet pages
- Soft-touch only when the price remains reasonable

Avoid:

- High-gloss finish
- Excessive foil
- Raised print used only for decoration
- Overly ornate luxury treatments
- Thin disposable flyer paper for primary brokerage meetings

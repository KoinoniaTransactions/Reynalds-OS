# Koinonia Invoice and Estimate Visual Standard

Status: Working Visual Standard v1  
Owner: Koinonia Transactions  
Purpose: Keep future Koinonia invoices, estimates, and billing documents consistent with the approved service and pricing structure.

## Goal

Invoices and estimates should look professional, clear, and organized while preserving approved service boundaries and billing rules.

## Document Types

This standard applies to:

- Estimates.
- Proposals.
- Invoices.
- Scope confirmations.
- Monthly support billing summaries.

## Visual Direction

- Clean white or warm-light document.
- Koinonia Transactions clearly at top.
- Simple black/charcoal text.
- Light gold accent line or header detail.
- No unnecessary imagery.
- Clear line items.
- Clear due date and payment status.
- Clear scope and boundary notes.

## Header Fields

Recommended fields:

- Koinonia Transactions.
- Website.
- Phone/Text.
- Email.
- Invoice or estimate number.
- Date.
- Due date.
- Client/Realtor/team.
- Related file/property if applicable.
- Package/service.
- Status.

## Line Item Rules

Each line item should identify:

- Service name.
- Scope summary.
- Billing model.
- Amount.
- Notes if needed.

Example:

Transaction Coordination Plus  
Prepaid contract-to-close coordination  
Amount: $389.00

## Approved Billing Models

Use the approved billing models from `invoice_estimate_template.md`:

- Prepaid Transaction Coordination.
- Pay-at-Closing Coordination.
- Contract & Document Support.
- Licensed Showing Coverage.
- Monthly Operations Partnership.
- Custom Operations Support.

Current approved pricing anchors:

- Contract & Document Support: starting at $75.
- Licensed Showing Coverage: starting at $50.

- Growth Operations Support: $599/month.
- Partner Operations Support: $999/month.

## Legal / Entity Caution

Before using invoices with real clients, confirm:

- Legal business name.
- Entity status.
- Tax handling.
- Payment account.
- Invoice numbering.
- Terms.
- Late-payment policy.
- Any required disclosures.

Until legal structure is confirmed, do not add LLC or parent/sub-company claims.

Koinonia provides real estate operations support. Koinonia does not provide legal advice, tax advice, financial advice, brokerage supervision, or client decision-making replacement. Contract and document support is prepared from Realtor instructions, and Realtors remain responsible for final review, brokerage compliance, client advice, negotiation decisions, and approval.

## Approved Billing Models

### Prepaid Transaction Coordination

Applies to: Transaction Coordination Plus  
Price: $389  
Billing: Due before transaction work begins.  
Rule: Lower price because payment is collected upfront. If the transaction cancels after work begins, the fee remains earned unless otherwise decided by Koinonia.

Invoice line example:

Transaction Coordination Plus — Prepaid contract-to-close coordination  
Amount: $389.00

### Pay-at-Closing Coordination

Applies to: Pay-at-Closing Coordination  
Price: $599  
Billing: Due only after successful closing.  
Rule: No coordination fee if the transaction does not close. Higher price reflects Koinonia assuming payment risk.

Invoice line example:

Pay-at-Closing Coordination — Contract-to-close coordination due at successful closing  
Amount: $599.00

### Contract & Document Support

Applies to: Contract and document support based on Realtor instructions.  
Starting point: $75+ depending on document type and scope.

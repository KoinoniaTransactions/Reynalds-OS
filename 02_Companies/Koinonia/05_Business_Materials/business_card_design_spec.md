# Koinonia Business Card Design Spec

Status: Working Design Spec v1  
Owner: Koinonia Transactions  
Purpose: Define business card direction before producing final print-ready artwork.

## Goal

Create a professional business card that supports real Koinonia use and can also be visible in the Google Business verification video.

The card should communicate:

- Koinonia Transactions.
- Real estate operations support.
- Colorado Realtor support.
- Direct contact options.
- Calm, premium, organized brand feel.

## Recommended Format

Standard business card size:

- 3.5 in x 2 in.
- Horizontal layout preferred.
- Two-sided card preferred.

## Visual Layout Direction

Front:

- Clean white or warm-light background.
- Koinonia name as the main anchor.
- Small gold accent line or mark.
- Contact details grouped cleanly.
- No address unless a public business address exists later.

Back:

- Short brand promise centered or strongly placed.
- Four service lines grouped cleanly.
- Gold accent used lightly.
- Plenty of white space.

## Design Notes

- Do not use stock people.
- Do not use a fake office image.
- Do not overload the card with pricing.
- Do not claim LLC status until legal formation is completed.
- Keep it readable from a quick glance.
- Make it suitable for print and for showing on camera.

## Print Preparation Checklist

Before ordering cards:

- [ ] Confirm final legal/business name.
- [ ] Confirm email spelling.
- [ ] Confirm phone number.
- [ ] Confirm website domain.
- [ ] Confirm whether a logo/mark is finalized.
- [ ] Export print-ready PDF with bleed if required.
- [ ] Keep editable source file in the business materials folder or linked design tool.

## Google Video Use

For verification, a printed card or high-quality mockup may be shown as a real working business material.

Do not present a mockup as already printed cards unless cards have actually been printed.

# Koinonia Google Video Materials Setup

Status: Working Setup Plan v1  
Owner: Koinonia Transactions  
Purpose: Define exactly what should be visible during Google Business Profile video verification after branded materials are prepared.

## Principle

The video should show real Koinonia business readiness without wasting effort on props that will not be used again.

Materials shown in the video should also support:

- Future Realtor consultations.
- Email follow-up.
- Print collateral.
- Billing setup.
- Brand consistency.
- Internal operating clarity.

## Office Setup

Known office setup:

- L-shaped desk.
- Laptop.
- 30-inch screen.
- 50-inch screen used with the computer.

Use this setup intentionally.

## Recommended Screen Layout

### Laptop

Open one of:

- Google Business Profile dashboard for Koinonia Transactions.
- Koinonia email/admin access.
- Koinonia working business materials folder.

### 30-Inch Screen

Open:

- https://www.koinoniatransactions.com

Recommended page:

- Home page first.
- Contact/scheduler if needed.

### 50-Inch Screen

Open one of:

- Service sheet PDF.
- Brochure/flyer PDF.
- Invoice/estimate template.
- Branded materials folder.

## Desk Materials

Place these neatly on the desk:

- Printed service sheet, if available.
- Printed invoice/estimate template, if available.
- Printed business card mockup or real cards, if available.
- Printed brochure/flyer, if available.

If not printed yet, use digital versions and do not claim they are printed.

## What To Say

Suggested natural wording:

“Hi, this is Jeremiah with Koinonia Transactions. Koinonia provides real estate operations support for Colorado Realtors, including transaction coordination, contract and document support, licensed showing coverage, and monthly operations support. This is my working office setup where I manage the business, communication, scheduling, service materials, billing templates, and client support systems.”

Then show:

1. Office setup.
2. Live website.
3. Business Profile or email/admin access.
4. Service sheet.
5. Invoice/estimate template.
6. Business card or brochure draft.
7. No sensitive information.

## What Not To Say

Do not say:

- This is a public storefront.
- Customers visit this address during business hours.
- Koinonia is an LLC unless filed.
- Reynalds parent company owns this unless structure is filed.
- Any fake or premature legal-entity statement.

## Privacy Sweep

Before recording:

- Close all client files.
- Hide property addresses.
- Hide contracts.
- Hide financial/banking details.
- Hide tax documents.
- Hide passwords.
- Remove personal/family items from frame.
- Turn off notifications.
- Use a clean desktop or browser window.

- [ ] Google Business Profile details reviewed.

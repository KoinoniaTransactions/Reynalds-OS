# Koinonia Tri-Fold Brochure Design Spec

Status: Active Design Spec  
Owner: Koinonia Transactions  
Purpose: Define the six-panel tri-fold brochure used for networking, brokerage conversations, events, and compact digital sharing.

## Goal

Create a concise brochure that gives a Realtor enough information to understand where Koinonia fits without turning the piece into a compressed Service Guide or pricing sheet.

The brochure should feel:

- professional
- calm
- organized
- premium without being flashy
- service-centered
- easy to scan

Koinonia must read as a real estate operations partner, not software, a generic virtual assistant, or a replacement brokerage.

## Format

- US Letter: 8.5 in x 11 in
- Landscape orientation
- Two-sided
- Three panels per side
- Standard roll/letter fold unless printer requirements specify otherwise
- Print-ready PDF should be exported from the approved source

Do not assume the physical brochure has been printed until actual print production occurs.

## Content Hierarchy

Outcome first → mechanism second → boundaries third.

The brochure should introduce the problem Koinonia solves before asking the reader to process five service descriptions.

Detailed pricing belongs primarily in the Pricing Insert and Service Guide. The brochure may use service language and referral benefit messaging, but should not become price-driven.

## Six-Panel Structure

### Panel 1 — Front Cover

- Koinonia Transactions
- Real Estate Operations Support for Colorado Realtors
- Primary promise: **You focus on your clients. We'll keep the business running behind you.**
- Tagline: **Real Estate Operations. Elevated.**
- Circled K brand mark

Keep the cover clean. Do not attempt to list every service on the front.

### Panel 2 — Operational Pressure

Primary idea:

**More transactions should create more opportunity, not more chaos.**

Explain the operational work competing for Realtor attention and position Koinonia as dependable support behind the client relationship.

Technology should remain secondary. The brochure may mention systems or portal visibility only as tools supporting service delivery.

### Panel 3 — Five Core Services

Present all five current Koinonia Transactions lanes:

1. Transaction Support / Contract-to-Close Coordination
2. Contract & Document Support
3. Licensed Showing Coverage
4. Professional Open House Coverage
5. Monthly Operations Partnership

Use short definitions only. The Service Guide owns deeper scope.

Open House positioning:

**Your listing. Professionally prepared, promoted, and hosted.**

### Panel 4 — Keep or Refer

Use the approved decision framework:

**Want to keep the client? We'll help you carry the operation.**  
**Don't want to take the client? You may be able to refer the opportunity instead.**

The five service lanes belong under keep-the-client support.

The **40% Referral Partner Option** must appear in a visually separate subsection and must not be styled as a sixth equal service.

Public referral language may state a 40% referral fee on qualifying successfully closed referred business. Do not expose private settlement mechanics or brokerage-specific compensation details.

Include the trust rule in concise form:

A Koinonia Transactions support relationship never becomes a brokerage referral unless the Realtor explicitly chooses to refer the client.

### Panel 5 — Why Koinonia / Boundaries

Use trust-building points such as:

- licensed real estate perspective
- organized operational execution
- clear communication
- defined professional boundaries
- dependable follow-through
- support designed around the Realtor-client relationship

Include concise professional boundary language.

## Visual Direction

Use the current Koinonia print system:

- warm white / paper background
- near-black / charcoal typography
- restrained Koinonia gold
- soft neutral beige
- circled gold K mark
- generous negative space
- thin gold rules sparingly
- clear typographic hierarchy

Avoid:

- crowded flyer styling
- large software screenshots
- generic stock people
- fake office scenes
- excessive decorative icons
- making the Referral Partner option look like the primary Koinonia product

## Fold Safety

Keep important text and marks away from panel fold lines.

When preparing final printer files:

- confirm printer bleed requirements
- confirm exact fold type and panel widths
- account for the slightly narrower fold-in panel if required by printer specification
- verify front/back orientation with a physical paper mockup before ordering

# Koinonia Brochure Design Spec

Status: Working Design Spec v1  
Owner: Koinonia Transactions  
Purpose: Define brochure direction before creating a final flyer, trifold, or digital PDF.

## Goal

Create a simple branded brochure that explains Koinonia’s service categories and business positioning in a way that supports real sales conversations and Google verification.

## Recommended First Version

Start with a one-page flyer before creating a trifold.

Reason:

- Faster to produce.
- Easier to print.
- Easier to show in video.
- Easier to email as PDF.
- Less likely to become overcrowded.

A trifold can be created later after final brand and legal structure are confirmed.

## One-Page Flyer Layout

### Top Section

Koinonia Transactions  
Real estate operations support for Colorado Realtors.

You focus on your clients.  
We'll keep the business running.

### Middle Section

Four service blocks:

- Transaction Support
- Contract & Document Support
- Licensed Showing Coverage
- Monthly Operations Partnership

### Support-Fit Section

Use the “Why Realtors Use Koinonia” message:

- Files need structure.
- Deadlines need tracking.
- Paperwork needs to move.
- Clients need updates.
- Showings need coverage.
- Daily operations need follow-through.

### Boundary Section

Koinonia provides operational support and does not replace Realtor judgment, brokerage compliance, legal advice, tax advice, financial advice, or final approval responsibility.

## Visual Direction

- Calm, premium, organized.
- Light background.
- Black/charcoal text.
- Gold accents.
- Simple cards or columns.
- No stock people.
- No fake office imagery.
- Avoid dense paragraphs.

## Future Trifold Panels

If a trifold is created later:

1. Cover.
2. Who Koinonia helps.
3. Services.
4. Why Realtors use Koinonia.
5. Boundaries / how it works.
6. Contact / consultation CTA.

## Google Video Use

For verification, use either:

- A printed one-page flyer.
- A PDF open on a monitor.
- A draft clearly presented as working business material.

Do not imply printed brochures exist unless printed brochures actually exist.

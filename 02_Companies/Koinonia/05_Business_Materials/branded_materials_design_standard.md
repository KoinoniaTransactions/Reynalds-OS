# Koinonia Branded Materials Design Standard

Status: Working Design Standard v1  
Owner: Koinonia Transactions  
Purpose: Keep all Koinonia printed and digital business materials visually consistent, useful for Google Business verification, and reusable for real client-facing operations.

## Core Rule

Do not create throwaway materials only for Google verification.

Every business card, service sheet, brochure, invoice, estimate, or verification asset should also be suitable for real Koinonia business use.

## Brand Position

Koinonia Transactions is a real estate operations partner for Colorado Realtors.

Koinonia should not be presented as:

- A generic virtual assistant company.
- A discount paperwork service.
- A buyer/seller brokerage replacement.
- A legal, tax, financial, or brokerage-supervision provider.
- An unlimited task bucket.
- A fake storefront or office open to the public unless that becomes true later.

## Brand Feel

All materials should feel:

- Professional.
- Organized.
- Calm.
- Confident.
- Responsive.
- Premium.
- Helpful.
- Thoughtful.
- Detail-oriented.

## Visual Direction

Use the approved Koinonia website feel:

- Light and airy.
- Clean white or warm-light background.
- Black / charcoal typography.
- Gold accent used sparingly.
- Simple spacing.
- Premium, not flashy.
- Organized, not busy.
- No stock people.
- No cluttered real estate clichés.
- No fake office/storefront imagery.

## Color Guidance

Use brand-consistent colors:

- Primary dark text: black or near-black.
- Background: white or warm off-white.
- Accent: muted gold.
- Secondary support: soft gray / warm neutral.

Gold should be an accent, not the dominant color.

## Typography Guidance

Use clean, professional typography.

Recommended hierarchy:

- Business name: strongest visual anchor.
- Service category or tagline: secondary anchor.
- Contact details: clear and readable.
- Service list: concise and skimmable.

Avoid tiny text, crowded blocks, or too many font styles.

## Messaging Rules

Preferred language:

- Real estate operations support.
- Colorado Realtors.
- Transaction coordination.
- Contract and document support.
- Licensed showing coverage.
- Monthly operations support.
- Organized follow-through.
- Support behind the client relationship.

Avoid:

- “We do everything.”
- “Unlimited support.”
- “Legal review.”
- “Compliance approval.”
- “Brokerage supervision.”
- “Guaranteed results.”
- “Best in Colorado.”
- Unsupported legal entity names.

## Legal Entity Rule

Until the legal structure is actually filed and confirmed, branded materials should use:

Koinonia Transactions

Do not use:

- Koinonia Transactions LLC
- Reynalds parent company claims
- Subsidiary claims
- Registered office claims

unless those are true and documented.

## Address Rule

Do not print a private home address on public materials unless the business intentionally chooses a public client-facing address later.

For now, use:

- Website.
- Phone/Text.
- Email.
- Service area: Colorado.

## Google Verification Use

For the Google video, materials should help prove:

- Business identity.
- Service offering.
- Management access.
- Operational readiness.
- Consistency with the website and Google Business Profile.

They should not imply a storefront, public office, legal entity, or walk-in availability unless that is true.

## Materials Covered

This standard governs:

- Business cards.
- Service sheets.
- Brochures.
- Invoice and estimate layouts.
- Verification printouts.
- Future PDF exports.
- Future Canva, Figma, Adobe, or print-shop versions.

## Continuity Rule

Future sessions should extend these specs before redesigning from scratch.

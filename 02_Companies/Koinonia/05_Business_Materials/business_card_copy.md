## Design Notes

- Use Koinonia brand style.
- Do not use stock people.
- Keep layout clean, calm, and premium.
- Avoid overloading the card with pricing.
- Do not include legal entity status unless confirmed.
- Do not include a public office address unless the business intentionally uses a public client-facing location.

## Brand Rule

Koinonia should be presented as a real estate operations partner, not a generic virtual assistant, discount paperwork service, buyer/seller brokerage replacement, or software company.

**The service is the product. The portal and systems are delivery, visibility, communication, and work-completion tools.**

## Current Service Architecture

Five core Koinonia Transactions service lanes:

1. Transaction Support / Contract-to-Close Coordination
2. Contract & Document Support
3. Licensed Showing Coverage
4. Professional Open House Coverage
5. Monthly Operations Partnership

The 40% Referral Partner Option is a separate brokerage referral path and is not a sixth Koinonia Transactions service.

## Service Boundary Rule

Materials must preserve the approved boundaries:

- No legal advice.
- No tax advice.
- No financial advice.
- No brokerage supervision.
- No client decision-making replacement.
- No unlimited assistant positioning.
- No unsupported address, hours, reviews, or legal-entity claims.

A Koinonia Transactions support relationship never becomes a brokerage referral unless the Realtor explicitly chooses the referral path.

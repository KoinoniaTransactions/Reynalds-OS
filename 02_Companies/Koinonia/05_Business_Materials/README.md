## Brand Rule

Koinonia should be presented as a real estate operations partner, not a generic virtual assistant, discount paperwork service, or buyer/seller brokerage replacement.

## Service Boundary Rule

Materials must preserve the approved boundaries:

- No legal advice.
- No tax advice.
- No financial advice.
- No brokerage supervision.
- No client decision-making replacement.
- No unlimited assistant positioning.
- No unsupported address, hours, reviews, or legal-entity claims.

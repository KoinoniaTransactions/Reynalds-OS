# Koinonia Paid Social Campaign 01 — Coverage

Status: Production Working Spec  
Owner: Koinonia Transactions  
Campaign family: Coverage / Everything at Once / Bigger Than the Transaction  
Primary audience: Colorado Realtors  
Primary objective: Qualified lead / consultation request  
Primary destination: Koinonia services/coverage landing experience  

## Campaign Position

Koinonia is a real estate operations partner for Colorado Realtors.

Core campaign idea:

> Real estate does not happen one thing at a time.

Supporting positioning:

> Your business is bigger than the transaction. Your support should be too.

Primary response line:

> Keep the client. Get the coverage.

Secondary response line:

> You sell. We've got you covered.

## Claims Discipline

The phrase "Your TC can't do that" may be used only when the visual/problem points to work that is outside ordinary transaction coordination, such as overlapping field coverage or the need for licensed operational support.

Do not use the line to imply that traditional transaction coordinators cannot perform normal transaction-coordination responsibilities such as deadline tracking, file organization, document management, communication coordination, closing coordination, or contract-to-close management.

Production-certified Koinonia service claims currently supported by the service catalog:

- Transaction Management / contract-to-close coordination.
- Contract Preparation & Writing support based on client-provided instructions and subject to client approval.
- Licensed Showing Coverage.
- Business Operations Support.

Do not imply legal advice, negotiation authority, brokerage supervision, compliance approval, guaranteed results, or that Koinonia "does everything."

If future standalone open-house coverage, physical closing attendance, or other licensed field services are promoted, confirm that those services have been operationally approved before the ad is activated.

# AUDIO DIRECTION

Voice:
- Conversational, human, grounded.
- Never use a traditional announcer voice.
- Slightly quick, confident pace for TikTok/Reels.
- Calm professional pace for LinkedIn.
- Avoid exaggerated enthusiasm.

Music:
- Instrumental only.
- Modern understated percussion / light pulse.
- No cinematic corporate crescendo.
- Keep VO dominant.
- Use platform-cleared commercial audio or properly licensed original/royalty-free music.

SFX:
- Notification ping.
- Lockbox click.
- Light keyboard/paper movement.
- Subtle transition whoosh only where useful.

---

# VISUAL DIRECTION

Use Koinonia's premium operational brand, but paid-social creative should feel native rather than like a brochure.

Rules:
- Realtor problem first; Koinonia second.
- No logo in the opening 2–3 seconds unless the test specifically calls for it.
- Use warm off-white / black / charcoal with muted gold accent.
- Gold is accent, not dominant.
- Avoid cluttered service menus in the first half of the ad.
- Favor POV details: phone, keys, lockbox, calendar, contract screen, laptop, checklist.
- People should feel candid and documentary rather than posed stock imagery.
- Avoid fake storefront imagery.
- Keep text inside platform-safe areas.

# Koinonia Presentation Folder Design Spec

Status: Active Vendor-Neutral Design Spec  
Owner: Koinonia Transactions  
Purpose: Define the presentation-folder visual system, panel hierarchy, contents, and production requirements before a printer-specific dieline is selected.

## Goal

Create a premium physical folder that makes the Koinonia collateral system feel organized, established, and intentional without turning the folder itself into another brochure.

The folder should feel:

- light and airy
- professional
- calm
- premium without being flashy
- organized
- durable and evergreen
- clearly part of the same family as the Brokerage Introduction Sheet, Service Guide, Pricing Insert, Tri-Fold Brochure, and Digital Introduction Packet

## Production Status

This specification is vendor-neutral and is **not yet a printer-ready dieline**.

Do not create final production geometry until the selected printer provides its exact folder template, including:

- finished folder dimensions
- spine width if any
- pocket height and shape
- business-card slot placement
- bleed requirements
- safety margins
- score/fold lines
- glue areas
- acceptable color mode and PDF standard

The printer's supplied dieline becomes the production authority for geometry only. Koinonia brand/content rules remain authoritative for the design.

## Strategic Rule

The folder shell must remain evergreen.

Do not permanently print:

- service pricing
- temporary offers
- detailed referral compensation terms
- software screenshots
- rapidly changing package inclusions
- operational process diagrams that may evolve

Those details belong in replaceable inserts.

## Pockets

Pocket artwork should remain minimal so inserted materials stay visually dominant.

Preferred pocket treatment:

- warm white or soft beige pocket
- thin gold rule near top edge if permitted by dieline
- small circled K or Koinonia wordmark optional

Optional restrained meaning treatment:

Koinonia  
/koy-noh-NEE-uh/  
fellowship · joint participation · sharing in common

Do not place long service descriptions on the pockets.

If a business-card slot exists, place it on the pocket that allows the card to remain visible without obscuring the consultation CTA or inserted materials.

## Standard Insert Architecture

The physical folder should function as a modular kit.

Default stack:

1. Brokerage Introduction Sheet
2. Service Guide
3. Pricing Insert
4. Business Card in slot

Optional meeting-specific inserts may be added behind the core set.

Recommended logic:

- Intro Sheet = first impression / recap
- Service Guide = deeper fit and scope
- Pricing Insert = replaceable exact pricing
- Business Card = personal connection
- Proposal / estimate = only when appropriate
- Referral documents = only when the Realtor explicitly chooses that path

Do not automatically print the Digital Introduction Packet for the physical folder; it is designed primarily for digital follow-up.

The Tri-Fold Brochure is generally used separately for networking/events rather than duplicated inside every folder.

## Visual System

Use the approved Koinonia print language:

- near-black / charcoal typography
- warm white or paper background
- Koinonia gold as restrained accent
- soft neutral beige for support areas
- circled gold K mark
- serif-forward headings paired with clean sans-serif supporting text
- generous breathing room
- thin gold rules sparingly

Avoid:

- full-bleed dark luxury styling
- glossy real estate clichés
- stock people
- software screens
- large house silhouettes, keys, roofs, or generic real-estate iconography
- crowded service menus
- oversized QR codes
- pricing on the permanent shell

## Material / Finish Guidance

Final stock and finish should be chosen with the printer, but the preferred direction is:

- substantial professional cover stock
- matte or uncoated/soft-touch feel rather than high-gloss promotional finish
- crisp black typography
- muted metallic-look or printed gold accent

Actual foil stamping, embossing, spot UV, or premium specialty finishes should be treated as optional upgrades only after printer quotes are reviewed. The design must still work well using standard CMYK printing.

## Folder Assembly QA

Before placing a production order:

1. Obtain the exact printer dieline.
2. Apply this design system to the dieline without changing the approved content hierarchy.
3. Print a full-size paper proof or vendor proof.
4. Fold/assemble the proof physically.
5. Insert the actual Brokerage Introduction Sheet, Service Guide, and Pricing Insert.
6. Confirm the business card remains visible and secure.
7. Confirm QR code scans from the physical proof.
8. Check that pocket height does not hide key insert headlines.
9. Check text against fold, glue, slot, and trim safety zones.
10. Verify exterior front/back orientation before approving production.

## Completion Definition

The Presentation Folder concept is considered marketing-design complete when:

- evergreen exterior/interior copy is approved
- insert architecture is approved
- vendor-neutral visual direction is approved
- no pricing/referral mechanics are permanently baked into the folder shell

It becomes **print-production ready only after** a selected printer dieline is applied and physically proofed.

Koinonia Transactions

## Where Koinonia Fits

Realtors do not usually need help being Realtors. They need dependable support carrying the operational work around the client relationship.

Koinonia is designed for moments like:

- the transaction is under contract and someone needs to own the operational follow-through
- documents need to be prepared from the Realtor's instructions
- a client needs access when the Realtor cannot make the showing
- a listing deserves a professional open house when the Realtor cannot give up the full time block
- the business has grown beyond what the Realtor can personally keep organized every week

## Five Core Service Lanes

### Transaction Support / Contract-to-Close Coordination

Once a transaction is under contract, Koinonia organizes the operational side of the file—timeline, deadlines, documents, communication coordination, outstanding items, closing preparation, and file completion.

Pricing options:

- **$389 prepaid**
- **$599 due at successful closing**

The $599 option includes one Professional Open House Coverage session for an applicable listing transaction. No coordination fee is due if the transaction does not successfully close under the approved pay-at-successful-close structure.

### Contract & Document Support

**You make the decision. We turn that decision into an organized, review-ready document workflow.**

Licensed preparation support based on the Realtor's instructions for offers, counters, amendments, addenda, notices, and other approved transaction documents.

Starting at **$75** for single-document support. Larger packages are scope-based.

### Licensed Showing Coverage

Professional licensed backup when a client needs property access and the Realtor cannot be in two places at once.

Starting at **$50** for local showing coverage. Distance, urgent timing, extended coverage, and access complexity may affect pricing.

### Professional Open House Coverage

**Your listing. Professionally prepared, promoted, and hosted.**

A prepared, promoted, professionally hosted open house for listing agents who want the event handled without personally giving up the full preparation and hosting block.

Pricing:

- **$200 standalone, up to 3 hours**
- **one included** with an applicable $599 pay-at-successful-close Transaction Support engagement
- **$100 each** for additional qualifying sessions under that engagement, up to 3 hours

The $389 prepaid Transaction Support option does not include an open house.

### Monthly Operations Partnership

Recurring CRM, pipeline, follow-up, task, checklist, calendar, template, workflow, and business-operations support without hiring a full-time assistant.

Current tiers:

- **Starter — $299/month, up to 3 hours**
- **Growth — $599/month, up to 7 hours**
- **Partner — $999/month, up to 12 hours**

Monthly support is capacity-based and scope-based. It is not unlimited assistant access.

## Keep the Client or Refer the Opportunity

Koinonia's core services are built for Realtors who want to keep the client relationship and need help carrying the operation.

There is also a separate brokerage referral path when the Realtor does not want or cannot take the client.

**Want to keep the client? We'll help you carry the operation.**  
**Don't want to take the client? You may be able to refer the opportunity instead.**

### 40% Referral Partner Option

A qualifying successfully closed referred opportunity may earn a **40% referral fee**.

The referral option is not a sixth Koinonia Transactions service. Complete brokerage, compensation, transaction, and referral terms are provided in the formal referral documents before client handoff.

A Koinonia Transactions support relationship never becomes a brokerage referral unless the Realtor explicitly chooses to refer the client.

## Professional Boundary

Koinonia provides operational support. Realtors remain responsible for client advice, negotiation decisions, brokerage compliance, legal questions, professional judgment, representation strategy, and final approval.

Koinonia is not positioned as unlimited assistant access, brokerage supervision, legal/tax/financial advice, or a replacement for the Realtor-client relationship.

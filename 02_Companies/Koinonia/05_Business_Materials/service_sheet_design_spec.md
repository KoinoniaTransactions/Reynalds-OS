# Koinonia Service Sheet Design Spec

Status: Working Design Spec v1  
Owner: Koinonia Transactions  
Purpose: Define the one-page service sheet layout for client conversations, Google verification, and future PDF production.

## Goal

Create a one-page service sheet that clearly explains what Koinonia does without making the company feel like a generic task service.

The service sheet should be useful for:

- Realtor consultations.
- Referral conversations.
- Google Business verification.
- Email follow-up.
- Printed desk material.
- Future downloadable PDF.

## Recommended Format

- One page.
- Letter size: 8.5 in x 11 in.
- Portrait orientation.
- PDF export.
- Printer-friendly.

## Page Structure

### Header

Koinonia Transactions  
Real estate operations support for Colorado Realtors.

Contact line:

Website: koinoniatransactions.com  
Phone/Text: 719-745-8497  
Email: jeremiah@koinoniaadmin.com

### Opening Statement

Koinonia helps Realtors protect client service by organizing the operational work that competes for their time.

### Four Service Blocks

Use four clear sections:

1. Transaction Support
2. Contract & Document Support
3. Licensed Showing Coverage
4. Monthly Operations Partnership

Each section should include:

- One-line service definition.
- 3 to 5 practical support examples.
- Starting price or “scope-based” note when appropriate.
- Boundary note only where needed.

### Footer

Suggested footer:

Support behind the client relationship.

## Visual Direction

- Clean white or warm-light page.
- Strong title.
- Four-card or four-section layout.
- Gold accent for section dividers or icons.
- Black/charcoal body text.
- Use spacing instead of heavy boxes.
- Keep it professional and calm.

## Pricing Display

Use current approved public starting points:

- Transaction Support — starting at $389.
- Contract & Document Support — starting at $75.
- Licensed Showing Coverage — starting at $50.
- Monthly Operations Partnership — starting at $299/month.

Do not overpublish every exception, rush rule, mileage rule, or scope variation.

## Boundary Language

Include concise boundary language:

Koinonia provides operational support. Realtors remain responsible for client advice, negotiation decisions, brokerage compliance, legal questions, and final approval.

## Google Video Use

For the video, this can be:

- Printed and placed on the desk.
- Open as a PDF on a monitor.
- Used alongside the website and invoice template.

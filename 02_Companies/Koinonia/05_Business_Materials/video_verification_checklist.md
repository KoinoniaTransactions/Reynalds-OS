# Koinonia Google Business Video Verification Checklist

Status: Working Checklist v1  
Purpose: Prepare for Google Business Profile video verification without exposing sensitive information or weakening the listing.

## Do Not Record Yet Unless Ready

Do not start video verification until:

- The office area is cleaned.
- Sensitive information is hidden.
- Google Business Profile details are accurate.
- Koinonia website is live.
- Koinonia email/admin access is ready.
- Business materials are prepared.
- The user is ready to complete a continuous video.

## Physical Setup

Use the actual office setup:

- L-shaped desk.
- Laptop.
- 30-inch monitor.
- 50-inch monitor.
- Organized workspace.
- Koinonia materials visible digitally or printed.

## Screens to Prepare

Open before recording:

1. Koinonia website:
   - https://www.koinoniatransactions.com
2. Google Business Profile dashboard for Koinonia Transactions.
3. Koinonia email/admin account:
   - jeremiah@koinoniaadmin.com
4. Service sheet.
5. Invoice/estimate template.
6. Business card or brochure draft.

## Privacy Check

Before recording, remove or hide:

- Client names.
- Contracts.
- Property addresses.
- Transaction documents.
- Tax documents.
- Bank information.
- Passwords.
- Personal documents.
- Family photos or private household details.
- Other people’s faces.

## Suggested Video Flow

### 1. Start With Business Identity

Suggested wording:

“This is Jeremiah with Koinonia Transactions. Koinonia provides real estate operations support for Colorado Realtors, including transaction coordination, contract and document support, licensed showing coverage, and monthly operations support.”

### 2. Show Workspace

Show the desk, laptop, monitors, and office setup.

Suggested wording:

“This is the workspace where I manage Koinonia business communication, scheduling, service materials, document support, and operations follow-through.”

### 3. Show Website

Show the live Koinonia website.

Suggested wording:

“This is the live Koinonia website at koinoniatransactions.com.”

### 4. Show Admin Access

Show the Google Business Profile dashboard or Koinonia admin/email access.

Suggested wording:

“This shows I manage the business profile and business communication.”

### 5. Show Business Materials

Show the service sheet, invoice/estimate template, business card copy, or brochure draft.

Suggested wording:

“These are Koinonia service and billing materials for transaction coordination, contract and document support, licensed showing coverage, and monthly operations support.”

### 6. Close

Suggested wording:

“This completes the verification video for Koinonia Transactions.”

## Final Checklist

- [ ] Continuous video.
- [ ] No edits or cuts.
- [ ] No private client information.
- [ ] No tax/bank/password information.
- [ ] No misleading address or storefront claim.
- [ ] Business name visible.
- [ ] Website visible.
- [ ] Admin/email access visible.

- [ ] Workspace visible.

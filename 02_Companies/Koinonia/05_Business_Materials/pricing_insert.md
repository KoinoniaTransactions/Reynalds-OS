# Koinonia Transactions — Pricing Insert

Status: Active Client-Facing Pricing Companion  
Purpose: Easy-to-update pricing companion for the Brokerage Introduction Sheet and Service Guide.

## Role of This Piece

Pricing supports the service decision; it is not the Koinonia value proposition.

Use this insert after a Realtor understands which operational support fits the need. Keep the pricing clear, concise, and easy to update without redesigning the primary marketing pieces.

## Transaction Support / Contract-to-Close Coordination

**Same operational coordination. Two payment structures.**

### Prepaid — $389 per transaction

Best fit when the Realtor prefers the lower upfront rate.

- Due before transaction work begins.
- Lower price reflects upfront payment.
- If the transaction cancels after work begins, the fee remains earned unless Koinonia decides otherwise.
- Professional Open House Coverage is not included.

### Pay at Successful Close — $599 per transaction

Best fit when the Realtor prefers Koinonia to assume the closing/payment risk.

- Due only after a successful closing.
- No coordination fee if the transaction does not close.
- Higher price reflects Koinonia assuming closing/payment risk.
- For an applicable listing transaction, includes **one Professional Open House Coverage session**.

## Contract & Document Support

**Starting at $75 for single-document support.**

Multi-document, offer-package, urgent, or materially expanded requests are scope-based. Scope and price are confirmed before work begins.

## Licensed Showing Coverage

**Local coverage starts at $50.**

Distance, access complexity, urgent timing, extended showing time, multiple-property coverage, or materially expanded scope may affect pricing.

## Professional Open House Coverage

- **Standalone — $200, up to 3 hours.**
- **Included — one session** with an applicable $599 pay-at-successful-close Transaction Support engagement for a qualifying listing transaction.
- **Additional qualifying sessions — $100 each, up to 3 hours** under that same $599 engagement.
- The $389 prepaid Transaction Support option does not include an open house.

Standard scope may include approved flyer preparation, available approved signage setup/takedown, approved digital promotion, up to three hours of licensed hosting, visitor notes, and a post-event summary.

## Monthly Operations Partnership

- **Starter — $299/month · up to 3 hours.**
- **Growth — $599/month · up to 7 hours.**
- **Partner — $999/month · up to 12 hours.**

Monthly support is capacity-based and scope-based. It is not unlimited assistant access.

No automatic Contract & Document Support discount or other service discount is included with a monthly tier unless a specific approved pricing variation is documented.

## Separate Referral Partner Option

The Referral Partner Option is **not a Koinonia Transactions service or service price**.

**40% referral fee on qualifying successfully closed referred business.**

Complete brokerage, compensation, transaction, and referral terms are provided in the formal referral documents before client handoff.

A Koinonia Transactions support relationship never becomes a brokerage referral unless the Realtor explicitly chooses to refer the client.

## Pricing Notes

- Starting prices assume standard scope.
- Nonstandard scope may require a documented custom quote.
- Service availability and timing remain subject to capacity and agreed scope.
- Koinonia does not publish or promise discounts that are not contained in an approved pricing rule or documented custom support variation.

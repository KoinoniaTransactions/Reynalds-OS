# Koinonia Transactions — Pricing Insert

Status: Active Client-Facing Pricing Companion  
Purpose: Easy-to-update pricing companion for the Brokerage Introduction Sheet and Service Guide.

## Pricing Philosophy

Pricing supports the service decision; it is not the Koinonia value proposition.

Koinonia competes on dependable execution, clarity, professional boundaries, and operational partnership. Use this insert after the Realtor understands which type of support fits the need.

## Transaction Support

### Prepaid Transaction Support — $389

- $389 per transaction.
- Due before transaction work begins.
- Lower price reflects upfront payment.
- If the transaction cancels after work begins, the fee remains earned unless Koinonia decides otherwise.
- Does **not** include Professional Open House Coverage.

### Pay-at-Successful-Close Transaction Support — $599

- $599 due only after a successful closing.
- No coordination fee if the transaction does not close.
- Higher price reflects Koinonia assuming closing/payment risk.
- For an applicable listing transaction, includes **one Professional Open House Coverage session**.

## Contract & Document Support

- **Single-document support: starting at $75.**
- Multi-document, offer-package, urgent, or materially expanded requests are scope-based.
- Scope and price are confirmed before work begins.

## Licensed Showing Coverage

- **Local showing coverage: starting at $50.**
- Distance, access complexity, urgent timing, extended showing time, multiple-property coverage, or expanded scope may affect pricing.

## Professional Open House Coverage

- **Standalone session: $200, up to 3 hours.**
- **One session included** with an applicable $599 pay-at-successful-close Transaction Support engagement for a qualifying listing transaction.
- **Additional qualifying sessions: $100 each, up to 3 hours** under that same $599 engagement.
- The $389 prepaid Transaction Support option does not include an open house.

Standard service scope may include approved flyer preparation, available approved signage setup/takedown, approved digital promotion, up to three hours of licensed hosting, visitor notes, and a post-event summary.

## Monthly Operations Partnership

### Starter — $299/month

Up to 3 hours of recurring operations support.

### Growth — $599/month

Up to 7 hours of recurring operations support.

### Partner — $999/month

Up to 12 hours of recurring operations support.

Monthly support is capacity-based and scope-based. It is not unlimited assistant access.

No automatic Contract & Document Support discount or other service discount is included with a monthly tier unless a specific approved pricing variation is documented.

## Separate Referral Partner Option

The Referral Partner Option is not a Koinonia Transactions service or service price.

**40% referral fee on qualifying successfully closed referred business.**

Complete brokerage, compensation, transaction, and referral terms are provided in the formal referral documents before client handoff.

A Koinonia Transactions relationship never becomes a brokerage referral unless the Realtor explicitly chooses to refer the client.

## Pricing Notes

- Starting prices assume standard scope.
- Nonstandard scope may require a documented custom quote.
- Koinonia does not publish or promise discounts that are not contained in an approved pricing rule or custom support variation.
- Service availability and timing remain subject to capacity and agreed scope.

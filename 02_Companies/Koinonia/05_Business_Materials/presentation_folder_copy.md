## Standard Folder Contents

Recommended default stacking order:

1. **Brokerage Introduction Sheet** — first visible leave-behind and conversation recap.
2. **Service Guide** — deeper service explanation.
3. **Pricing Insert** — current replaceable pricing companion.
4. **Business Card** — placed in the folder's card slot when available.

Optional meeting-specific additions:

- estimate or proposal
- engagement-specific scope sheet
- referral documents when the Realtor explicitly chooses the referral path
- other approved brokerage or operational materials relevant to the conversation

The Tri-Fold Brochure is generally a separate networking/event piece and does not need to be included in every presentation folder.

The Digital Introduction Packet is primarily a digital follow-up and does not need to be printed by default.

## Referral Rule

The permanent folder shell should not market the 40% Referral Partner Option as a sixth service.

Referral information belongs in the replaceable collateral or meeting-specific documents when relevant. A Koinonia Transactions support relationship never becomes a brokerage referral unless the Realtor explicitly chooses the referral path.

## Professional Boundary

The folder shell should not attempt to carry full boundary language. The included Brokerage Introduction Sheet and Service Guide contain the appropriate professional-boundary statements.

Koinonia remains positioned as operational support, not unlimited assistant access, brokerage supervision, legal/tax/financial advice, or a replacement for the Realtor-client relationship.

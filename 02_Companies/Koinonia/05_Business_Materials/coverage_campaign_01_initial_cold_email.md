# Why This Copy Fits the Campaign

The email deliberately uses the same core campaign truth as paid social:

- real estate does not happen one thing at a time;
- the pain is overlapping responsibilities, not merely paperwork;
- Koinonia offers broader operational coverage;
- the approved service stack is transaction management, contract preparation, licensed showing coverage, and business operations support;
- the Realtor keeps the client relationship and gains capacity behind it.

The email does not use `Your TC can't do that` as the opening line because a first cold email should not insult or challenge a prospect's current support structure. The broader Coverage campaign can still use that hook in creative where the specific problem is clearly outside ordinary transaction coordination.

# Format Standard

Use a plain, lightly formatted email rather than a graphic-heavy newsletter for the first cold touch.

Reasons:

- should feel like a direct professional introduction;
- easier to read quickly on mobile;
- avoids turning the first touch into a brochure;
- keeps the message focused on one problem and one next step.

Do not attach a brochure to Email 1 unless separately approved.

# Engagement Handling

- Open: low-confidence signal only; no visit trigger.
- Verified human click: owner notification / engagement watch.
- Repeat verified click or click + meaningful website activity: office-visit review candidate.
- Positive/question reply: immediate human follow-up; automated follow-up stops.
- Consultation/lead form: immediate high-priority human follow-up.
- Negative reply / DNC / unsubscribe: suppress.
- Bounce: stop sending to the failed address until corrected/reverified.

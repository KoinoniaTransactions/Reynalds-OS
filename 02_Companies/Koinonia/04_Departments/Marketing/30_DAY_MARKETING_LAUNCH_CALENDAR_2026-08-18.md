# Koinonia Transactions — First 30-Day Marketing Launch Calendar

Status: Active Launch Plan  
Launch Window: August 18, 2026 through September 16, 2026  
Owner: Marketing + Sales  
Governing Sources: Marketing & Growth Engine, Digital Marketing Execution Playbook, Marketing Learning & Relationship Capture System

## Goal

Launch Koinonia's first repeatable digital acquisition and relationship-development cycle without scattering effort across too many channels or automating before the sales logic is proven.

Primary 30-day outcomes:

1. Establish a consistent Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok presence.
2. Begin controlled direct Realtor and brokerage outreach.
3. Launch one focused Meta lead-generation test.
4. Create a reliable same-day lead follow-up habit.
5. Capture every meaningful contact's source, pressure, exact language when useful, next action, and next-action date.
6. Learn which problems and hooks create qualified conversations and first paid engagements.

## Channel Decision

Use:

- Facebook
- Instagram
- TikTok
- email
- website
- direct Realtor outreach
- brokerage/team outreach

Do not use:

- LinkedIn

## 30-Day Focus Rule

Do not start additional paid channels during this launch window.

Paid test:

- Meta: Facebook + Instagram lead generation

Organic creative-learning channel:

- TikTok

TikTok paid advertising is a Phase 2 action after Koinonia has organic content worth amplifying.

## Working Paid-Test Budget

Meta launch test:

- $20/day
- 14-day initial test
- maximum initial test spend: $280
- no automatic budget increase
- review lead quality before extending or increasing spend

This is a Koinonia working test budget, not a platform minimum or guarantee of results.

## Success Hierarchy

Judge results in this order:

1. meaningful conversations
2. qualified consultations or direct service requests
3. first paid engagements
4. source-to-paid conversion
5. cost per qualified relationship / first paid engagement
6. diagnostic metrics such as CPL, clicks, views, reach, watch time, saves, and comments

Do not optimize around cheap leads that do not become meaningful Realtor relationships.

# Daily Lead Response SOP

Whenever a meaningful lead appears from Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, email, website, event, or direct outreach:

1. Create or update one relationship record.
2. Record source and source detail.
3. Record the pressure category.
4. Preserve exact language when useful.
5. Respond in the channel where the conversation is active unless moving channels is actually useful.
6. Ask one diagnostic question before prescribing when the need is unclear.
7. Recommend the smallest sufficient engagement.
8. Assign the next action.
9. Assign the next-action date.
10. Do not leave an active relationship without a next step.

## Diagnostic question bank

Transaction/file:

- "What's the part of the file that keeps falling back on you right now?"

Documents:

- "Do you already know the decision you want reflected, or are you still working through the decision with the client?"

Showing:

- "What's the date, location, and access situation?"

Open House:

- "What's the property, preferred date, and what has already been planned or promoted?"

Monthly Operations:

- "If I looked at your business today, what is least organized: pipeline, follow-up, tasks, CRM, calendar, or workflow?"

Referral:

- "Are you saying you want to keep the client and need capacity help, or that you've decided you do not want/cannot take the client?"

That last question protects the keep-client vs referral boundary.

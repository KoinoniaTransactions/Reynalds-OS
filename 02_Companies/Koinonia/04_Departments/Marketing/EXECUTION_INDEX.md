# Koinonia Marketing Execution Index

Status: Active Execution Index  
Business: Koinonia Transactions  
Effective Date: 2026-08-17

## Purpose

Keep the strategy, operating rules, learning system, and active campaign execution clearly separated so temporary launch tactics do not become accidental permanent strategy.

## Hierarchy

### 1. Strategy

`README.md` — Marketing & Growth Engine

Defines:

- category and positioning
- audiences
- funnel
- channel strategy
- campaign pillars
- content model
- conversion paths
- measurement principles

### 2. Relationship Learning

`MARKETING_LEARNING_RELATIONSHIP_CAPTURE_SYSTEM.md`

Defines:

- one shared relationship learning record
- prospect problem-language capture
- diagnosis and service recommendation logic
- lifecycle and capture moments
- learning-signal levels
- weekly/monthly/quarterly review cadence

### 3. Execution Rules

`DIGITAL_MARKETING_EXECUTION_PLAYBOOK.md`

Defines:

- approved digital channel stack
- contact-to-client response rules
- Facebook / Instagram / TikTok / email roles
- organic social cadence
- paid-media sequence
- source taxonomy
- UTM rules
- follow-up and nurture logic
- lead-response expectations
- first-50-relationship operating rules

Current approved social/digital channels:

- Facebook
- Instagram
- TikTok
- email
- website
- direct Realtor outreach
- brokerage/team outreach

LinkedIn is not used for Koinonia Transactions marketing.

### 4. Activation Control

`MARKETING_ACTIVATION_READINESS_PLAN.md` and `30_DAY_MARKETING_ACTIVATION_TEMPLATE.md`

Define the current activation gates and relative execution schedule. These documents control live
implementation sequencing.

`STAGE_2A_ORGANIC_CONTENT_PRODUCTION_FOUNDATION.md`

Defines the build-and-review layer between Stage 1 infrastructure readiness and live Stage 2
publishing. Stage 2A authorizes production of the three-asset organic pilot package, but it does
not authorize publishing, scheduling, outreach, email, or paid media.

### 5. Historical Launch Content Library

`30_DAY_MARKETING_LAUNCH_CALENDAR_2026-08-18.md`

Contains the prepared first-cycle copy and campaign assets, including:

- day-by-day launch actions
- Facebook and Instagram posts
- TikTok scripts
- Instagram Story prompts
- Realtor email sequence
- warm-contact follow-up
- brokerage outreach sequence
- permission-based nurture email
- Meta lead-generation campaign build sheet
- lead-response SOP
- Month 1 reporting template

Its fixed Aug 18–Sep 16 dates and automatic-send assumptions are superseded. Use the content only
after the applicable activation gate is approved, and schedule it relative to the actual activation
date. Month 2 should be adjusted from real relationship/outcome data rather than copied forward.

## Governance Rule

If a campaign test reveals a useful learning-layer change, update the execution layer first.

Do not change stable service, pricing, brand, referral, or positioning rules based on a single campaign result or isolated anecdote.

# Koinonia Properties — Business Positioning and Local Discovery Strategy

Decision date: 2026-11-03  
Owner decision: Approved strategic positioning; execution remains gated  
Scope: Koinonia Properties residential brokerage, property management, and relationship to Koinonia Transactions  
Authority: This document governs the **new cross-business positioning decision** and proposed marketing architecture, not legal registration, brokerage licensing, operational readiness, or permission to publish.

## 1. Approved positioning

**Koinonia Properties will be positioned as a full-service real estate company, equally committed to residential real estate sales and property management, with property management as the initial Google Maps / Google Business Profile discovery priority.**

Interpretation:
- Neither residential brokerage nor property management is a secondary or incidental business line.
- The overall **Koinonia Properties** name represents both lines equally.
- The Properties website should ultimately present distinct, equally prominent customer pathways for **buying and selling** and **rental ownership / property management**, while retaining appropriate paths for renters and existing residents.
- Prioritizing property management on Maps does **not** mean making management the dominant public identity across all channels.
- Any primary Google Business Profile category must accurately describe the actual operating business and comply with Google's then-current guidelines; the working candidate is **Property management company**, with **Real estate agency** as an additional category if eligible and accurate.
- Do not create a separate third Maps profile for real estate sales simply to distinguish it from property management.

## 2. Brand and legal-entity direction — separate decision from implementation

The owner has selected **two LLCs as the preferred planned structure**, pending formation and regulatory validation:

1. **Koinonia Transactions LLC** — existing entity; B2B Realtor/team/brokerage support and administrative/operational services.
2. **Koinonia Properties LLC** — **proposed new entity**, intended to be the appropriately licensed real estate brokerage encompassing residential sales, leasing, and property management.

These are two proposed market-facing businesses under the shared Koinonia brand family; **three marketed service lines**, not three mandatory LLCs. A holding-company relationship may be documented separately when confirmed. The existence, formation, licensing status, trade names, broker-of-record, and operating authority of Properties LLC **must not be inferred from this strategy approval**.

Do not represent Koinonia Transactions as an independent licensed brokerage merely because a related company may hold brokerage authority. Licensed work, supervision, compensation, referrals, advertising, and disclosures require applicable approval and documented compliant handling.

The existing Koinonia Transactions brand and approved public commercial claims remain governed by their own current canonical sources; this document does not replace that marketing model or authorize Transactions site edits.

## 3. Distinct customer and acquisition models

| Business line | Core customer | Positioning | Primary acquisition paths | Primary conversion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Koinonia Transactions | Realtors, teams, brokerages | White-glove real estate operations and professional support | Professional outreach, referrals, partnerships, dedicated B2B content, search and social | Consultation / service inquiry |
| Koinonia Properties — Residential Sales | Buyers and sellers, including investment buyers/sellers | Full-service residential brokerage and representation | Local sales SEO, listings, referrals, search ads, community content, social media | Buyer or seller consultation |
| Koinonia Properties — Property Management | Rental owners, landlords, real estate investors | Responsible rental management through clear communication, steady systems, and property care | Google Maps / local discovery, property-management SEO, search ads, owner referrals | Rental analysis / management consultation |

Marketing assets should reflect **audience intent** rather than funneling every visitor to a single generic services page.

## 4. Google Maps / Business Profile strategy

**Working target: two independently eligible Google Business Profiles, not three.**

- **Koinonia Transactions:** preserve the existing verified profile; represent only legitimate services and in-person activities.
- **Koinonia Properties:** seek a dedicated service-area business profile for the genuine Properties brokerage/management operation when established and eligible.
- Proposed Properties category architecture: primary `Property management company`, additional `Real estate agency` where both are correct and Google offers those categories.
- Property management is the **initial local Maps discovery priority**, while residential sales retains equal prominence on the website and independent paid/organic lead generation.
- Both brands may operate from the same home office; **no customers are received at that home office**. For any qualifying service-area profile, hide the residential street address rather than displaying it as a storefront.
- Work may be delivered remotely or by genuine in-person appointments/field services according to the engagement.
- Different LLCs, websites, customer groups, and branding help explain operational distinction, but **do not guarantee** a second profile at the same address. Google may request verification evidence or treat a profile as a duplicate.
- **Never** create artificial offices, invented staff, false signage, sham separate operations, or duplicate listings to influence approval.
- Create no new Google Business Profile, alter no existing profile categories, and submit no verification until a later owner-approved implementation checkpoint.

The choice to optimize for property-management discovery is an **initial marketing hypothesis**, not a claim that actual Parker or Castle Rock rankings, search demand, conversion, or CAC have been proven. Validate competitor Maps results, local rankings, search volume, and lead performance before significant spend or irreversible category decisions.

## 6. Marketing separation with shared platform

A single cross-company operating system may support shared reporting and infrastructure while maintaining company-level record ownership, permissions, marketing consent, customer segments, lead routing, attribution, and appropriate brokerage/client confidentiality.

Maintain distinct conversion journeys and metrics:
- Transactions: qualified Realtor/team leads and service engagements.
- Sales: buyer/seller inquiries, consultations, appointments, and closed transactions.
- Property management: rental analyses, qualified owner leads, agreements, and managed units.

Do not infer that sharing a platform permits unrestricted customer data sharing, cross-selling or transfer of regulated client information. Follow consent, privacy, brokerage supervision, referral and advertising requirements.

## 7. Culture and voice

Preserve the shared Koinonia culture: professional, calm, organized, responsive, trustworthy, thoughtful, detail-oriented, personal and service-oriented. Avoid overpromising, inflated statistics, fake testimonials, and unfinished technology claims.

- Transactions: emphasize relieving Realtor operational burden.
- Residential sales: emphasize trust, clear guidance, responsive representation and client-centered decisions.
- Management: emphasize clear owner communication, predictable processes, responsible property care, and transparent service scope.

Do not mechanically reuse Transactions' Realtor-specific slogan as the Properties sales-and-management value proposition.

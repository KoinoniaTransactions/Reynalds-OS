# Koinonia Transactions — Brokerage Relationship Playbook

Status: Build-Mode Operating Playbook  
Business: Koinonia Transactions  
Owner: Marketing + Sales  
Effective Date: 2026-08-17

## Purpose

Define how Koinonia develops brokerage and team relationships as a separate account-based marketing lane.

This playbook is a build-stage system definition. It does not authorize contacting brokerages or agents.

Until activation is explicitly approved:

- do not email brokerage leaders
- do not call brokerage offices
- do not request presentation time
- do not DM agents as part of brokerage outreach
- do not enroll brokerage contacts in nurture

---

# Core Brokerage Principle

A brokerage relationship is more valuable than a one-time presentation if it creates repeated, trusted access to Realtors who understand when Koinonia is useful.

The objective is not:

**Get one office to buy everything.**

The objective is:

**Become a credible optional operations-support resource that brokerage leaders are comfortable allowing agents to learn about.**

Koinonia should be positioned as:

- practical support
- clearly bounded
- agent-client relationship preserving
- complementary to brokerage systems
- optional, not compulsory

---

# Brokerage Account Types

## 1. Large Brokerage Office

Potential value:

- many agents
- recurring meetings/training opportunities
- leadership relationship can create repeated exposure

Risk:

- more internal systems/vendors
- leadership gatekeeping
- slower approval path

## 2. Independent Brokerage

Potential value:

- direct access to leadership
- faster relationship development
- strong local credibility

Risk:

- smaller total agent base

## 3. Real Estate Team

Potential value:

- concentrated production
- obvious scheduling/capacity pressure
- easier to identify a specific use case

Risk:

- may already have internal ops staff

Position Koinonia as overflow/specialized capacity rather than replacing internal staff.

## 4. Brokerage Network / Multi-Office Group

Potential value:

- one relationship can scale to multiple offices

Risk:

- requires more formal approval and consistent messaging

Do not pursue scale before one-office learning is clean.

# Account Selection Framework

A brokerage account is a good research target when several of these are true:

- Colorado-based or meaningful Colorado agent presence
- active residential Realtor population
- regular office/team meetings
- visible training/agent-development culture
- leadership contact path is public/legitimate
- local geography makes showing/open-house services useful
- Koinonia can explain a small useful entry point
- relationship could create more than one meaningful Realtor conversation

Do not prioritize solely by office size.

A smaller office with an accessible, education-oriented leader may be more valuable than a huge office with no practical relationship path.

# Account Priority

## Priority A

Strong fit plus clear leadership access and a useful education angle.

## Priority B

Good fit but relationship path is less direct or internal support may already be extensive.

## Priority C

Potential future account but not worth early activation effort.

Priority does not authorize outreach.

---

# Right Leadership Contact

Preferred initial contact roles:

- team leader
- managing/employing broker
- office leader
- productivity/agent-development leader
- training/education leader
- team owner/operator

Avoid starting with multiple leaders at the same account simultaneously.

Choose one primary relationship owner.

If the primary contact redirects Koinonia to another leader, record the handoff and use that route.

---

# Brokerage First Ask

The preferred first ask is educational, not a vendor pitch.

Core concept:

**A 15–20 minute practical session on protecting client service when transaction volume and operational workload compete for an agent's time.**

The session should help agents recognize:

- transaction/file capacity pressure
- document workload
- showing conflicts
- open-house/listing capacity
- CRM/follow-up pressure
- when to keep a client with support vs deliberately refer the opportunity

The purpose is to teach where support fits.

Do not make the first meeting a pricing presentation.

---

# Leader Outreach Structure — Future Implementation

## Message 1 — Practical Education Ask

Structure:

1. why the office/team is relevant
2. one-sentence Koinonia category
3. describe the practical agent session
4. emphasize optional support and boundaries
5. ask whether it would be useful for their agents

Suggested frame:

**I’d like to provide your agents a short practical session on protecting client service when transaction volume starts competing with their time.**

## Message 2 — Clarify What Agents Learn

Explain the real scenarios:

- two showings at once
- open-house conflict
- file follow-through
- document workflow
- business organization

## Message 3 — Pressure Question

Ask what pressure the leader sees most often among agents.

This begins market learning before the presentation.

## Message 4 — Close the Loop

Stop active pursuit respectfully if there is no response.

Do not run an endless brokerage sequence.

# Pre-Meeting Checklist

Before any brokerage presentation:

## Account Context

- correct brokerage/team name
- correct office/location
- leader/host confirmed
- expected audience size
- agent experience mix if known
- session length confirmed
- in-person/virtual format confirmed

## Koinonia Materials

Use the presentation-folder stack when in person:

1. Brokerage Introduction Sheet
2. Service Guide
3. Pricing Insert
4. Business Card

Digital packet may be sent afterward when useful.

Do not overload the room with every collateral asset.

## Operational Readiness

- consultation/contact path live
- QR/link destination tested if used
- current service/pricing truth verified
- referral path clearly separated
- contact capture method ready
- follow-up owner identified

## Boundaries

Be prepared to explain:

- Koinonia does not replace broker supervision
- Koinonia does not replace Realtor decision-making
- Koinonia support relationship does not become a referral without explicit Realtor choice
- scope/licensing boundaries

---

# 15–20 Minute Presentation Format

Recommended structure:

## Minute 0–2 — Why This Matters

Opening problem:

**More transactions should create more opportunity, not more chaos.**

Ask agents to think about the operational task that most often competes with client time.

## Minute 2–5 — The Five Pressure Areas

Explain briefly:

1. transaction/file follow-through
2. contracts/documents
3. showing conflicts
4. open-house/listing capacity
5. CRM/follow-up/business organization

Do not start with pricing.

## Minute 5–10 — Real Scenarios

Use practical situations:

- two clients need showings at the same time
- listing deserves an open house but agent cannot host
- transaction workload keeps returning to the agent
- decision is made but document workflow needs execution
- follow-up/CRM work is consuming selling/client time

## Minute 10–13 — How Koinonia Fits

Explain the five service lanes and smallest-useful-engagement philosophy.

Key line:

**The first engagement should earn the second engagement.**

## Minute 13–15 — Keep vs Refer

Clarify:

- need capacity but want to keep client -> Koinonia Transactions support
- do not want/cannot take client -> separate 40% Referral Partner path

Protect the invariant:

**A Koinonia Transactions support relationship never becomes a brokerage referral unless the Realtor explicitly chooses the referral path.**

## Minute 15–20 — Questions / Discussion

Ask:

**Which of these pressure points shows up most often in your business?**

Use the discussion as learning data.

# Presentation Style

Keep it:

- practical
- educational
- conversational
- low-pressure
- scenario-based
- respectful of existing systems

Avoid:

- long company history
- software demo
- exhaustive pricing walkthrough
- criticizing the brokerage's processes
- implying agents are disorganized
- claiming every agent needs Koinonia

# Agent-Level Follow-Up Boundary

Attending a brokerage presentation does not automatically enroll an agent into sales email.

Individual follow-up is appropriate when:

- agent asks a question requiring follow-up
- agent provides contact information for follow-up
- leader makes a direct introduction
- agent requests material/consultation
- another legitimate relationship context exists

Do not obtain a full office roster and automatically email every attendee afterward unless the brokerage and applicable email process clearly support it.

---

# Post-Meeting Follow-Up

## Leader Follow-Up

Within a normal professional timeframe:

- thank the leader
- summarize useful themes heard
- ask whether any follow-up resource would help
- propose next account-level step only if warranted

## Individual Agent Follow-Up

Reference:

- the actual session
- their actual question/pressure
- the smallest relevant next step

Do not send generic "thanks for attending, here are all five services" emails to every person.

# Brokerage Account Status Model

## Research

Account identified but not reviewed.

## Qualified

Good-fit account with a legitimate leadership path.

## Ready for Activation Review

Leader/contact/message prepared but no outreach authorized.

## Outreach Active

Used only after implementation activation.

## Conversation

Leader/host has meaningfully engaged.

## Session Planned

Presentation scheduled.

## Session Completed

Presentation delivered; learning/follow-up captured.

## Ongoing Relationship

Repeated access, referrals, agent introductions, or trusted-resource status exists.

## Hold / Not Now

Potential fit but timing is wrong.

## Closed / No Response

Active sequence complete without response.

## Do Not Contact

Explicit stop or non-fit.

- organization/account

- sessions
- source
- next action
- next-action date

# Brokerage Learning Questions

After each meaningful brokerage interaction, ask internally:

- what pressure does this leader see most often?
- what support do agents already have?
- where does Koinonia complement existing systems?
- what creates skepticism?
- which service is easiest to understand?
- which service creates confusion?
- does the referral path need more/less explanation?
- what question should the next presentation answer better?

---

# Brokerage Relationship Expansion Rule

Do not ask for another office introduction before delivering value in the current relationship.

Expansion becomes appropriate when:

- leader found session useful
- agents engaged meaningfully
- Koinonia has delivered successfully
- relationship trust exists

Then ask naturally whether another office/team leader might find the same session useful.

---

# Conflict / Existing Vendor Rule

If a brokerage already has:

- internal TC staff
- preferred vendors
- showing support
- operations coaching

Do not attack or undermine those resources.

Ask where gaps remain, if any.

Koinonia may fit as:

- overflow
- licensed showing coverage
- open-house coverage
- narrow document support
- agent-specific optional capacity

If there is no gap, do not manufacture one.

---

# Brokerage Compliance / Boundary Rule

Before implementation, verify any brokerage-specific policies about outside vendors, agent education, solicitation, and licensed activity.

Koinonia should cooperate with leadership rather than route around brokerage rules.

Do not present Koinonia as authorized/preferred by a brokerage unless the brokerage has explicitly granted that status.

---

# Build-Mode Activation Gate

Brokerage outreach remains inactive until all are true:

1. Koinonia has entered implementation mode.
2. account-selection framework is approved.
3. first target accounts are freshly researched.
4. leader contact route is legitimate.
5. leader email sequence is approved.
6. presentation deck/material stack is final.
7. website/contact destination is live.
8. meeting capture template is ready.
9. individual agent follow-up boundaries are understood.
10. account-level CRM handling is ready.
11. compliance/brokerage-policy considerations are reviewed.
12. the user explicitly approves brokerage activation.

Until then:

**Accounts remain research/qualified only. No outreach occurs.**

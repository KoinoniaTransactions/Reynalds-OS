# Koinonia Digital Marketing Execution Playbook

Status: Active Execution Layer  
Business: Koinonia Transactions  
Owner: Marketing + Sales  
Effective Date: 2026-08-17

## Purpose

Turn Koinonia's approved Marketing & Growth Engine into a repeatable acquisition, follow-up, nurture, and conversion system.

This document does not replace the governing Marketing & Growth Engine or Marketing Learning & Relationship Capture System. It operationalizes them.

Core operating rule:

**Every meaningful contact gets a source, a pressure/problem, a next action, and a next-action date.**

Koinonia should never have a meaningful relationship sitting in a database with no known next move.

## Approved Digital Channel Stack

Use:

- Facebook
- Instagram
- TikTok
- email
- Koinonia website
- direct Realtor outreach
- brokerage/team relationship outreach
- referral-network relationship building

Do not use:

- LinkedIn

LinkedIn should not be included in campaign calendars, paid-media plans, outreach sequences, content-production requirements, or reporting unless this governing decision is intentionally changed later.

## Channel Roles

### Facebook

Primary role:

- Colorado Realtor/community reach
- brokerage and professional-network reinforcement
- longer educational posts

- local credibility
- Meta paid acquisition and retargeting

### Instagram

Primary role:

- visual brand reinforcement
- Reels and short-form scenario content
- service proof/process content
- Stories for reminders, availability, event activity, and lightweight engagement
- Meta paid acquisition and retargeting

Instagram is not simply a static design gallery. Favor useful, human, scenario-based content.

### TikTok

Primary role:

- discovery
- human expertise
- problem recognition
- fast educational scenarios
- objection handling
- service-use cases
- testing hooks that can later become paid creative

TikTok should not be treated as Instagram Reels copied over without adaptation.

Preferred TikTok style:

- direct-to-camera when practical
- native-looking video rather than polished commercial production
- strong first sentence/hook
- one problem or lesson per video
- real estate operations scenarios
- clear but low-pressure CTA
- captions/on-screen text for comprehension

Start organic TikTok before meaningful paid TikTok spend. Use organic performance and audience response to identify videos worth amplifying through Spark Ads or adapting into Lead Generation campaigns.

Current platform implementation notes, last checked August 2026:

- TikTok supports native Lead Generation Instant Forms.
- TikTok supports website lead-generation campaigns.
- TikTok supports Messaging Ads.
- TikTok Spark Ads can amplify organic content.
- TikTok Lead Generation Instant Forms require a business privacy-policy URL.
- TikTok Ads Manager lead data is not a permanent CRM; leads should be moved into Koinonia's relationship system promptly.

## Core Acquisition Model

Every acquisition path should move toward the same relationship engine:

**Attention -> Contact Captured -> Pressure Identified -> Diagnosis -> Relevant Follow-Up -> Consultation or Smallest Useful Engagement -> Successful Delivery -> Second Engagement -> Advocate / Introduction / Referral Source**

Do not force every person into a consultation when a smaller direct service request is already obvious and appropriate.

## The Contact-to-Client Rule Set

### Situation 1 — Jeremiah personally meets a Realtor

Within the same day when practical:

Capture:

- name
- brokerage/team
- contact information available
- source/event/location
- exact problem language if useful
- likely pressure category
- material given
- agreed or likely next step

Within 24 hours:

Send a personal follow-up that references the actual conversation.

Do not send a generic mass-marketing email.

Recommended structure:

1. remind them where you met
2. reference the pressure they described
3. recommend the smallest useful starting point if clear
4. link/attach the Digital Introduction Packet only when useful
5. ask for one simple next action

### Situation 2 — Business card/contact information obtained but no deep conversation occurred

Within 24–48 hours:

Send a short personal introduction.

Goal:

**Get the Realtor to tell Koinonia which operational pressure costs them the most time.**

Do not immediately send every service and every price.

### Situation 3 — Realtor responds to a social post/comment/DM

Respond in the native platform first.

If the conversation becomes meaningful:

- identify their pressure
- ask permission to continue by email or phone when useful
- create/update the relationship record
- capture source as the specific social platform and content/campaign when known
- assign next action/date

Do not move someone off-platform merely to satisfy an internal workflow if the active conversation is progressing well where it started.

### Situation 4 — Organic TikTok engagement becomes a real lead

Examples:

- direct message asking for help
- comment clearly describing a service need
- profile/contact-form conversion attributable to TikTok
- lead-generation form submission

Action:

1. acknowledge quickly
2. identify whether this is transaction/file, document, showing, open-house, operations, or referral/no-capacity pressure
3. capture the relationship record
4. move to email/phone/consultation when appropriate
5. preserve the exact TikTok hook/video that generated the interaction

Do not judge TikTok only by views. Track whether specific hooks create meaningful conversations and paid relationships.

### Situation 5 — Website contact form submission

Immediate:

- send acknowledgment
- capture source/UTM/campaign when available
- assign lead owner

Same business day when practical:

- personally reply
- diagnose the pressure
- recommend the smallest useful next step

### Situation 6 — Meta Lead Form submission

Immediate automated action:

- confirmation/thank-you
- clear expectation that Koinonia will follow up

Operational action:

- create/update relationship record promptly
- source = Meta + campaign/ad/ad-set where available
- capture pressure response from form
- assign personal follow-up

Personal follow-up target:

- as quickly as practical during normal working hours
- same business day as a minimum operating standard

Do not rely on the lead form itself as the relationship record.

### Situation 7 — TikTok Lead Generation submission

Immediate automated action:

- confirmation/thank-you
- consultation or direct-contact next step

Operational action:

- move the lead into Koinonia's relationship system promptly
- capture source/campaign/video where available
- preserve the selected pressure response
- assign personal follow-up

Because platform lead storage is not permanent, TikTok lead data should not remain only in Ads Manager.

### Situation 8 — Cold/warm outbound email to a Realtor

Use personalized professional outreach.

Initial goal:

**Start a conversation about operational pressure, not force a service pitch.**

Recommended first-email structure:

- short reason for reaching out
- one sentence describing Koinonia's category
- one relevant question about operational pressure
- no large attachment unless context makes it useful

Follow-up sequence default:

- Day 0 — initial personal outreach
- Day 4 — useful scenario or observation
- Day 10 — relevant smallest-entry option
- Day 21 — close-the-loop message
- afterward — stop active pursuit and move to long-term nurture when appropriate

Do not send endless "checking in" emails.

### Situation 9 — Brokerage/team leader identified

Treat the brokerage/team as an account-level relationship while still creating individual relationship records for meaningful contacts.

Primary offer:

**Useful education and agent support, not a vendor sales pitch.**

Suggested meeting concept:

15–20 minute practical session on protecting client service when transaction volume and operational workload collide.

After the meeting:

- capture attendees/meaningful conversations when possible
- follow up individually based on each Realtor's pressure
- record material used
- track brokerage/team introduction source

### Situation 10 — Interested but not ready

Do not mark as dead unless the person clearly declines future contact.

Capture:

- reason not ready
- likely trigger
- expected timing if known
- next nurture date

Move to a useful nurture cadence rather than repeated sales pressure.

### Situation 11 — Existing Koinonia client

After successful delivery:

- capture proof point
- ask what next operational pressure remains
- recommend adjacent support only when earned

Examples:

- Showing -> Transaction Support
- Contract & Document -> Transaction Support
- Open House -> broader listing/transaction support
- Transaction Support -> Monthly Operations

Referral is not an automatic upsell from service work.

### Situation 12 — Realtor does not want/cannot take the client

Move into the separate Referral Partner path only when the Realtor explicitly chooses it.

Do not transform a normal Koinonia support relationship into a brokerage referral discussion without a clear client/opportunity decision from the Realtor.

## Pressure Categories

Every meaningful lead should map to one primary pressure when possible:

1. transaction/file capacity
2. contract/document workload
3. showing/schedule conflict
4. open-house/listing capacity
5. CRM/follow-up/business organization
6. referral/no-capacity client opportunity
7. brokerage/team operations
8. unclear/other

Preserve the prospect's own wording in addition to the structured category.

## Diagnosis Formula

Use the existing Koinonia formula:

**Because you told me [problem] is causing [consequence], I would start with [service] so we can [desired outcome].**

Start with the smallest sufficient engagement when possible.

## Organic Social Operating System

### Baseline weekly cadence

Facebook:

- 3 primary posts per week

Instagram:

- 3 primary feed/Reel posts per week
- Stories used as supporting activity when there is something useful to show

TikTok:

- 3–5 videos per week during the initial learning phase

TikTok receives the highest creative-testing cadence because discovery and hook learning are core goals.

### Weekly content jobs

Every week should include:

- Problem Recognition
- Education
- Proof / Process
- Invitation / Service Use Case

Do not require every individual post to perform all four jobs.

### Long-run content mix

Maintain the governing approximate ratio across the full content system:

- 40% operational education / problem recognition
- 25% service examples / use cases
- 15% proof / process / behind-the-scenes credibility
- 10% Professional Open House
- 10% Referral Partner

Dedicated campaign windows can temporarily change the ratio.

## TikTok Content System

### Primary TikTok series

1. **When a Realtor Needs Help With...**
   - one real scenario per video

2. **Operational Problems That Look Small Until Friday**
   - deadline, showing, file, CRM, open-house examples

3. **You Don't Need a Full-Time Assistant To...**
   - narrow service/use-case education

4. **Keep the Client / Refer the Opportunity**
   - explain the distinction carefully

5. **Behind the Operation**
   - preparation, checklists, process, materials, organization
   - never make the portal/software the product

6. **Real Estate Capacity Questions**
   - answer common Realtor objections and questions

### TikTok hook formula

Start with the pressure in the first sentence.

Examples:

- "If two clients need showings at the same time, you do not have to choose which client gets service."
- "A transaction should not depend on you remembering every next deadline yourself."
- "You can already have a TC and still need help with open houses."
- "More closings are supposed to create opportunity, not a bigger pile of follow-up."
- "Not every lead has to become another client on your calendar."

Then:

1. describe the real scenario
2. teach one useful idea
3. explain where Koinonia fits
4. give a soft CTA

### TikTok CTA examples

- "If this is the part of real estate stealing your time, send me a message."
- "If you are a Colorado Realtor dealing with this, Koinonia can help carry the operation."
- "If you want to see where support would fit in your business, the consultation link is in the profile."

Avoid hard-selling every video.

## Paid Media Sequence

### Phase 1 — Meta first

Primary paid platforms:

- Facebook
- Instagram

Initial campaign objective:

- qualified lead generation

Initial creative campaign:

**More Transactions Should Create More Opportunity, Not More Chaos.**

Lead-form qualifying question:

**What is taking the most time in your business right now?**

Answer choices:

- transaction/file follow-through
- contracts/documents
- showing conflicts
- open houses/listing support
- CRM/follow-up/organization
- I have client opportunities I do not want/cannot take
- something else

Keep lead-form friction low enough to convert while collecting enough information to route the conversation intelligently.

### Phase 2 — TikTok organic learning

Before meaningful TikTok ad spend:

- build consistent organic account activity
- test hooks and service scenarios
- identify videos creating profile visits, DMs, comments with buying intent, and website/consultation activity

### Phase 3 — TikTok paid amplification

Use winning organic content as the starting point.

Preferred progression:

1. Spark Ads to amplify proven organic videos
2. Lead Generation tests using native Instant Forms
3. website lead-generation tests after tracking is verified
4. retargeting/engaged-audience campaigns when enough activity exists

Do not launch paid TikTok simply because the account exists.

### Phase 4 — Quality optimization

As relationship/outcome data becomes reliable:

- send qualified lead and paid-engagement outcomes back to ad platforms where appropriate and compliant
- optimize toward real business value rather than cheapest form submission

Meta currently supports CRM/down-funnel signals through Conversions API, and TikTok supports lead-quality signal optimization. Implement only after Koinonia has clean source and outcome data.

## Email System

### Email lane A — personal relationship follow-up

Use for:

- people Jeremiah met
- introductions
- brokerage contacts
- meaningful social leads
- direct service inquiries

Voice:

- short
- specific
- personal
- problem-aware

### Email lane B — outbound relationship development

Use a controlled follow-up sequence.

Do not bulk-send generic pitches from a personal inbox.

### Email lane C — permission-based nurture/broadcast

Use for:

- useful operational education
- seasonal capacity reminders
- service-use examples
- occasional Open House campaign
- occasional Referral Partner education

Frequency starting point:

- 1–2 useful emails per month

Do not send a newsletter merely because a calendar says one is due.

### Email compliance requirement

Before scaled/broadcast email launches, ensure:

- accurate sender information
- non-deceptive subject lines
- valid business postal address in commercial email
- clear unsubscribe mechanism
- suppression of people who opt out
- records/process sufficient to honor opt-out requirements

Do not begin scaled bulk email until these operational requirements are satisfied.

## Social-to-Email Rule

Do not scrape every social follower into an email marketing list.

Move social relationships into email when:

- the person provides their email through a form
- they request information
- they provide contact information directly
- they otherwise give appropriate permission/context for professional follow-up

Maintain appropriate distinction between direct one-to-one business outreach and permission-based bulk marketing.

## UTM Naming Standard

Use a simple consistent convention for website links from campaigns.

Example structure:

- source: facebook / instagram / tiktok / email
- medium: paid_social / organic_social / email / referral
- campaign: plain-language campaign identifier
- content: creative/hook identifier

Do not create a new naming style for every campaign.

## Lead Status and Follow-Up Rules

Every active relationship must have:

- lifecycle stage
- next action
- next-action date

Suggested follow-up states:

- new / needs response
- contacted / awaiting reply
- conversation active
- consultation scheduled
- consultation complete / decision pending
- proposal or service request pending
- client / active
- nurture
- closed-lost
- do not contact

Do not confuse these operational follow-up states with the canonical relationship lifecycle; they support it.

## Lost / No-Response Rules

No response is not permission to chase indefinitely.

Default outbound sequence ends after the planned follow-ups.

Then either:

- move to long-term nurture when appropriate
- close as no response
- mark do-not-contact when requested

Capture lost/no-sale reason whenever known.

## 30-Day Launch Sequence

### Week 1 — Infrastructure

Complete:

- approved source taxonomy
- pressure categories
- follow-up statuses
- relationship minimum fields
- UTM naming
- contact-to-client SOP
- email compliance/footer requirements
- privacy-policy readiness for lead forms
- Meta Business/Ads account readiness
- TikTok Business Account readiness
- basic conversion-event definitions

Do not launch paid campaigns before lead routing is clear.

### Week 2 — Content & Outreach Assets

Build:

- 4 weeks of Facebook content
- 4 weeks of Instagram content
- 4 weeks of TikTok scripts/hooks
- personal warm follow-up templates
- outbound Realtor sequence
- brokerage outreach sequence
- nurture sequence
- Meta first-campaign creative/copy
- TikTok first organic video batch

### Week 3 — Launch Organic + Meta Test

Launch:

- consistent Facebook/Instagram/TikTok posting
- controlled direct Realtor outreach
- brokerage outreach
- first Meta Lead Generation campaign

Do not launch TikTok paid during the first week of organic posting unless there is already proven organic creative.

### Week 4 — Review & Optimize

Review:

- meaningful contacts by source
- qualified conversations by source
- pressure categories showing up
- exact language patterns
- Meta lead quality
- TikTok hooks generating buying-intent engagement
- consultation rate
- first paid engagement rate
- follow-up execution

Adjust learning-layer decisions only.

## Core KPIs

Primary business KPIs:

- meaningful new relationships
- qualified consultations
- consultation rate by source
- first paid engagements
- first paid engagement rate by source
- revenue by source
- repeat engagement rate
- first-to-second engagement conversion
- cross-service adoption
- brokerage/team introductions
- referral inquiries and accepted referrals

Diagnostic channel metrics:

- reach
- video views
- watch time
- saves
- comments
- profile visits
- website clicks
- cost per lead
- email opens/clicks where reliable

Do not optimize the business around diagnostic metrics alone.

## First 50 Relationship Rule

During the first 50 meaningful relationships:

- prioritize clean source and pressure capture
- personally review lead quality
- preserve exact language
- avoid over-automating diagnosis
- test message/hook variations
- measure which first services earn second engagements

Automation should support responsiveness and recordkeeping, not replace judgment.

## Automation Priority Order

Automate only after the manual operating logic is clear.

Priority order:

1. immediate lead acknowledgment
2. relationship-record creation/source attribution
3. follow-up reminders
4. nurture enrollment where appropriate
5. campaign/outcome reporting
6. lead-quality feedback to ad platforms

Do not automate:

- service diagnosis without human review when ambiguity matters
- referral-path conversion without explicit Realtor choice
- aggressive repeated outreach
- canonical strategy changes based on isolated signals

## Operating Summary

**Get attention through useful, recognizable problems.**  
**Capture the relationship.**  
**Listen for the pressure in the Realtor's own language.**  
**Diagnose before prescribing.**  
**Start with the smallest useful engagement.**  
**Follow up with a clear next action and date.**  
**Deliver well enough to earn the second engagement.**  
**Measure source quality and relationship value.**  
**Use Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, email, website, and direct/brokerage outreach.**  
**Do not use LinkedIn.**

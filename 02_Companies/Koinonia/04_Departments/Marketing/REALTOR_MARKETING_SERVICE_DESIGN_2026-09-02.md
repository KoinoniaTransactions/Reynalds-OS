# 1. Service Position

Koinonia Marketing & Growth should not be sold as a pile of graphics or a low-cost content subscription.

The product is **managed Realtor marketing execution** connected to the rest of the Realtor's business.

Core promise:

> **You tell us what is happening in your business. Koinonia turns it into consistent, professional marketing and keeps it moving.**

Koinonia can connect events already visible in the relationship — a new listing, open house, closing, client milestone, market update, database segment, or business goal — to marketing execution without forcing the Realtor to brief a separate agency for every request.

This is the structural advantage over standalone marketing providers.

---

# 2. What Koinonia Is Selling

Koinonia should sell five outcomes rather than dozens of creative tasks:

## A. Stay Visible

Maintain a consistent professional presence across the channels that matter without the Realtor planning and building every post.

Includes potential social calendar, branded content, captions, reels, publishing, Google Business Profile content, and visibility reporting.

## B. Market My Listings

Turn a new listing or open house into a coordinated marketing campaign using the media and property facts already being managed through Koinonia.

Includes potential listing launch assets, email, social, property pages, flyers, brochures, open-house promotion, paid promotion, and specialist media coordination.

## C. Stay in Front of My Database

Keep past clients, sphere, prospects, buyers, and sellers receiving intentional branded communication.

Includes potential newsletters, nurture, listing announcements, market updates, review/referral campaigns, and database segmentation.

## D. Grow My Brand

Improve the Realtor's public presentation and conversion infrastructure.

Includes potential brand profile, bios, website/landing pages, Google Business Profile, review system, marketing collateral, lead magnets, and campaign tracking.

## E. Help Me Generate Business

Coordinate campaigns intended to create appointments, leads, referrals, listing opportunities, or brokerage relationships.

Includes potential paid ads, farming/direct mail, database reactivation, referral campaigns, open-house lead workflows, landing pages, prospect research, and outreach support.

# 4. Recommended Marketing Products

These are working products, not yet approved pricing rules.

## Product 1 — Listing Launch Marketing

Purpose: Make every listing launch look organized and professional without requiring the Realtor to separately create/commission each piece.

### Base digital deliverables

- marketing intake populated from Listing Operations
- property messaging / feature copy from approved facts
- Just Listed social post
- Story/reel-ready vertical asset
- email/e-flyer
- printable/open-house flyer
- property QR / tracked link
- open-house promotional asset if scheduled
- under-contract / sold assets available later in lifecycle

### Optional specialist media

- photography
- drone
- video
- floor plan
- 3D tour
- virtual staging
- twilight media

### Optional amplification

- paid Meta campaign
- database email blast
- direct mail
- property landing page / single-property site

### Packaging principle

The **Koinonia coordination/creative fee** and **third-party media/ad/printing costs** must remain visible internally and contractually, even when the client sees one simple order experience.

---

## Product 2 — Social Presence

Purpose: Keep the Realtor visible and professionally branded without requiring them to create a content plan every week.

### Suggested baseline scope

- brand/profile setup
- monthly content calendar
- 8–12 core pieces/month
- mix of educational, operational, local, personality/brand, proof, and listing/event content
- captions
- branded graphics/carousels
- short-form/faceless video where appropriate
- scheduling/publishing to agreed channels
- one monthly performance summary

### Important rule

Do not promise manufactured personal authenticity. If the content requires the Realtor's voice, opinion, face, story, local insight, or personal video, create a low-friction input process and produce from that source material.

# 5. Working Pricing Architecture

## Market benchmarks observed September 2026

Current public market pricing ranges widely:

- DIY/template content: roughly $29–$100/month.
- Light managed social/content: roughly $197–$700/month.
- Managed Realtor social: roughly $495–$1,300/month.
- Full multi-channel marketing management: roughly $1,250–$3,200/month.
- Listing-specific digital marketing packs: approximately $75–$150+ per listing at low-cost providers, excluding major photography/media costs.
- Denver professional listing-media packages observed around $249–$1,399+ depending on photography, drone, video, 3D/floor plans, and luxury production.

## Koinonia pricing philosophy

Do not compete with $59 template libraries.

Koinonia is selling coordination, customization, integration with the Realtor's actual business, and white-glove execution.

# 7. Marketing Workflow Integration

Marketing should be triggered by real business events already known to Koinonia.

Examples:

## Listing lifecycle triggers

Listing intake approved → create marketing work order.  
Photography delivered → produce launch assets.  
Listing live → publish/send approved launch campaign.  
Open house scheduled → create/publish open-house assets.  
Price change → update approved marketing surfaces.  
Under contract → queue status asset/update.  
Closed → queue sold/proof/post-close campaign.

## Transaction lifecycle triggers

Closing confirmed → queue closing/client-experience workflow.  
Transaction closed → review/referral/post-close nurture workflow.

## CRM triggers

Lead entered → source/tag/follow-up workflow.  
Lead stalled → reactivation queue.  
Client anniversary → nurture touchpoint.  
Review missing → review-request workflow.  
Referral source identified → thank-you/nurture workflow.

This event-driven integration is a major Koinonia advantage over disconnected marketing vendors.

# 8. Realtor Input Requirement

White-glove does not mean zero information from the Realtor.

Koinonia should minimize input to structured approvals and occasional personal source material.

Recommended input system:

- onboarding brand questionnaire once
- saved brand assets / brokerage disclosures
- approved service areas/niches
- content preferences / prohibited topics
- easy voice-note/text submission for personal insights
- listing facts pulled from Listing Operations
- simple approve/revise workflow
- standing permissions for repeatable campaign types when appropriate

Goal:

> The Realtor spends minutes approving/feeding the system, not hours creating marketing.

---

# 9. Guardrails

Koinonia Marketing must not:

- invent property facts;
- invent production claims, awards, reviews, testimonials, or statistics;
- publish brokerage-sensitive advertising without required approvals/disclosures;
- use copyrighted media without appropriate rights;
- promise leads, closings, rankings, or ROI as guaranteed outcomes;
- misrepresent Koinonia as the brokerage or responsible Realtor;
- post personal-brand opinions/positions as though they came from the Realtor without approval;
- conceal external ad spend, print/postage, or specialist costs inside ambiguous pricing.

# 11. Working Recommendation

Launch design should prioritize three products:

1. **Listing Launch Marketing** — because it connects immediately to the listing-operations expansion and produces obvious tangible value.
2. **Marketing Partner** — a recurring mid-tier program for the busy solo Realtor.
3. **White-Glove Marketing Office** — premium managed marketing for high producers/small teams and the future full Koinonia partnership.

Then build the broader integrated **Hand Us the Listing** and **Realtor Management / White-Glove Partnership** offers around those capabilities.

The end state is not "Koinonia also does marketing."

The end state is:

> **Koinonia knows what is happening in the Realtor's business and makes sure the operational work and marketing around it actually get done.**

# 1. Transaction Management

## Price

**$450 per successful closing**

## Sales headline

### **Hand us the executed deal. We'll keep the contract-to-close work moving.**

## Short explanation

Once you have a signed contract, Koinonia takes ownership of the normal coordination, tracking, follow-up, document organization, and closing-preparation work required to keep the transaction moving while you remain focused on your client, negotiations, and professional decisions.

## What you hand us

- the executed contract/file;
- known client and transaction contacts;
- brokerage-specific requirements we need to follow;
- any instructions or unusual circumstances we should know about.

## What Koinonia handles

### Transaction setup

- create and organize the transaction file;
- identify parties and key contacts;
- extract important contract dates and deadlines;
- build the transaction timeline/checklist;
- identify obvious missing documents or information;
- organize received documents.

### Deadline management

- track contractual deadlines and milestones;
- maintain the active checklist;
- send routine reminders and follow-up;
- surface approaching deadlines;
- escalate exceptions that require your decision.

### Title, lender and third-party coordination

Routine administrative coordination with approved transaction participants, including as applicable:

- title/closing company;
- lender;
- inspector;
- appraisal process contacts;
- HOA/document providers;
- insurance contacts at Realtor/client direction;
- approved repair/service providers.

### Document and signature follow-through

- track documents and signatures;
- follow up on missing items;
- organize executed documents;
- route approved documents/information;
- maintain clean file/version organization;
- support routine amendments/addenda/notices from Realtor-provided direction where permitted by the approved operating model.

### Inspection, appraisal and repair administration

- inspection scheduling coordination when requested;
- inspection-deadline tracking;
- appraisal-status follow-up;
- repair/vendor scheduling coordination;
- repair-documentation follow-up;
- surface issues that require Realtor judgment.

### Closing preparation

- coordinate closing scheduling information;
- maintain closing-preparation checklist;
- coordinate final-walkthrough scheduling;
- coordinate approved possession/key logistics;
- send closing reminders;
- complete and organize the transaction file after closing.

### Communication and visibility

- routine participant follow-up;
- Realtor status updates;
- milestone confirmations;
- approved client-update drafts/templates where appropriate;
- escalation when something needs your professional attention.

## What you get back

You get an organized transaction with the routine operational work actively managed instead of sitting in your inbox or memory.

You should know:

- what has been completed;
- what is coming next;
- what is waiting on someone else;
- what requires your decision;
- whether the file is on track for closing.

## What the price includes

The $450 fee covers the normal coordination volume for **one standard transaction**.

We do **not** charge separately for ordinary:

- reminder emails;
- lender follow-ups;
- title follow-ups;
- inspection scheduling messages;
- standard document touches;
- routine status updates.

## No-close rule

**No successful closing, no standard Transaction Management coordination fee.**

## What remains with you

You remain responsible for:

- client advice;
- negotiation;
- deciding contract terms;
- professional judgment;
- brokerage-required approvals;
- legal/compliance decisions reserved to you or your brokerage.

## What may be separate

Examples:

- licensed field attendance/showings;
- extraordinary dispute/crisis work beyond normal coordination;
- unusually complex or multi-property transactions;
- unrelated custom projects;
- outside vendor/service costs.

Any materially different scope is discussed before expanded work proceeds.

## Real-world example

### You get a buyer under contract Friday night.

You send Koinonia the executed file. We organize the transaction, enter and track the dates, coordinate routine title/lender/inspection activity, follow missing documents and signatures, keep the repair/appraisal/closing process visible, and surface the decisions that belong with you.

You remain the Realtor. You do not remain the project manager for every administrative detail in the file.

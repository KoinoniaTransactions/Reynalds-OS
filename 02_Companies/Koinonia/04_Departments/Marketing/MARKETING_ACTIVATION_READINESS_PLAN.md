# Reconciliation Decisions

## 1. Original dated 30-day calendar timing is superseded

The original file:

`30_DAY_MARKETING_LAUNCH_CALENDAR_2026-08-18.md`

was created before the user changed the operating sequence to:

**Build the whole system first, then implement.**

Therefore its fixed August 18–September 16 dates and automatic outbound-send instructions are no longer controlling.

The original file remains useful as the prepared content/copy/script library and historical launch draft.

It must not be interpreted as authorization to send, publish, contact, or spend on those original dates.

A fresh activation calendar should be dated when implementation is actually approved.

## 2. Action language in the Digital Marketing Execution Playbook is conditional

The Digital Marketing Execution Playbook describes what to do **after** a channel or relationship lane is activated.

Examples such as:

- send within 24 hours
- publish on cadence
- follow up on Day 4
- activate Meta

are operating instructions, not automatic authorization.

This Activation Readiness Plan governs whether those instructions are live.

## 3. Activation is modular

"Implement marketing" does not mean turning every channel on at once.

Each layer has its own gate.

Recommended order:

1. Foundation
2. Organic social
3. Inbound lead handling
4. Controlled relationship/outreach lanes
5. Meta paid
6. Permission-based email nurture
7. Brokerage account activation
8. TikTok paid after organic learning

Some adjacent layers may overlap once their gates are passed, but no layer should be activated simply because another layer is ready.

## 4. Outreach requires a separate live decision

Prospecting research and outreach are different states.

Koinonia may build/research a fresh prospect pool during implementation preparation without sending anything.

No cold/personal Realtor email, brokerage email, DM, call, promotional text, or nurture enrollment occurs until that specific lane is activated.

## 5. No promotional SMS at initial launch

Promotional SMS is not part of the initial activation plan.

Service/scheduling messages may be handled separately when appropriate to an existing relationship.

## 6. TikTok paid remains later

TikTok organic is part of initial organic implementation.

TikTok paid remains gated behind organic learning and sufficient business-intent signal.

Meta remains the first planned paid acquisition test.

# Stage 1 — Foundation Activation

Goal:

Make the conversion and identity infrastructure real before generating demand.

Tasks:

1. surgically release launch-critical website privacy/attribution changes to approved production
2. test live website conversion path
3. configure Facebook profile
4. configure Instagram profile
5. configure TikTok profile
6. verify organic profile UTMs
7. verify contact destinations
8. confirm lead owner/process
9. establish active dashboard/control view

Exit criteria:

- live contact form works
- live attribution works
- privacy link works
- social identities are correct
- inbound lead response ownership is clear

No outreach or paid spend is required to complete Stage 1.

---

# Stage 2 — Organic Social Activation

Goal:

Begin public category education and learn which pressure themes earn attention and business intent.

Initial cadence:

- Facebook: up to 3 primary posts/week
- Instagram: up to 3 feed/Reel posts/week
- Stories when useful
- TikTok: 3–5 videos/week during learning phase

Use the prepared S1–S10, T1–T12, and ST1–ST3 library as starting material.

Do not blindly publish every prepared asset.

Before each asset:

- confirm current business truth
- final QA
- confirm CTA/destination
- publish
- capture meaningful engagement

Exit/continuation criteria:

- content process is sustainable
- meaningful comments/DMs are handled
- source/content IDs are captured
- exact pressure language begins accumulating

# Implementation Hold Points

Stop and resolve before continuing if any of these occur:

- live privacy/contact path fails
- lead ownership unclear
- source attribution materially broken
- service/pricing truth is inconsistent
- social/profile identity wrong
- email suppression not functioning
- paid lead retrieval broken
- spend controls unclear
- generated demand exceeds Koinonia's service capacity

# Whole-System Measurement Standard

Measure in this order:

1. meaningful conversations
2. qualified consultations/direct service needs
3. first paid engagements
4. repeat/cross-service engagements
5. source-to-business value
6. cost per meaningful relationship/paid engagement
7. diagnostic reach/click/view metrics

Core operating health:

- every meaningful Relationship has source
- pressure/exact language preserved when useful
- one next action
- one next-action date

1. reconcile and release only the launch-critical website changes to production

Then review the Stage 1 results before beginning Stage 2 organic publishing.

This preserves the build-first philosophy and ensures Koinonia can safely receive demand before creating it.

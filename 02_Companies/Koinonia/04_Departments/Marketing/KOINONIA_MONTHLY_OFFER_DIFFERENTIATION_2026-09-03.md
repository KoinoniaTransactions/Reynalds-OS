# Koinonia Monthly Offer Differentiation — 2026-09-03

Status: Working Recommendation / Owner Review Required  
Owner: Koinonia  
Purpose: Create a clear commercial and operational distinction between the proposed $750/month Marketing Management product and the $1,250/month Koinonia Partnership so the products are easy to understand, profitable to fulfill, and aligned with Koinonia's white-glove positioning.

This document does not replace canonical pricing or service objects until approved.

---

# 1. Core Decision

Keep both proposed monthly prices for launch testing:

- **Marketing Management — $750/month**
- **Koinonia Partnership — $1,250/month**

Do not raise the Partnership merely to make it feel premium.

The market currently supports these prices if the product distinction is clear and capacity is controlled.

The distinction should not be:

> "more marketing for more money."

It should be:

> **Marketing Management owns what the market sees.**

> **Koinonia Partnership helps manage both what the market sees and the recurring business operations behind it.**

That is the fundamental buying distinction.

---

# 2. Marketing Management — $750/month

## Simple customer definition

> **Koinonia manages your recurring real-estate marketing so your brand, listings, database, and online presence stay active without you having to plan and produce it all yourself.**

## What problem it solves

The Realtor does not need a general assistant or operational partner. They primarily need someone to take ongoing marketing execution off their plate.

The Realtor should be able to say:

> "I want Koinonia to handle my marketing."

and understand exactly what they are buying.

## Primary ownership areas

### Marketing planning

- monthly priorities;
- content/calendar planning;
- campaign recommendations;
- brand consistency;
- approval workflow.

### Social/content execution

- approximately 8–12 core content pieces per month as the working production target;
- copy/captions;
- branded static/carousel assets;
- templated/faceless short-form content where appropriate;
- scheduling/publishing to agreed channels;
- listing/open-house/under-contract/sold content integration from available Koinonia data.

### Email/database marketing

- one recurring newsletter/email rhythm;
- approved sphere/past-client communication campaigns;
- review/referral campaign support within agreed cadence;
- basic database segmentation for marketing purposes.

### Online presence

- routine profile/content updates;
- Google Business Profile content/support where eligible and authorized;
- review-generation workflow support.

### Reporting

- concise monthly performance summary;
- next-action marketing recommendations.

## What Marketing Management does NOT own

Marketing Management does not become a general virtual assistant package.

By default it does not include ongoing ownership of:

- CRM pipeline administration unrelated to marketing campaigns;
- daily lead follow-up tracking;
- appointment/calendar management;
- inbox administration;
- transaction administration;
- listing operations beyond standard marketing overlap;
- general vendor/business coordination;
- business SOP maintenance;
- recurring executive/admin requests;
- field assignments;
- unlimited custom projects.

Those needs point toward Koinonia Partnership or another Koinonia product.

## Internal capacity target

Do not publish hours as the product.

For unit-economics management, design Marketing Management around approximately:

> **7–9 hours of efficient direct production/management capacity per normal month**, depending on role cost, automation, active listings, approvals, and content mix.

Target direct monthly delivery cost remains approximately $300–$340 or less.

This is a management guardrail, not a client-facing hourly bucket.

# 3. Koinonia Partnership — $1,250/month

## Simple customer definition

> **Koinonia becomes your recurring real-estate operations and marketing partner — helping keep the work behind your clients, database, marketing, and day-to-day business moving.**

## What problem it solves

The Realtor does not merely need content.

They are losing time because they personally remain the hub for:

- CRM;
- follow-up;
- marketing;
- tasks;
- reminders;
- recurring business administration;
- vendor coordination;
- database nurture;
- systems/processes;
- overflow planning.

The Realtor should be able to say:

> "I need someone helping me run the business behind my real-estate work."

That is the Partnership customer.

---

# 4. Partnership Includes the Marketing Foundation

The $1,250 Partnership should include the core Marketing Management relationship rather than forcing a Realtor to buy $750 + $1,250.

The Partnership is therefore an **upgrade**, not a second monthly subscription layered on top.

Included marketing foundation:

- monthly marketing priorities/calendar;
- recurring social/content production at the standard managed cadence;
- copy/design/scheduling/publishing;
- recurring email/newsletter rhythm;
- database/review/referral marketing support;
- listing/event lifecycle content integration;
- routine online-presence support;
- concise monthly reporting.

Do not double-charge Partnership clients for the standard $750 marketing scope.

---

The additional $500/month is not primarily "more posts."

It buys an **ongoing managed operations layer** around the Realtor's business.

## Core operations ownership

### CRM / pipeline

- routine CRM hygiene;
- contact organization;
- tag/stage maintenance;
- next-action tracking;
- stalled-contact identification;
- pipeline organization;
- basic source/status reporting.

### Follow-up administration

Within an approved communication process:

- follow-up task creation/tracking;
- reminder workflows;
- draft/template support;
- past-client/referral touchpoint administration;
- lead/client next-step visibility;
- surface items needing Realtor action.

This is operational support, not unapproved licensed advice or negotiation.

### Calendar / tasks / recurring administration

- recurring task organization;
- appointment/admin coordination where agreed;
- deadline/reminder administration outside active TC files when appropriate;
- recurring business calendar items;
- basic communication drafting;
- routine business follow-through.

### Vendor / service coordination

- recurring vendor contact/coordination;
- service scheduling support;
- tracking requested work;
- approved invoice/expense organization for handoff;
- maintain useful vendor information/processes.

### Systems / workflows

- maintain recurring checklists;
- SOP/process upkeep;
- template maintenance;
- workflow improvement recommendations;
- basic automation/process administration using approved systems.

### Business visibility

- concise recurring operations brief;
- open loops / items needing Realtor action;
- periodic review of priorities and capacity.

# 6. Partnership Capacity Guardrail

The Partnership cannot mean unlimited operational labor.

Do not sell hours on the website, but internally manage the $1,250 product around a normal total delivery envelope.

Working target:

- Marketing Management component: approximately 7–9 efficient hours equivalent;
- Additional operations component: approximately 6–8 efficient hours equivalent;
- Normal total managed delivery capacity: approximately **13–17 hours equivalent per month**.

The exact client may vary because some activities are more automated/efficient than others.

This is not a promised hourly bank and should not be marketed as such.

Target total direct monthly delivery cost:

> **Approximately $500–$575 or less.**

This provides room for white-glove coordination, management overhead, quality control, and sustainable service.

If a client consistently requires materially more recurring workload, the correct response is a scope/capacity review — not dozens of tiny add-on invoices.

---

# 7. What Partnership Does NOT Include by Default

The $1,250 monthly fee does not create unlimited access to every Koinonia product.

Unless a future package explicitly says otherwise, these remain separately priced:

- **Transaction Management — $450 per successful closing**;
- **Hand Us the Listing — $350 per listing**;
- **Licensed Field Coverage — standard assignment pricing**;
- **Professional Open House — standard event pricing**;
- large custom marketing/business projects outside recurring scope;
- outside media, advertising, printing, postage, staging, repairs, and specialist expenses.

This is essential to protect unit economics.

---

# 8. Partnership Client Experience Should Still Feel Integrated

Separately priced does not mean separately managed.

For a Partnership client:

- Koinonia already knows the Realtor's business;
- systems/access are already established;
- brand/communication context is already known;
- listing information can flow into marketing;
- accepted listings can flow into transactions;
- field needs can be coordinated inside the relationship;
- follow-up can flow back into CRM/business operations.

The Realtor experiences **one Koinonia relationship**, even when high-variable services have their own simple usage price.

That is a core white-glove differentiator.

---

# 9. Do Not Discount the Core Products Yet

At launch, do not automatically create a second hidden price matrix for Partnership clients.

Avoid immediately introducing:

- $400 partner TC;
- $300 partner listing;
- $65 partner showing;
- separate loyalty tiers;
- complex monthly credits.

Those ideas can be evaluated after real usage data exists.

For launch, Partnership value should come from:

- continuity;
- priority relationship;
- proactive operations ownership;
- integrated context;
- reduced briefing/rebriefing;
- marketing included;
- one place to bring recurring operational needs.

This protects pricing simplicity.

# 12. Upgrade Trigger

Marketing Management should naturally lead to Partnership when the Realtor begins asking Koinonia to handle recurring work such as:

- "Can you keep my CRM cleaned up too?"
- "Can you make sure these people get followed up with?"
- "Can you keep my task list/calendar moving?"
- "Can you manage this vendor/process every month?"
- "Can I just send you this stuff instead of figuring out who it belongs to?"

At that point the issue is no longer marketing production; it is recurring operational ownership.

That is the Partnership upgrade.

---

# 13. Competitive Position

The proposed launch prices are intentionally competitive for September 2026.

Current public examples show:

- real-estate-focused managed social packages around the high-$600s/month and above;
- more substantial managed marketing packages approaching or exceeding $1,000/month;
- real-estate administrative VA support commonly reaching roughly $850/month part-time and $1,500+/month full-time, often with setup fees or minimum commitments.

Koinonia at $1,250/month is therefore not positioned as a cheap assistant.

It is positioned as an integrated, fractional real-estate operations relationship that also includes meaningful managed marketing.

The advantage should be sold as integration and accountability, not raw labor hours.

---

# 14. Recommended Owner Decision

Keep:

- **Marketing Management — $750/month**
- **Koinonia Partnership — $1,250/month**

Define them publicly as:

> **Marketing Management = Koinonia manages your marketing.**

> **Koinonia Partnership = Koinonia manages your marketing plus an agreed recurring layer of CRM, follow-up, workflow, and business operations.**

Do not add another monthly tier at launch.

Do not offer unlimited support.

Do not publish hours as the primary customer value.

Do not create partner discounts/credits until actual client usage provides enough data to determine whether they improve retention without harming margin.

This preserves the simple pricing architecture while making the $500 step-up commercially meaningful and operationally defensible.

Status: Working Recommendation / Owner Review Required

This is a content/sales architecture recommendation. It does not itself publish claims or replace canonical objects until approved.

---

# 1. Core Website Principle

The website must answer four questions quickly:

1. **What is Koinonia?**
2. **What can I hand off?**
3. **How do I hire Koinonia?**
4. **What does it cost?**

The site should not make the Realtor understand Koinonia's internal departments, staffing model, vendor model, database objects, or fulfillment architecture.

The experience should feel like:

> **Bring Koinonia the need. We help get it handled.**

The brand position remains:

> **You focus on your clients. We'll keep the business running.**

Supporting message:

> **One relationship. More of your business covered.**

---

# 2. Resolve the Umbrella vs Product Problem

Koinonia has five capability umbrellas and five primary commercial products, but the website must not make this feel like ten unrelated services.

Use these layers intentionally:

## Layer A — What Koinonia can handle

This is the **capability discovery layer**:

1. Transactions & Contracts
2. Listing & Seller Support
3. Licensed Field Coverage
4. Marketing & Growth
5. CRM & Business Operations

These answer:

> **What kinds of work can I bring to Koinonia?**

## Layer B — How you hire Koinonia

This is the **commercial buying layer**:

1. Transaction Management — $450 / successful closing
2. Hand Us the Listing — $350 / listing
3. Licensed Field Coverage — from $75 / assignment
4. Marketing Management — $750 / month
5. Koinonia Partnership — $1,250 / month
6. Custom Project — quoted before work begins

These answer:

> **What am I actually buying and what does it cost?**

The website should explicitly explain this distinction once so the breadth feels simple rather than duplicative.

This should be brief and visible near pricing, not buried in legal text.

**Your Koinonia fee covers the normal coordination and execution included in the service you choose. Some properties or campaigns require outside expenses such as professional photography, drone/video, staging, printing, postage, advertising spend, repairs, or other specialty services.**

> "Everything is included."

# 20. Conversion Principle

The site should not ask the Realtor to make a complicated purchasing decision before talking with Koinonia.

The purpose of the page is to create three beliefs:

1. **Koinonia can probably help with more than I expected.**
2. **The pricing is understandable and competitive enough to try.**
3. **I can bring them the need without figuring out the entire solution myself.**

If those beliefs are created, the website has done its job.

## 2. Tenant placement guarantee

Local competitors set the bar at 12 months: Grace Castle Rock re-leases for free if a tenant it placed breaks the lease in the first 12 months ([Grace Property Management](https://www.rentgrace.com/Castle-Rock-property-management)), and Bergan advertises a "12-month lease guarantee with free re-leasing" ([Bergan & Co](https://www.berganco.com/areas-we-serve/parker-colorado)). A national example is narrower: a six-month guarantee that applies only if the placed tenant is evicted (research file `source_data/`).

Proposed Koinonia terms (to match the local market):

- If a tenant Koinonia placed **breaks the lease or is evicted within the first 12 months** of the lease, Koinonia finds a new tenant with **no placement fee**.
- One free re-placement per original placement, while the property stays under Koinonia management.
- Does not apply when the departure is caused by the owner (selling, ending management, refusing needed repairs, overriding Koinonia's screening recommendation) or when the tenant uses a lawful termination right (for example, military or domestic-violence lease termination rights).
- Normal costs still apply: turnover repairs, cleaning, legal/eviction costs and any advertising pass-throughs defined in the agreement.

Cost check: the guarantee gives up one placement fee per triggered case — about $1,400 at $2,800 rent. If 1 in 10 placements triggers it, the expected cost is about $140 per placement. Track the trigger rate from day one.

Website wording (draft): "12-month tenant guarantee: if a tenant we place breaks the lease or is evicted in the first year, we find a new tenant with no placement fee. Conditions apply — see the management agreement."

## 3. Contractor markup

Confirmed: 10% on Koinonia-coordinated contractor work under both the 10% and 8% plans, itemized as a separate line; no markup on work the owner arranges and pays directly. This sits at the low end of the Denver 10–15% range ([RentPro](https://renpro.com/property-management-cost-denver-co/)). The website should disclose it on `/pricing` and in the "what's included" table.

## 4. Owner payout date

Decision: owners are paid on one fixed day each month. **Proposed: the 10th** (or the next business day), covering rent that has cleared by then. A fixed date answers a common owner question and is easy to state on the site. Confirm the day and the cut-off rule with the trust-accounting setup.

## 5. Referral rewards — credits only

Decision: referral rewards are credits, not cash. Proposed structure:

- **Owner refers an owner** who signs a management agreement: a credit toward the referrer's management fees (amount to be set — e.g., one month's management fee).
- **Koinonia Transactions / sales client becomes a management client:** a credit on their first placement or first months of management (amount to be set).
- **Real estate agents:** any referral compensation goes broker-to-broker only, as the managing broker approves.

Compliance note: a fee credit is still something of value. Confirm with the managing broker/attorney that credits to unlicensed referrers are permitted under Colorado DRE rules before advertising the program (see file 05).

## 7. Response standard

Decision, public wording (draft): "During business hours, we respond with next steps within one hour. We start working on the issue the same or next business day. Emergencies are handled right away through our emergency line."

- This beats what owners expect: 43% expect a same-day response and 42% next business day ([Buildium](https://www.buildium.com/blog/how-rental-owners-evaluate-a-property-managers-performance/)).
- It is also a speed-to-lead advantage if applied to new owner inquiries (file 04 §3).
- Still needed: published business hours (proposed Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–5 p.m. Mountain), an after-hours emergency process, and a check that "next business day" never delays Colorado's warranty-of-habitability deadlines, which can be shorter for health and safety conditions (the 2026-11-04 pricing memo already says emergencies and statutory responses can't wait on preferences).
- Track the 1-hour reply rate as a published metric once there is enough data (file 03 §4).

## 1. National leaders — what each does best

### Evernest — "one source" for management, brokerage and rehabs
- Hero: "Nationally Known, Locally Grown Full-Service Property Management"; three entry paths directly under the hero: **Search Rentals / Rent Your Property / Buy or Sell a Property** ([Evernest](https://www.evernest.co/)).
- Three named guarantees with conditions: Maintenance Guarantee (first year), Resident Eviction Guarantee (replace resident and waive leasing fee if evicted in first 12 months), "100% Happiness Guarantee" — cancel anytime without penalty ([Evernest](https://www.evernest.co/)).
- Proof: "Over 15,000 5-Star Reviews"; "12,000 Rentals managed nationwide… 18,000 Reviews with an average 4.4★" ([Evernest residential page](https://www.evernest.co/residential-property-management)).
- Process transparency: publishes the $500 owner maintenance reserve, the auto-dispatch threshold (below $500 dispatch, above $500 call the owner), and a 21-day leasing goal ([Evernest process](https://www.evernest.co/our-process)).
- Content that bridges sales and management: blog posts like "House Won't Sell? When Renting Beats a Price Cut" and "renting out a home after a work move" ([Evernest](https://www.evernest.co/)).
- **Copy for Koinonia:** three-path hero; publish the approval threshold ($250 is already approved) and owner response expectations; rent-vs-sell content.

### Mynd — investor-grade transparency
- Hero: "Less hassle. Better results." / Colorado page: "Stress less. Earn more." ([Mynd](https://www.mynd.co/); [Mynd Colorado](https://www.mynd.co/locations/colorado)).
- Named, capped guarantees: "Rental Income Guarantee… cover up to one-month rent" and "$3,000 Eviction Protection Plan" ([Mynd Colorado](https://www.mynd.co/locations/colorado)).
- Portal described by tasks, not buzzwords: approve or deny maintenance requests, real-time cash flow and YTD earnings, video inspections ([Mynd property management](https://www.mynd.co/property-management)).
- State page carries an 8-question FAQ (what a Colorado PM does, screening, leasing, updates, pricing, additional fees, STRs) ([Mynd Colorado](https://www.mynd.co/locations/colorado)).
- **Copy for Koinonia:** describe the owner experience as concrete tasks the owner can do; state-specific FAQ block.

### Belong — radical fee honesty
- A full blog post titled "Belong's fees, explained honestly: what homeowners actually pay," with an apples-to-apples annual cost comparison and the line "traditional property managers advertise a lower-looking monthly rate and recover the difference through line items" ([Belong fees article](https://belonghome.com/blog/belongs-fees-explained-honestly-what-homeowners-actually-pay)).
- Homeowner page includes a side-by-side table of fees and "FREE" inclusions (setup, inspection, pricing analysis, photos, 3D tours, tax docs; no early termination, vacancy or admin fees) ([Belong homeowners](https://belonghome.com/homeowners)).
- Referral program with a specific payout: "Refer a great homeowner and get $1,000 when their lease starts" ([Belong](https://belonghome.com/)).
- **Copy for Koinonia:** an "all-in first-year cost" worked example on `/pricing`; a "what's included at no charge" table (Koinonia already approved $0 setup, $0 vacancy, included initial assessment, move-in and move-out inspections).

### Doorstead — owner role made explicit, rent estimate first
- Hero: "Your rental is an asset. Stop working it like a job." Subhead: "Your only job is to say 'Yes' to the tenant." Primary CTA: **"See my rent estimate"** ([Doorstead](https://www.doorstead.com/)).
- Rent estimate page: "Free rent estimate in 2 minutes… No commitment required," three-step explanation, sample report with comparables ([Doorstead rent estimate](https://www.doorstead.com/rent-estimate)).
- Plan comparison table with management %, placement %, platform fee, termination fee side by side ([Doorstead compare plans](https://www.doorstead.com/compare-plans)).
- Proof stats: "480+ 5-Star Google Reviews," "49% faster leasing," "1,500+ Properties managed" ([Doorstead](https://www.doorstead.com/)).
- **Copy for Koinonia:** state the owner's role in each plan (Managed for You vs Managed With You); a two-column comparison table on `/pricing`.

### Bay Property Management Group — the SEO city-page machine
- Every market has its own page with **itemized local pricing**, e.g., Baltimore: "Management fee 5-8%; Leasing fee 1st month's rent; Renewal fee 25%; $99 Start up Fee" ([BMG Baltimore](https://www.baymgmtgroup.com/property-management-in-baltimore/)).
- Operating metrics on the homepage: 8,000+ units, "<1% of tenants placed by BMG require eviction," 23-day average time on market, 79% renewal rate, response "within a maximum of 24 hours" ([BMG](https://www.baymgmtgroup.com/)).
- "Is hiring a property management company worth it?" — a BMG-vs-self-managing comparison table ([BMG](https://www.baymgmtgroup.com/)).
- Local FAQ that mirrors search queries: "How Much Do Baltimore Property Managers Charge?", "Should I Hire a Property Management Company or Self-manage My Baltimore Rental?" ([BMG Baltimore](https://www.baymgmtgroup.com/property-management-in-baltimore/)).
- **Copy for Koinonia:** city pages with local pricing, local FAQs, local market data; a "Koinonia vs. self-managing" table.

### Real Property Management (Neighborly) and PMI — franchise playbooks
- RPM: "We do more than manage properties. We deliver peace of mind." CTA: "Get an assessment from your local… office." Lead tool: "Wealth Optimizer" portfolio analysis ([RPM](https://www.realpropertymgt.com/)).
- PMI: "Managing a property shouldn't feel like a house and chain"; CTA "Get a free, no-obligation rental analysis"; process named in four stages (Ready, Select, Protect, Renew); owner payout commitment "by the tenth working day of each month" ([PMI](https://www.propertymanagementinc.com/); [PMI residential](https://www.propertymanagementinc.com/residential-property-management)).
- **Copy for Koinonia:** a named, memorable process; a specific owner-payout date commitment (requires owner approval and trust-accounting confirmation).

### Keyrenter, Green Residential, TurboTenant, PURE HomeRiver
- Keyrenter: outcome trio "Get Your Time Back / Build Your Portfolio / Design The Life You Want"; metrics "0.2% annual eviction rate," "96% of residents paying rent on time"; "We promise never to leave you in the dark" ([Keyrenter](https://www.keyrenter.com/)).

## 4. Things the leaders do that Koinonia should **not** copy

- Unverifiable superlatives ("#1 Property Management Company," "the best") — conflicts with Koinonia's voice rules and invites DRE advertising scrutiny.
- JavaScript-animated counters: Evernest's counters read as "0+" to a non-JavaScript reader (how search and AI crawlers often see a page). Render any metric server-side, and never show a metric before real data exists.
- Hiding all pricing behind "select your market" (Evernest, Mynd) — works for national brands, not for a single-market company competing against Bergan's published 10%/50%.
- Tenant-paid recurring "admin fees" used to keep owner fees low (Grace charges $25/month) — Colorado HB25-1090 (effective 2026-01-01) restricts the fees landlords may require and requires advertised rental prices to disclose the total price, so any such fee needs attorney review; it also cuts against Koinonia's transparency positioning (see file 05).

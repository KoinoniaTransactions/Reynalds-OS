## 1. How owners search (national demand)

US monthly volumes from Google Keyword Planner as published by [BrandRocket](https://www.brandrocket.net/resources/property-management-marketing-google-ads-owner-searches) and [Your Firm Growth](https://yourfirmgrowth.com/property-management-keywords/). Use them for **relative** priority only; Douglas County volumes must be pulled from Keyword Planner before budgeting.

| Buyer stage | Example queries (US monthly searches) | Where Koinonia wins it |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware — "I have a house to rent" | how to rent out a house (4,400); renting out my house (480); **should i rent or sell my house (390)** | Rent-vs-sell guide + calculator; accidental-landlord guide |
| Problem aware | how to evict a tenant (720); tenant not paying rent (260); how to screen tenants (170) | Colorado landlord-law articles (for-cause eviction, deposits, screening) |
| Solution aware | how much does property management cost (2,900); property management fees (2,900); what does a property manager do (2,900); is a property manager worth it (140) | `/pricing` + fee guide; "Koinonia vs self-managing" |
| Service aware | property management company (49,500); property management near me (40,500); property management companies near me (18,100); rental property management (12,100) | **Google Business Profile / Map Pack** + city pages |
| Most aware | "koinonia properties reviews / fees" | Review volume, `/pricing`, `/standards` |

Key findings:
- The four service-aware terms total about **120,000 US searches/month**; early-stage terms about one-eighth of that ([BrandRocket](https://www.brandrocket.net/resources/property-management-marketing-google-ads-owner-searches)).
- "Near me" terms are served by the Map Pack, not website rankings; **"[city] property management" terms are the highest-converting owner tier**; pricing/fee terms "convert extremely well" because searchers are close to hiring ([Your Firm Growth](https://yourfirmgrowth.com/property-management-keywords/)).
- Some "property management company"/"near me" searches are tenants looking for their landlord's portal — owner ads and pages need owner-specific language to filter them ([BrandRocket](https://www.brandrocket.net/resources/property-management-marketing-google-ads-owner-searches)).
- Map each search intent to **exactly one page**, put the target query in the title tag and H1, and keep owner and renter pages separate ([QCK](https://qck.co/blog/seo-for-property-management)).
- Build city pages only where there is real local detail — otherwise they risk being treated as doorway pages ([QCK](https://qck.co/blog/seo-for-property-management)).

## 4. City page template (built only for real markets)

Each page must contain information a reader cannot get from the other city pages ([QCK](https://qck.co/blog/seo-for-property-management)). The strongest local example is Bergan's Parker page ([Bergan & Co](https://www.berganco.com/areas-we-serve/parker-colorado)).

1. H1 "[City] Property Management" + one-sentence local promise + **rental analysis form inline** (address field above the fold).
2. Local rent snapshot with source and date. Example data available today: Castle Rock average rent $3,045 ([Zillow, Sept 2026](https://www.zillow.com/rental-manager/market-trends/castle-rock-co/)); median $2,543, down 4.8% YoY ([Zumper, Nov 2026](https://www.zumper.com/rent-research/castle-rock-co)); Douglas County Rentometer averages 3BR $3,007 / 4BR+ $3,809 ([Rentometer, Sept 2026](https://www.rentometer.com/average-rent-in/county/douglas-county-co)). Prefer RentCast data the site already licenses, with attribution.
3. Neighborhood profiles written from real knowledge (Bergan's Parker page profiles The Pinery, Cottonwood, Parker Meadows, Sterling Ranch and Historic Downtown). Castle Rock candidates: The Meadows, Crystal Valley Ranch, Castlewood Ranch, Terrain, Plum Creek, Founders Village, Downtown.
4. Local rules: Parker requires no rental license but may require a business/tax license ([TurboTenant Parker FAQ citing Parker Mun. Code § 4.03.050](https://www.turbotenant.com/property-management/services/colorado/parker/)); Castle Rock has no long-term rental license program ([TurboTenant Castle Rock](https://www.turbotenant.com/tenant-placement/colorado/castle-rock/)); Douglas County requires a license for short-term rentals in unincorporated areas ([Douglas County](https://www.douglasco.gov/planning/development-review-regulations/short-term-rentals/)). HOA rental restrictions are common — verify each before publishing.
5. Pricing summary (same numbers as `/pricing`) and the two plans.
6. Local FAQ (5–8 questions) with FAQPage schema: cost in [city]; rental license; how long to rent; HOA rules; seasonal maintenance (hail, freeze-thaw, irrigation winterization); typical tenant profile; rent vs sell.
7. Local reviews (only real ones) and the local person responsible.
8. Current listings in that city (when there are any).
9. Closing CTA: rental analysis + call/text.

## 5. Technical SEO and AI-search checklist

| Item | Evidence | Current state |
|---|---|---|
| `RealEstateAgent` / `LocalBusiness` schema with `areaServed` (cities), `priceRange`, phone, hours; service-area business with hidden address | 58.6% of audited PM sites use LocalBusiness schema ([Clienvora](https://www.clienvora.com/blog/property-management-seo/)) | `Organization` + `Service` JSON-LD exist without `areaServed` |

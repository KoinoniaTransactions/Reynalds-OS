| Visitor → lead | 2.0% → 8 leads | 3.0% → 24 leads |

| **New doors / month** | **≈0.4** | **≈2.4** |

Implications:
- At benchmark rates, organic web traffic alone is slow; **referrals, realtor partners and Koinonia's own sales clients must carry year one** (section 4).
- The biggest lever is lead → consultation. Benchmark is 15%; speed to lead (section 3) and a booking link raise it.

### Unit economics per door (approved pricing)

At $2,800 rent: Managed for You yields $280/month ($3,360/year) plus $1,400 per placement; Managed With You yields $224/month ($2,688/year) plus $1,400 per placement. With average tenure of 4.2 years ([CUFinder](https://cufinder.io/blog/benchmarks/property-management/)), one 10% door is worth roughly $14,000+ in management fees before placement, renewal and inspection fees — which justifies a customer acquisition cost of several hundred dollars per signed door. Contractor markup income is excluded until volumes are known.

## 3. Speed to lead and follow-up

- Responding within 5 minutes instead of 30 makes a lead about 21× more likely to qualify; responding within an hour makes a business about 7× more likely to qualify the lead; the average company response time was 42 hours and 23% never responded ([LetHub, summarizing MIT/InsideSales, HBR 2011 and Drift](https://www.lethub.co/blog/rental-lead-follow-up-automation)).
- Recommended Koinonia standard (owner approval needed): owner leads get a text + email within 5 minutes (automated) and a human call within 1 business hour during business hours.
- Follow-up cadence: immediate → day 2 → day 7 → day 14 → monthly market update ([ClearLead Digital](https://www.clearleaddigital.com/blog/increase-property-management-leads)).

### Lead form changes

| Field | Today | Recommended | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name, email | Required | Required | — |
| Phone | Optional | **Required** (with text consent checkbox) | Enables 5-minute text/call |
| Property address | Yes | Yes (first field, triggers estimate) | Lowest-friction first step |
| Units | No | **Add** (1 / 2–4 / 5+) | 0 of 59 audited forms ask unit count ([Clienvora](https://www.clienvora.com/blog/property-management-seo/)); it qualifies and routes |
| Plan interest | No | Add (For you / With you / Not sure) | Matches the two-plan offer |
| Timeline | No | Add (Now / 1–3 months / Exploring) | Prioritizes follow-up |
| Situation | No | Add (Moving / Investor / Inherited / Switching managers / Rent or sell?) | Routes to the right script |

Keep the first step to about three fields — CUFinder recommends a maximum of three form fields ([CUFinder](https://cufinder.io/blog/benchmarks/property-management/)) — then ask the qualifiers on step two after the estimate appears.

## 4. Channel strategy (sequenced)

DoorGrow's advice for new PM companies: build a referral system, a review engine and a trust-building website first, add realtor partnerships, and only then spend on paid ads ([DoorGrow](https://doorgrow.com/property-management-marketing-strategies/)). Referrals are the top tactic for 30% of property managers ([Nolan, citing Buildium/NARPM](https://getnolan.ai/resources/property-management-leads/)) and account for about 15% of new business on average ([CUFinder](https://cufinder.io/blog/benchmarks/property-management/)).

| Phase | Channel | Actions | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 — Pre-launch | Licensing + entity | Confirm brokerage of record and DRE requirements (file 05) | Blocks all marketing |
| 1 — Launch (months 0–3) | Website + GBP | City pages, pricing, rental analysis, schema; GBP verified as service-area business | Owner copy approval |
| 1 | **Internal cross-sell** | Every Koinonia sales client who might rent: offer Rent-or-Sell analysis; every buyer of an investment property: offer management | Unique advantage of the dual-line model |
| 1 | Review engine | Ask every client at defined moments | — |
| 2 — Months 3–6 | Realtor partners | Referral partnership for agents whose clients want to rent instead of sell; promise to return the client for the eventual sale | Referral compensation only as DRE rules allow |
| 2 | Content | Rent-or-sell guide, Colorado law updates, quarterly rent report | — |
| 3 — Months 6+ | Google Ads | Exact/phrase "[city] property management," pricing and rent-or-sell terms; owner-only ad language; manual CPC until about 30 leads/month ([BrandRocket](https://www.brandrocket.net/resources/property-management-lead-generation-google-ads-manual-bidding)); start around $1,000/month | Tracking and speed-to-lead working |
| 3 | Meta retargeting | Retarget rental-analysis visitors only; Fair Housing special ad category rules apply | HUD digital advertising guidance (file 05) |

Avoid early: purchased lead networks (low conversion, ~10%), broad "near me" ads (tenant clicks).

## 5. Consultation script outline

1. Their situation (moving, investor, inherited, switching, undecided).
2. The property: rent estimate, condition, HOA rules.
3. Rent vs sell: honest numbers from both sides of Koinonia.
4. Plan fit: For You vs With You.
5. Walk through the fee table and first-year example.
6. What happens in the first 30 days.
7. Ask for the agreement; confirm next step and date.

## 6. KPIs for the dashboard

Visitors by page and source · rental analyses started/completed · lead → consultation % · first-response time · consultation → signed % · doors under management · days on market · owner churn · reviews count and rating · cost per lead and per signed door by channel.

# 05 — Compliance Guardrails for Website Copy

Research date: 2026-11-17 · Status: research summary, **not legal advice** — confirm with a Colorado real estate attorney and the managing broker before launch

## 1. Licensing gate (blocks launch)

- Colorado requires a real estate license to manage property for others for compensation (C.R.S. 12-10-201(6)); see the [Colorado Division of Real Estate](https://dre.colorado.gov/working-licensed-real-estate-professionals) and the [2025 Colorado Real Estate Manual, Chapter 3](https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/2025%20Colorado%20Real%20Estate%20Manual-Chapter%2003.pdf).
- The 2026-11-03 positioning memo records Koinonia Properties LLC as **proposed, not formed or licensed**. Until that changes, the site must not claim to be a licensed brokerage, must not take management agreements, and must name the actual brokerage of record wherever services are offered.
- Copy rules: show brokerage name and license status as the DRE requires; no "#1/best" claims; any metric must be real, dated and defined.

## 2. Tenant-facing price and fee rules

| Rule | What it means for the site | Source |
|---|---|---|
| HB25-1090 (effective 2026-01-01) — total price disclosure | Any advertised price (rent listings, fees) must clearly disclose the maximum total the person may pay; required tenant fees belong in the advertised total (utilities excepted). The act also bars certain landlord fees — get counsel's list before adding any tenant fee | [Colorado General Assembly](http://leg.colorado.gov/bills/hb25-1090) |
| Application fee limited to actual cost | The $35 application fee must reflect actual screening cost; publish what it covers | [C.R.S. 38-12-903](https://law.justia.com/codes/colorado/2023/title-38/tenants-and-landlords/article-12/part-9/section-38-12-903/) |
| HB23-1099 — portable tenant screening reports | `/apply` must explain acceptance of portable screening reports and related limits | [Colorado General Assembly](https://leg.colorado.gov/bills/hb23-1099) |
| HB25-1249 — security deposits | Deposit language must follow the new normal-wear-and-tear and move-out walk-through rules | [Colorado DRE summary](https://dre.colorado.gov/hb25-1249-summary) |
| HB24-1098 — for-cause eviction | Don't promise owners "easy" non-renewals or evictions; describe the legal process accurately | [Colorado General Assembly](https://leg.colorado.gov/bills/hb24-1098) |

## 3. Fair Housing

- Follow HUD's guidance on advertising through digital platforms: no targeting or exclusion by protected class, use the housing special ad category on Meta, and review audience tools ([HUD FHEO guidance](https://archives.hud.gov/news/2024/FHEO_Guidance_on_Advertising_through_Digital_Platforms.pdf)).
- Every page: Equal Housing Opportunity logo and statement.
- City and neighborhood pages: describe amenities, commute, schools by name only (no "great for families," "safe area," "ideal for young professionals" or similar steering language).
- Screening criteria: written, applied the same to everyone, and published on `/apply`.

## 4. Marketing claims checklist

| Claim type | Allowed when |
|---|---|
| "Licensed" | The entity and brokerage of record are actually licensed |
| Metrics (days on market, response time, renewal rate) | Based on Koinonia's own data, dated, with definitions |
| Guarantees | Written into the management agreement first |
| Reviews/testimonials | Real, unedited in meaning, with consent |
| Competitor comparisons | Factual, sourced and dated; no disparagement |
| Rent estimates | Labeled as estimates with data source (RentCast) and date |
| Referral rewards | Approved by the managing broker under DRE rules |
| SMS follow-up | Express consent captured on the form |

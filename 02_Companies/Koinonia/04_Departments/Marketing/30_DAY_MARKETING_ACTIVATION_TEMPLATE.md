# Koinonia Transactions — 30-Day Marketing Activation Template

Status: Pre-Activation Template  
Business: Koinonia Transactions  
Owner: Marketing + Sales

## Purpose

Replace fixed historical launch dates with a relative 30-day implementation sequence that begins only after Stage 1 Foundation Activation is explicitly approved.

This template does not authorize activation by itself.

The historical `30_DAY_MARKETING_LAUNCH_CALENDAR_2026-08-18.md` remains a prepared content/copy library but is no longer the controlling activation schedule.

## Activation Rules

- Day 1 is the first approved implementation day, not a fixed calendar date.
- No email, DM, call, brokerage outreach, nurture enrollment, paid ad, or social publication occurs before its applicable gate is approved.
- Organic publishing and inbound response may activate before outbound relationship development.
- Meta is the first planned paid channel.
- TikTok paid remains later and requires organic learning.
- Promotional SMS is not part of the initial activation plan.

---

# Stage 1 — Foundation Activation

## Day 1

- approve/release only launch-critical website privacy/attribution changes
- verify `/privacy`
- verify consultation/contact path
- run one test Relationship creation
- run one UTM attribution test
- verify existing first-touch preservation

## Day 2

- configure Facebook profile
- configure Instagram profile
- configure TikTok profile
- verify profile links/UTMs
- confirm Koinonia identity/branding

## Day 3

- confirm inbound lead owner
- confirm response standards
- confirm dashboard/control view
- verify social DMs/comments can be monitored

**Stage 1 hold point:** do not continue if conversion, privacy, attribution, or ownership is broken.

This is a build-and-review stage. It is not a live publishing stage.

- S1 More Opportunity, Not More Chaos — Facebook/Instagram static master

- T2 Two Clients Need Showings at the Same Time — TikTok/Reel recording package

Complete:

- platform-ready copy/script packages

**Stage 2A hold point:** no asset may move from `Approved — Not Scheduled` to `Scheduled` or
`Published` without explicit user approval.

# Week 2 — Establish Organic Rhythm

## Days 8–14

Target rhythm:

- Facebook: up to 3 primary posts/week
- Instagram: up to 3 feed/Reel posts/week
- Stories when useful
- TikTok: 3–5 videos/week

Use S2–S5 / T3–T6 as the likely next pool, subject to QA and current relevance.

Do not activate outbound automatically.

At Day 14 review:

- top pressure theme
- strongest business-intent content
- response handling quality
- whether Stage 4 outbound should even be considered
- whether Meta readiness work should begin

# Stage 4 — Controlled Outbound (Separate Approval)

This stage may begin during Week 2 or later only if separately approved.

Possible lanes:

- warm Realtor follow-up
- public professional one-to-one outreach
- brokerage leader outreach
- professional partner relationship development

Before any lane activates:

- fresh list/research
- legitimate contact path
- message reviewed
- suppression/do-not-contact checked
- limited batch size
- Relationship capture ready

No public prospect research is automatically contacted.

# Week 3 — Organic Learning + Optional Meta Activation

## Days 15–21

Continue organic publishing from approved library.

Likely content pool:

- S5–S8
- T7–T10
- relevant Story prompts

If Meta activation is explicitly approved and all gates pass:

- activate $20/day test
- avoid major edits for about 72 hours unless broken
- respond same business day
- track qualified conversations, not just CPL

If Meta is not approved or not ready:

- remain organic
- do not force a paid launch to preserve the calendar

---

# Week 4 — Review, Refine, Do Not Overreact

## Days 22–28

Continue best organic pressure themes.

Use remaining prepared content or remakes based on actual learning.

If Meta is active:

- perform 72-hour review when enough delivery exists
- classify Green / Yellow / Red
- no automatic budget increase

If outbound is active:

- replies take priority over new cold sends
- no endless follow-up
- no automatic nurture enrollment

If brokerage outreach is active:

- track account-level next actions
- do not email every agent by default

---

# Days 29–30 — Month 1 Review

Review:

1. which sources created meaningful Realtor relationships
2. which pressure categories repeated
3. which exact language repeated
4. which content generated buying-intent engagement
5. which first services were requested
6. which first services were purchased
7. whether Meta produced relationships worth paying for, if active
8. whether outbound produced useful conversations, if active
9. whether brokerage relationship work produced leader/agent opportunities, if active
10. which TikTok themes deserve another organic version

Choose no more than three major Month 2 changes.

Do not activate TikTok paid solely because 30 days have passed.

---

# TikTok Paid Gate — Outside Automatic 30-Day Sequence

TikTok paid activates only after:

- sufficient organic volume/learning
- repeatable Realtor business-intent theme
- selected Spark candidate
- Ads Manager/billing/permissions
- privacy/lead retrieval readiness
- separate user approval

Preferred progression:

1. Spark Ads
2. native Lead Generation
3. website lead generation after tracking
4. retargeting after enough audience volume

---

# Month 1 Success Hierarchy

Judge in this order:

1. meaningful conversations
2. qualified consultations/direct service needs
3. first paid engagements
4. repeat/cross-service signals
5. source-to-business value
6. cost per meaningful conversation/paid engagement when paid channels are active
7. diagnostic reach/click/view metrics

The calendar is a framework. It must never override readiness, relationship quality, compliance, service capacity, or explicit activation approval.

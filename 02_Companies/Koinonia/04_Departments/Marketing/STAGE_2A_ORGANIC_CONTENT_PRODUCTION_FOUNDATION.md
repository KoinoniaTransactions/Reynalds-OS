# Koinonia Transactions — Stage 2A Organic Content Production Foundation

Status: Active Build and Review
Business: Koinonia Transactions
Owner: Marketing + Sales
Effective Date: 2026-09-01

## Purpose

Create the smallest complete organic-content production package before Koinonia begins live
publishing.

Stage 2A exists because finished strategy, copy, scripts, profile setup, and analytics do not by
themselves constitute a production-ready campaign. The first pilot assets must be produced and
reviewed before implementation begins.

## Scope Authorized

Stage 2A authorizes only:

1. one reusable Koinonia social-static master
2. one S1 Facebook/Instagram static asset
3. one T1 TikTok/Reel faceless-animation package
4. one T2 TikTok/Reel faceless-animation package
5. content records, QA, dashboard baseline, and approval preparation

## Explicitly Not Authorized

- publishing or scheduling social content
- sending marketing email
- cold DM, Realtor outreach, or brokerage outreach
- Meta or TikTok paid activation
- promotional SMS
- portal or unrelated Reynalds OS changes
- production of the full 30-day content library before pilot learning

## Exit Decision

Stage 2A ends with one of three decisions:

- **Approve pilot publishing** — move only the approved assets to `Scheduled`.
- **Revise pilot package** — keep all assets unpublished until revision passes.
- **Hold activation** — retain assets as `Approved — Not Scheduled`.

No calendar date or historical launch date authorizes publication.

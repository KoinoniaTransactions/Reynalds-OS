# Koinonia Consultation Scorecard

Status: Active Supporting Tool  
Date: 2026-08-14  
Governing Source: `BRAIN/KOINONIA_CONSULTATION_INTERVIEW_SYSTEM.md`

## Purpose

Provide a two-layer consultation record:

1. a live consultation sheet that is quick enough to use while talking;
2. a post-consultation record completed immediately afterward for CRM and operational follow-through.

The scorecard supports judgment. It does not replace the canonical consultation method or current sales/CRM controls.

# Diagnosis Checkpoint

Before presenting Koinonia, all four should be answerable:

- What is the main pressure?
- What is the consequence?
- What do they want instead?

# B. Quick Fit Score

Use only as a decision aid.

Score each factor 0–2:

- 0 = weak / absent
- 1 = possible / unclear
- 2 = strong

Factors:

1. Clear operational need
2. Meaningful consequence
3. Koinonia scope fits problem
4. Willingness to delegate
5. Values process / communication
6. Respects professional boundaries
7. Realistic ability/timing to engage
8. Clear first engagement exists

Maximum: 16.

Suggested guide:

- 13–16: Strong Fit
- 8–12: Possible Fit
- 0–7: Weak Fit / Reassess

## Override Rule

Any serious licensing, professional-boundary, safety, brokerage, authorization, or relationship-standard problem can make the prospect a No Fit regardless of numerical score.

Never let the score override obvious judgment.

# D. One-Line Executive Summary

Every consultation should produce one sentence in this format:

"[Realtor] is a [business context] whose primary operational pressure is [problem], causing [consequence]. Koinonia recommended [service] beginning with [first engagement]. Current outcome is [status], with [person] responsible for [next action] by/on [date or trigger]."

The sentence should let an authorized Koinonia team member understand the opportunity later without reconstructing the entire meeting.

---

# E. 60-Second Post-Call Check

Before moving to the next appointment, confirm:

- Did I record the real problem?
- Did I record the consequence?
- Did I prescribe one starting point?
- Did I record the decision?
- Do I know exactly what happens next?

If yes, the consultation record is complete.

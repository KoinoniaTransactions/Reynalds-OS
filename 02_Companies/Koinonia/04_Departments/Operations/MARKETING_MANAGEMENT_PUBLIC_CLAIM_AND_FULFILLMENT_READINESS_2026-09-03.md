# 3. Advertising / Brokerage Control

Colorado Rule 6.10 applies to advertising of real-estate brokerage services, including websites, flyers, mailings, social media, and similar communications.

Koinonia must therefore operate as the Realtor/brokerage's marketing support provider, not as an independent source of unapproved brokerage claims.

## Koinonia may own

- marketing planning;
- content drafting;
- graphic production;
- scheduling/publishing workflow;
- campaign coordination;
- routine performance reporting;
- administrative optimization.

## Realtor/brokerage retains

- approval of material property facts;
- professional claims and representations;
- required brokerage identity/attribution;
- legal/compliance judgments;
- listing-price/status decisions;
- any claim requiring professional substantiation or brokerage approval.

Public-facing content must not imply Koinonia is the client's Brokerage Firm.

# 5. Account Access — GREEN WITH AUTHORIZATION

Preferred model:

- client remains owner/primary owner of business accounts;
- Koinonia receives manager/editor/agency/delegated access where available;
- each Koinonia user uses their own authorized account;
- access is removed promptly when engagement ends.

## Google Business Profile

Google permits business owners to add managers and authorized representatives. Koinonia should use manager/authorized-representative access rather than taking ownership away from the client or sharing account passwords.

Koinonia must not claim or verify a client's profile without express authorization and must keep the business owner informed about material changes.

---

# 6. Email / Newsletter Marketing — GREEN WITH COMPLIANCE CONTROLS

Recurring commercial email is approved when the sending workflow satisfies applicable CAN-SPAM requirements.

Operational requirements include:

- accurate From/Reply-To/header information;
- non-deceptive subject lines;
- clear commercial identity where required;
- valid physical postal address;
- functioning opt-out/unsubscribe method;
- suppression of opted-out recipients;
- timely honoring of opt-out requests;
- client/database ownership/source recorded;
- no use of harvested or obviously improper email lists;
- Koinonia and client understand that outsourcing the send does not eliminate the advertiser's compliance responsibility.

## Koinonia standard

Prefer permission-based, relationship-based, or client-owned databases even where broader commercial email may technically be lawful.

Purchased lists should not be part of the default Marketing Management workflow.

# 7. SMS / Text Marketing — YELLOW / SEPARATE CONSENT WORKFLOW

SMS/text-message marketing is **not part of the standard launch promise**.

Do not activate recurring promotional texting merely because a client supplies phone numbers.

Before Koinonia offers recurring SMS marketing, create a separate consent, opt-out, platform, recordkeeping, and applicable federal/state telemarketing compliance workflow.

Transactional appointment/service texts may be handled separately from promotional marketing according to the relevant client relationship and system rules.

# 8. Review / Referral Marketing — GREEN WITH AUTHENTICITY CONTROLS

Koinonia may support:

- review-request campaigns;
- past-client referral reminders;
- testimonial collection/organization;
- approved review reposting;
- client-story content with permission.

Do not:

- fabricate reviews;
- suppress or alter legitimate negative reviews in a misleading way;
- condition a requested review on being positive;
- make undisclosed compensated endorsement arrangements;
- publish private client information without permission.

Any referral-fee or incentive program involving settlement-service providers or other regulated arrangements requires separate review and is not implied by this marketing product.

- one recurring newsletter/email rhythm;
- routine listing/event integration;
- routine review/referral support;
- approved publishing to the agreed standard channels;
- concise monthly performance summary.

The exact monthly mix may adapt to listings, events and Realtor priorities.

This is a managed-capacity service, not unlimited creative production.

---

# 14. Approval Model

Use two approval layers.

## Standing-approved content

After onboarding, routine evergreen/brand content may use a standing approval model if the client/brokerage permits it.

## Explicit approval content

Require Realtor/brokerage approval before publication when content contains:

- new/material property facts;
- pricing/status/offer/concession information;
- legal/compliance-sensitive statements;
- claims about performance/results/credentials not already approved;
- paid-ad campaign claims/budgets;
- sensitive client/testimonial information;
- anything the client's brokerage requires for pre-approval.

Koinonia should maintain approval evidence in the client/campaign record.

---

# 15. Reporting Standard

Monthly report should be concise and decision-oriented, not vanity-metric-heavy.

Include where available:

- work/content published;
- reach/impressions/engagement trend;
- email delivery/open/click trend where useful;
- lead/conversion signals that can actually be attributed;
- review/referral activity;
- listing/event marketing completed;
- notable wins/friction;
- recommended next actions.

Do not represent correlation as guaranteed causation.

---

# 16. Outside Expense Rule

Additional only when required and approved:

- ad/media spend;
- printing/postage;
- professional photography/video/drone;
- premium production vendors;
- specialty client-specific software;
- paid stock/licensing where specifically required;
- other approved third-party marketing costs.

Koinonia earns its normal margin through the visible management/service fee by default, not hidden outside-vendor markups.

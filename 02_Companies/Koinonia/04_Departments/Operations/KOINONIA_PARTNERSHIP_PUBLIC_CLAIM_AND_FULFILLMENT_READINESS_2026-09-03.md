**Koinonia Partnership is approved for controlled public launch at $1,250/month.**

# 2. Standard Included Foundation

The standard Partnership includes the approved Marketing Management relationship plus agreed recurring operations such as:

- CRM/pipeline maintenance;
- contact organization, tags, stages and source maintenance;
- next-action/follow-up tracking;
- stalled-contact identification;
- task/calendar administration;
- past-client/review/referral workflow administration;
- routine communication drafting and approved sending;
- vendor/business coordination;
- checklist/template/SOP upkeep;
- basic approved workflow/automation administration;
- recurring operations brief/review;
- priority support relationship and continuity.

The exact mix is established during onboarding and may evolve through recurring review without turning the product into unlimited task access.

---

# 3. Client Onboarding / Access Gate

Before recurring operations begin, capture:

- Realtor and Brokerage Firm identity;
- primary contacts/escalation path;
- systems used by the Realtor;
- approved user/access method for each system;
- communication authority;
- CRM/data ownership/source;
- vendor/payment authority;
- recurring tasks/cadence;
- professional decisions that always require Realtor approval;
- spending limits/approval rules;
- privacy/confidentiality expectations;
- offboarding/access-removal procedure.

## Access standard

Prefer delegated/user-specific access over shared passwords wherever the system supports it.

Koinonia should not take ownership of the Realtor's core business accounts or data unless that is explicitly required and approved for a specific system.

Access should be removed promptly when the relationship ends or a team member no longer needs it.

# 5. Follow-Up Administration — GREEN WITH APPROVED COMMUNICATION PROCESS

Koinonia may:

- create and track follow-up tasks;
- prepare approved templates/drafts;
- send routine administrative messages where authorized;
- schedule consultations/appointments;
- track responses and next actions;
- escalate substantive client questions to the Realtor;
- administer past-client/referral/review touchpoints.

Koinonia does not independently:

- negotiate;
- provide real-estate advice;
- choose contract terms;
- answer legal/compliance questions;
- make promises/concessions on the Realtor's behalf;
- create a Brokerage Relationship merely through follow-up communication.

Marketing email/SMS rules remain governed by the Marketing Management readiness standard.

Large custom software development, complex integrations, or major implementation projects may require a separate project quote.

# 9. Data / Confidentiality Controls

The Partnership may expose Koinonia to client/contact information and business systems.

Operational controls:

- use access only for the approved business purpose;
- do not export/copy databases unnecessarily;
- do not reuse one client's data for another client;
- limit access to personnel/providers who need it;
- avoid storing credentials in ordinary notes/messages;
- remove access on offboarding;
- honor client instructions regarding confidential business/client information;
- escalate suspected unauthorized access/data exposure immediately.

A formal privacy/security policy should continue to mature as system integrations expand, but this does not block controlled launch of ordinary CRM/business-operations support.

# 10. Capacity / Scope Standard

The product is sold as a managed relationship, not an hourly bank.

Working internal launch envelope:

- Marketing Management component: approximately 7–9 efficient equivalent hours/month;
- operations component: approximately 6–8 efficient equivalent hours/month;
- total normal managed capacity: approximately 13–17 efficient equivalent hours/month.

These numbers are internal management targets, not public guaranteed hours.

If a client repeatedly exceeds normal capacity:

1. document the recurring overage pattern;
2. identify whether the work is temporary, project-based, or a permanently larger operating need;
3. review scope/capacity with the client;
4. adjust the relationship or quote the materially different work before proceeding;
5. do not create dozens of surprise micro-fees.

---

# 11. Separate Products Remain Separate

By default, Partnership does not include unlimited use of:

- Transaction Management;
- Hand Us the Listing;
- Licensed Field Coverage;
- Professional Open Houses;
- large custom projects;
- outside expenses.

Partnership clients still receive an integrated experience: Koinonia already knows the client, systems, brand, workflows and context, so other Koinonia services should require less re-briefing and duplicate setup.

Do not introduce partner discounts/credits at launch until actual usage data shows they improve retention/economics.

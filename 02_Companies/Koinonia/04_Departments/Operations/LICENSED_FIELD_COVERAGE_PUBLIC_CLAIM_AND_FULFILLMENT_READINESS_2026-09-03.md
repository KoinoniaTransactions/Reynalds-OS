## Decision

**Licensed Field Coverage is approved for controlled public launch at the current working prices, with strict ministerial/REAP boundaries and client-specific authorization controls.**

Approved public launch categories:

1. buyer showing coverage;
2. professional open-house hosting;
3. inspection/appraisal/media/stager/vendor/contractor access;
4. approved property-access/documentation tasks;
5. approved lockbox/key/sign tasks when property, brokerage, MLS, and access authority permit;
6. vacation/overflow field coverage using the same approved assignment types.

The product is **not** an independent brokerage-representation service.

Koinonia's standard assignment fee compensates Koinonia for an approved administrative/REAP field assignment. If an assignment crosses into Real Estate Brokerage Services, the standard field assignment model stops and the required brokerage relationship, supervision, compensation, and other licensing rules control.

---

# 1. Internal Provider Standard

Colorado Commission Position 20 permits an unlicensed REAP or a licensed REAP without a Brokerage Relationship to provide access to a property and conduct showings or open houses when authorized by the seller or listing Broker, provided the person remains within the permitted administrative/ministerial lane.

Koinonia will use a stricter commercial standard for the most client-facing assignments:

## Active-license-required assignments

Koinonia should require an **active Colorado real-estate broker license** for:

- buyer showing coverage;
- multi-property buyer tours;
- professional open-house hosting;
- any field assignment where a consumer is expected to interact substantially with the Koinonia provider.

The provider's license does **not** create a Brokerage Relationship with the consumer and does not authorize the provider to give advice or negotiate on behalf of the consumer under the standard Koinonia assignment.

## Access-only / property-task assignments

For inspection, appraisal, photographer, stager, contractor, lockbox, key, sign, documentation, or similar access-only tasks, Koinonia may later determine that an unlicensed trained REAP is appropriate where Colorado law, brokerage policy, property authority, MLS/lockbox rules, insurance, and the specific task permit it.

At launch, use licensed providers whenever there is uncertainty.

# 2. Colorado REAP Operating Basis

Current Colorado Commission Position 20 states that an unlicensed REAP or a licensed REAP without a Brokerage Relationship may, among other administrative tasks:

- distribute objective property information prepared by the Broker;
- gather listing information;
- if authorized by the seller or listing Broker, provide property access and conduct showings or open houses;
- complete administrative tasks helping Brokers fulfill their duties;
- deliver paperwork;
- schedule property repairs/services when the Broker has authority.

Commission Position 20 also requires care when a licensed REAP begins performing licensed brokerage duties. If the licensed REAP negotiates, prepares contracts, or otherwise performs Real Estate Brokerage Services on behalf of a principal, the appropriate Brokerage Relationship must be established.

## Koinonia rule

The standard Licensed Field Coverage product is designed to remain in the **administrative/ministerial REAP lane**.

The provider may:

- provide authorized property access;
- conduct the approved showing/open house;
- distribute approved objective property information;
- follow Realtor instructions;
- document observations and consumer feedback without adding professional conclusions;
- collect visitor/lead information;
- escalate questions or decisions to the requesting Realtor.

The provider may not under the standard assignment:

- recommend whether a buyer should purchase the property;
- advise on price or offer strategy;
- negotiate;
- prepare a contract or amendment;
- interpret contractual/legal rights;
- make inspection/property-condition conclusions;
- create a new Brokerage Relationship with the consumer;
- make decisions reserved to the requesting Realtor/brokerage.

# 3. Compensation Boundary

Colorado Commission Position 8 states that a Broker may not accept commission or valuable consideration for performing Real Estate Brokerage Services except from the Broker's Brokerage Firm.

Therefore:

## Standard Koinonia field fee

The normal **$75+ assignment fee / $200 open-house fee** applies to the controlled administrative/REAP coverage assignment described in this product.

## If the assignment becomes brokerage service

If the requesting Realtor wants the Koinonia provider to perform actual Real Estate Brokerage Services rather than ministerial coverage:

1. do not simply add a Koinonia surcharge;
2. stop and identify the required Brokerage Relationship;
3. identify the provider's Brokerage Firm and supervision requirements;
4. ensure compensation follows the applicable brokerage-firm rules;
5. use a separately approved operating model before work proceeds.

The public Field Coverage product must not be used to route brokerage-service compensation around a Brokerage Firm.

# 4. Required REAP Disclosure

Commission Position 20 states that an unlicensed REAP or a licensed REAP without a Brokerage Relationship should promptly disclose that the person is not acting as a Broker in the transaction and identify the Broker for whom the REAP works.

Koinonia should standardize that disclosure.

## Working verbal disclosure for consumer-facing assignments

> **I'm here today providing coverage on behalf of [Requesting Realtor] for this appointment. I'm not establishing a separate brokerage relationship with you or replacing your Realtor. Questions about the property, pricing, terms, strategy, or representation should go back to [Requesting Realtor].**

This can be shortened naturally in context but the meaning must remain.

## Open-house host disclosure

At an open house, the Koinonia host should clearly operate on behalf of the listing Realtor/brokerage for the limited event assignment and should not imply that Koinonia is the listing brokerage.

For unrepresented visitors:

- collect contact information under the approved lead-capture process;
- provide objective Realtor-approved property information;
- route substantive questions/representation requests to the listing Realtor;
- do not independently establish a buyer Brokerage Relationship during the Koinonia host assignment unless a separate compliant brokerage model has been intentionally established.

# 6. Access / Security Standard

No field assignment proceeds without confirmed lawful access.

Required controls:

- use the showing service specified by the listing when scheduling a showing;
- verify the appointment and access instructions before dispatch;
- do not bypass listing-broker showing restrictions;
- do not use another person's MLS/showing/lockbox credentials in violation of platform rules;
- use only the provider's authorized credentials/access method;
- do not copy, retain, or redistribute access codes except as necessary for the authorized assignment;
- secure the property before leaving;
- report access/security irregularities immediately.

REcolorado guidance emphasizes that showing services and listing access instructions must be followed and warns that third-party verification or use of the wrong showing service can create security problems.

# 8. Professional Open-House Coverage — GREEN / CONTROLLED LAUNCH

## Approved public promise

> **Professional open-house coverage when you need the property hosted without giving up the seller relationship or lead handoff.**

## Standard event

Working price: **$200 per standard event**.

Working standard event includes:

- up to approximately three hours of hosting;
- normal local travel within accepted assignment economics;
- basic setup/teardown;
- approved property materials/information;
- visitor registration/lead capture;
- basic property security/access management;
- event completion summary;
- lead handoff to requesting Realtor.

## Host boundaries

Host may:

- welcome visitors;
- provide approved objective property information;
- collect visitor information;
- record questions/interest;
- communicate event logistics;
- route prospects to the listing Realtor.

Host does not independently:

- negotiate;
- discuss seller motivation beyond approved objective facts;
- advise on offer price/strategy;
- promise terms/concessions;
- establish representation with visitors under the Koinonia event assignment;
- make unapproved claims about property condition/material facts.

## Listing-status gate

Before hosting, confirm the property is in a status that permits an open house under the applicable MLS/listing rules.

REcolorado currently prohibits showings/open houses in Coming Soon status and Withdrawn status; Private Exclusive restrictions also require special care.

# 10. Property Documentation / Status Visits — YELLOW

Permitted only with clear written instructions and authority.

Examples:

- take current-condition photos for the requesting Realtor;
- confirm visible completion of a specifically identified repair without opining on quality;
- confirm property appears occupied/vacant when requested;
- visually confirm a specified item is present/removed;
- document obvious access/security concern.

Provider must not convert the assignment into:

- an inspection;
- a property-condition report with professional conclusions;
- property-management services;
- recurring surveillance/security service;
- repair certification;
- legal occupancy determination.

Any recurring property-management-style assignment requires a separate service/legal review.

# 12. Final Walk-Through — RED / NOT STANDARD PUBLIC PRODUCT

Do not advertise **final walk-through representation** as part of standard Field Coverage.

Reason:

A final walk-through commonly involves client questions about property condition, contract compliance, repairs, possession, remedies, and whether to proceed. Those issues can quickly require brokerage judgment or advice.

Possible future narrow model:

- access-only escort/documentation under highly specific written instructions;
- requesting Realtor remains actively available for all decisions/advice;
- brokerage approval and compensation model confirmed.

Until that operating model is separately approved, public claim remains gated.

---

# 13. Closing Attendance / Document Review — RED

Do not advertise generic “closing coverage” as standard Licensed Field Coverage.

The following are not included:

- explaining closing documents;
- reviewing legal/title/loan documents for the consumer;
- advising the consumer whether to sign;
- resolving closing disputes;
- representing the Realtor/client at closing as a substitute broker.

A purely logistical presence may be evaluated later, but it is not part of the launch product.

---

# 14. Disputes / Negotiation / Repair Resolution — RED

A field provider encountering a dispute must document and escalate.

Examples:

- buyer says repair is unacceptable;
- seller/occupant refuses access;
- inspector identifies a serious issue;
- buyer wants to negotiate from the property;
- possession condition differs from expectation;
- consumer asks whether they should proceed/terminate/object.

Standard response:

1. do not negotiate;
2. do not interpret contract rights;
3. document what occurred;
4. contact requesting Realtor;
5. remain only if safe and instructed;
6. close/escalate assignment as directed.

---

# 15. Safety Standard

Provider safety and property security take priority over completion.

Before assignment:

- verify identity/context supplied by client;
- confirm property/access;
- review known occupants/pets/hazards;
- confirm contact/escalation path;
- avoid assignments with unresolved safety concerns.

During assignment:

- do not force entry;
- do not confront occupants;
- do not enter visibly unsafe areas;
- leave/escalate if conditions become unsafe;
- call emergency services when appropriate.

After assignment:

- confirm property securement;
- document material access/safety issue;
- report immediately where required.

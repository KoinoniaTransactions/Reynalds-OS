# Koinonia Realtor Capability Catalog — Working Master

Date: 2026-09-02  
Status: Working Master / Pre-Certification  
Owner: Koinonia  
Purpose: Maintain the broad inventory of work a Realtor may reasonably ask Koinonia to handle, coordinate, or manage under the white-glove one-stop support model.

> This catalog is broader than the current production-certified Service Catalog. Inclusion here does **not** automatically authorize public advertising, pricing, or fulfillment. Each capability must move through operational, pricing, legal/licensing, insurance, vendor, and service-standard review before production certification.

## Strategic Rule

Koinonia should keep the public service architecture simple while making the depth of support obvious.

The practical customer question is:

> **What can I hand to Koinonia instead of doing it myself or finding another vendor?**

The desired answer is: nearly any legitimate operational need around running and serving a Realtor's business, subject to clear legal, brokerage, professional-responsibility, capacity, and economic boundaries.

## Client-Facing Umbrellas

1. Transactions & Contracts
2. Listings & Seller Operations
3. Licensed Field Coverage
4. Marketing & Growth
5. CRM, Client & Business Operations

Vacation / Overflow Coverage and other cross-service solutions should be sold as packages or outcomes rather than additional foundational boxes.

# Fulfillment Tags

**KD — Koinonia Direct**  
Performed by Koinonia operations resources under an approved SOP.

**LC — Licensed Coverage**  
Requires or is best fulfilled by an appropriately licensed/authorized real-estate professional under the approved brokerage/compensation model.

**MS — Managed Specialist**  
Koinonia owns coordination and quality control while an approved specialist/vendor performs the technical work.

**AB — Agent/Broker Gate**  
Koinonia may prepare, organize, coordinate, or execute instructions, but final judgment, advice, approval, supervision, negotiation, or authority remains with the responsible Realtor/brokerage.

**RG — Regulatory / Operating Gate**  
Do not publicly promise until the operating/legal/insurance/authority model has been specifically approved.

A capability can have multiple tags.

# Maturity Tags

**CERTIFIED** — Supported by an existing production-certified service object.  
**CURRENT** — Already represented in current public/working Koinonia materials but may require canonical reconciliation.  
**DESIGN** — Approved strategic direction; scope/SOP/pricing still needs certification.  
**PARTNER** — Intended capability primarily through a Koinonia-managed specialist/vendor.

# Marketing / Advertising Compliance Controls

For Colorado clients, Realtor advertising remains the responsibility of the broker and brokerage firm. Koinonia marketing workflows must therefore include an approval/compliance layer for client-facing advertising.

Operational rules should include:

- preserve the client's brokerage legal/trade name and required disclosures;
- use only agent/broker-approved claims and property information;
- maintain approval records for public advertising where appropriate;
- remove or update expired/changed listing advertising promptly when required;
- verify rights/permissions for photography, video, music, logos, testimonials, reviews, and other assets;
- obtain appropriate permission before interior property photography/video outside normal listing-media authorization;
- do not fabricate awards, production claims, testimonials, results, scarcity, offers, or property facts;
- clearly separate ad spend, specialist/vendor cost, and Koinonia service fees in pricing/accounting;
- preserve source/lead attribution and consent requirements for email/SMS/paid campaigns.

# Capability Activation Workflow

Before a DESIGN/PARTNER/GATED capability becomes production-certified:

1. Confirm client need/use case.
2. Define exact scope and exclusions.
3. Determine fulfillment tag(s).
4. Confirm licensing/brokerage/legal requirements.
5. Confirm insurance/vendor requirements.
6. Define intake fields.
7. Define service standard / turnaround.
8. Define completion evidence.
9. Define escalation path.
10. Determine unit economics and pricing rule.
11. Determine whether included in a package or standalone.
12. Build portal/work-queue support.
13. Update canonical service object(s).
14. Update website/sales copy.
15. Only then activate paid advertising claims.

# Strategic End State

Koinonia should become the operations layer behind the Realtor's business:

> **One trusted relationship for transactions, contracts, listings, licensed field coverage, marketing, CRM, client follow-through, and the day-to-day systems that keep a real estate business moving.**

The public message should communicate capability without overwhelming the Realtor with internal categories.

The operating system should make every request visible, assignable, measurable, billable, and repeatable regardless of whether fulfillment is Koinonia Direct, Licensed Coverage, or a Koinonia-Managed Specialist.

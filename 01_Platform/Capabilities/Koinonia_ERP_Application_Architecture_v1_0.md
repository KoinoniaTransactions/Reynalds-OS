# ROS-0038 — Koinonia ERP v1.0 Enterprise Application Architecture

## Vision

Koinonia ERP is an Operations Platform for Real Estate Professionals. Transaction coordination is Version 1, but the platform is designed to expand over time without changing its core architecture.

## Design Philosophy

Every screen should answer one of four questions:

1. What do I need to know?
2. What do I need to do?
3. Who needs something from me?
4. What should happen next?

## Core Modules

### Home Dashboard
Daily executive snapshot, priorities, activity, risk, revenue, and AI recommendations.

### CRM
Relationship engine for leads, agents, brokerages, referral partners, vendors, and clients.

### Transactions
Core transaction workspace with overview, timeline, deadlines, documents, communication, QA, exceptions, financials, and activity log.

### Contract Center
Draft requests, urgent work, revisions, completed drafts, versions, and archives.

### Showing Center
Today's showings, upcoming showings, unassigned requests, provider availability, feedback, and revenue.

### Operations
Internal work engine with queues for new work, waiting, ready, QA, and follow-up.

### Finance
Packages, pricing, invoices, payments, revenue, profitability, outstanding balances, and forecasting.

### Customer Success
Reviews, testimonials, referral opportunities, past client follow-ups, relationship health, and client anniversaries.

### Knowledge
Searchable repository for SOPs, playbooks, templates, policies, training, engineering principles, and certified objects.

### Reports
Executive reports for revenue, transactions, quality, capacity, marketing, customer success, and repository health.

### Administration
Users, roles, permissions, packages, pricing, templates, automations, integrations, and branding.

### ROS
Object Explorer, Change Log, Roadmap, Version History, Repository Health, Development Journal, and AI Command Center.

## Engineering Principle #27

Every screen should reduce work, reduce uncertainty, or improve decision-making. If it does none of those, it should not exist.

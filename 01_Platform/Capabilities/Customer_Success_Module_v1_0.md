# ROS-0052 — Koinonia Customer Success Module v1.0

## Purpose

Build the Customer Success module inside the interactive Koinonia ERP application shell.

## Application File

`07_Application_Prototypes/ROS_Koinonia_Interactive_App_Shell_v5_0.html`

## Included Sections

- Customer Success KPI cards
- Success Pipeline
- Review & Testimonial Queue
- Referral Opportunities
- Relationship Nurture Calendar
- Create Success Task panel
- AI Recommendation
- Customer Success Health
- Success Rule

## Core Function

The Customer Success module turns completed work into reviews, testimonials, referrals, repeat business, and long-term relationship value.

## Principle

Operations ends at closing. Relationship management begins after closing. The transaction is finite; the relationship is continuous.

# Workspace Customization

## Purpose

Allow ROS to support multiple business units, brands, and operating environments from one platform.

## Initial Workspaces

- Koinonia
- Environmental
- Family
- Executive
- Future Companies

## Workspace Settings

- Branding
- Modules enabled
- Object types enabled
- Package catalog
- Pricing catalog
- Workflows
- Templates
- Automations
- Permissions
- Dashboards
- Reporting views

## Rule

The platform should be shared. The workspace experience should be specialized.

# Permission-Aware Navigation

## Purpose

Ensure each user sees only the modules, objects, commands, and actions appropriate to their role.

## Role-Based Examples

### Owner / Executive
All modules, reports, settings, and repository controls.

### Operations Manager
Operations, transactions, QA, workflows, tasks, and reports.

### Transaction Coordinator
Assigned transactions, tasks, documents, communications, and QA.

### Contract Support
Contract Center, draft requests, templates, terms, and versions.

### Showing Provider
Assigned showings, access instructions, safety notes, and feedback submission.

### Finance
Invoices, payments, packages, revenue reports, and finance workflows.

### Customer Success
Reviews, referrals, testimonials, and relationship nurture.

## Rule

Navigation should reveal responsibility and hide unnecessary complexity.

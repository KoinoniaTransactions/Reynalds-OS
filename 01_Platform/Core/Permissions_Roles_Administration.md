# Permissions, Roles & Administration Foundation

## Purpose

Define who can see, edit, approve, and administer ROS objects.

## Initial Roles

### Owner / Executive
Full system access.

### Operations Manager
Access to operations, transactions, tasks, QA, and workflows.

### Transaction Coordinator
Access to assigned transactions, documents, communication, and tasks.

### Contract Support
Access to drafting requests, documents, terms, versions, and delivery templates.

### Showing Provider
Limited access to assigned showing requests, instructions, safety notes, and feedback.

### Finance
Access to packages, invoices, payments, revenue reports, and financial objects.

### Customer Success
Access to reviews, testimonials, referral opportunities, relationship follow-ups.

### Read-Only / Viewer
Can view approved information but cannot modify operational objects.

## Permission Types

- View
- Create
- Edit
- Delete
- Archive
- Approve
- Assign
- Export
- Administer

## Administration Areas

## Rule

Access should match responsibility.

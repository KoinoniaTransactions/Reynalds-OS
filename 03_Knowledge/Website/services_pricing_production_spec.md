# Services & Pricing Production Specification

## Status

Strategy approved. Repository implementation pending verification.

This file records the approved strategy for the Koinonia Services & Pricing page. It does not certify implementation until real repository files pass the website production workflow.

## Mission

Help Realtors understand Koinonia as a long-term operational partner, not merely a transaction coordination vendor.

## Page Journey

1. Hero — Create immediate clarity.
2. Trust — Explain why Realtors can trust Koinonia.
3. Services — Show the four operational support categories.
4. How It Works — Make the process feel simple.
5. Support Levels — Present support fit before price comparison.
6. FAQ — Remove final hesitation.
7. CTA — Make the next step easy.

## Approved Sections

### Hero

Purpose: make a Realtor keep reading.

Approved formula:

- eyebrow
- concise headline
- short supporting paragraph
- one primary CTA
- approved professional workspace/computer image when used

### Trust

Purpose: answer "Why should I trust Koinonia?"

Approved pillars:

- Organized Processes
- Proactive Communication
- Dependable Partnership
- Detail-Driven Execution

### Services

Purpose: answer "Can Koinonia support how I do business?"

Approved categories:

- Transaction Management
- Contract Preparation & Writing
- Licensed Showing Coverage
- Business Support

### How It Works

Purpose: remove uncertainty.

Messaging rule: write from the Realtor's perspective using "You..." language before "We..." language.

### Support Levels

Purpose: help Realtors identify the support that fits their business.

Presentation rule: lead with business need and support fit; price is supporting information, not the headline.

### FAQ

Purpose: remove final hesitation.

Rule: a question belongs in the FAQ only if answering it increases the likelihood of contacting Koinonia.

### Final CTA

Purpose: remove friction.

Tone: calm confidence; no urgency pressure. Brand feeling: "When you're ready, we're ready."

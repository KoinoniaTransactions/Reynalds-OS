# Koinonia Brand & Marketing System v2

Status: Active Canonical Direction

The Koinonia client-acquisition system is organized around a service-first real estate operations brand.

## Core Positioning

- Category: Real Estate Operations Support for Colorado Realtors.
- Promise: **You focus on your clients. We'll keep the business running behind you.**
- Tagline: **Real Estate Operations. Elevated.**
- Koinonia sells dependable real estate operations support; the portal is a delivery, communication, visibility, and work-completion tool, not the product.
- Marketing hierarchy: outcome first, mechanism second, boundaries third.

## Current Service Architecture

Five core Koinonia Transactions service lanes:

1. Transaction Support / Contract-to-Close Coordination
2. Contract & Document Support
3. Licensed Showing Coverage
4. Professional Open House Coverage
5. Monthly Operations Partnership

The **40% Referral Partner Option** is a separate brokerage referral path, not a sixth Transactions service.

## Key Marketing Decisions

- The Brokerage Introduction Sheet is the primary leave-behind.
- The Introduction Sheet should lead with the Realtor's benefit, not merely a list of services.
- Detailed pricing should be separated into a Service Guide or Pricing Insert.
- The gold K inside a thin circle is the canonical print brand mark.
- Text must not overlap the brand mark.
- Print materials should use generous space, clear hierarchy, restrained gold accents, and the black/warm-white/gold visual system.
- Koinonia is presented as a real estate operations partner, not software, a generic virtual assistant, or a replacement brokerage.
- Professional boundaries must remain visible.
- The faith and Koinonia-meaning treatments should remain intentional without overwhelming the practical business message.
- The Referral Partner Option should be obvious enough to discover while remaining visually and conceptually separate from the five core service lanes.
- Public referral copy may state the 40% qualifying closed-business referral fee, but detailed settlement/brokerage mechanics belong in the formal referral documents before client handoff.

## Website Hero Governance

- Hero imagery remains bright, light, airy, organized, professional, and premium through restrained cream/black/gold visual language.
- Marketing copy remains live HTML/CSS rather than being baked into the image.
- Brand consistency comes from the shared visual system, not identical laptop orientation.
- Most heroes should emphasize physical professional workspace cues and may show the back or side of technology.
- A front-facing screen is allowed when seeing the workflow materially strengthens the service story.
- Front-facing screens must remain occasional so Koinonia never begins to look primarily like a software company.
- The approved Referral Partner hero is the canonical precedent for this controlled exception.
- Do not create more front-facing-screen heroes merely to make that image appear consistent; use them only when the service story calls for them.

## Release/Continuity Reminder

The public website, `/jeremiah` digital business card, Referral Partner page, and portal are separate release concerns. `koinonia-production` is the source of truth for what is live. Portal-development work must be reconciled with current production before eventual release; it must never overwrite the cumulative public-site line.

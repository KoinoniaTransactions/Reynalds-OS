# Koinonia Brand & Marketing System v2

Status: Active Canonical Direction

The Koinonia client-acquisition print system is now organized as a cohesive brokerage introduction system.

## Current Build Order

1. Business Card — approved
2. Brokerage Introduction Sheet — active priority
3. Service Guide
4. Pricing Insert
5. Digital Introduction Packet
6. Tri-fold Brochure
7. Presentation Folder

## Key Decisions

- The Brokerage Introduction Sheet is the primary leave-behind.
- The Introduction Sheet should lead with the Realtor's benefit, not merely a list of services.
- Detailed pricing should be separated into a Service Guide or Pricing Insert.
- The gold K inside a thin circle is the canonical print brand mark.
- Text must not overlap the brand mark.
- Print materials should use generous space, clear hierarchy, restrained gold accents, and the black/warm-white/gold visual system.
- Koinonia is presented as a real estate operations partner.
- Professional boundaries must remain visible.
- The faith and Koinonia-meaning treatments should remain intentional without overwhelming the practical business message.

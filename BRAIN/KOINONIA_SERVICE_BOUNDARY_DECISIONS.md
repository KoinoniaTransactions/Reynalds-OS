# Koinonia Service Boundary Decisions

Status: Finalized Working Defaults v1  
Owner: Koinonia Transactions  
Purpose: Define the working boundaries for Koinonia services before additional website pricing, package, or visual refinements are made.

---

## 1. Guiding Principle

Koinonia should be positioned as a real estate operations partner, not a generic virtual assistant service or unlimited task bucket.

The service model should create clarity for Realtors while protecting Koinonia’s capacity, professionalism, and boundaries.

---

## 2. Public Pricing Posture

Current recommendation:

- Keep public starting prices
- Keep monthly tier pricing public
- Avoid publishing every possible rush fee, distance rule, mileage rule, or exception
- Confirm final scope after consultation

Public pricing should create trust and help Realtors self-qualify without making the company feel like a cheap task menu.

### Current Public Starting Points

- Transaction Support — starting at $389
- Contract & Document Support — starting at $75
- Licensed Showing Coverage — starting at $50
- Monthly Operations Partnership — starting at $299/month

---

## 3. Monthly Operations Partnership Rules

Monthly support is capacity-based and scope-based.

It should not be described or treated as unlimited assistant access.

### Monthly Tiers

Starter:

- $299/month
- Light recurring support
- Up to 3 hours/month

Growth:

- $599/month
- Consistent operating rhythm
- Up to 7 hours/month

Partner:

- $999/month
- Deeper operations partnership
- Up to 12 hours/month

---

## 4. Rollover Policy

Recommended default:

Monthly support hours do not roll over by default.

Reason:

- Keeps capacity predictable
- Prevents large hour backlogs
- Protects the business from surprise workload spikes
- Encourages consistent monthly use

Possible public wording:

> Monthly support is designed to create a steady operating rhythm. Unused monthly capacity does not roll over unless separately agreed in writing.

---

## 5. Additional Monthly Hours

If a monthly client needs more support than their plan includes, additional work may be available by agreement.

Recommended rule:

- Additional hours are subject to availability
- Additional work should be approved before it begins
- The rate should be higher than the effective monthly plan rate

Open decision:

- Final additional-hour rate

Suggested range:

- $75–$100/hour depending on support type and urgency

---

## 6. Rush Request Policy

Rush requests should be available only when capacity allows.

Recommended rule:

- Rush work is not guaranteed
- Rush work may require a premium fee
- Rush work should not override existing client obligations unless specifically accepted

Possible public wording:

> Rush requests may be available depending on timing, complexity, and current capacity.

Open decision:

- Final rush fee structure

---

## 7. Contract & Document Support Boundaries

Koinonia may support contract and document preparation based on Realtor instructions.

Koinonia does not replace:

- Realtor judgment
- Brokerage compliance
- Legal advice
- Negotiation strategy
- Client decision-making
- Final approval responsibility

Recommended public wording:

> Koinonia prepares documents based on Realtor instructions. Realtors remain responsible for client advice, negotiation decisions, brokerage compliance, legal questions, and final approval.

---

## 8. Showing Coverage Boundaries

Licensed Showing Coverage should remain practical and flexible.

Recommended default:

- Local showing coverage starts at $50
- Distance, access complexity, urgent timing, or extended showing time may change pricing
- Extended-distance showing coverage should be custom-priced
- Koinonia should confirm showing details before accepting the request

Open decisions:

- Local service radius
- Mileage policy
- Extended-distance pricing
- Minimum notice expectation

Suggested starting point:

- Local coverage: standard radius to be determined
- Extended coverage: custom quote based on distance, timing, and complexity

---

## 9. Transaction Support Boundaries

Transaction Support should be contract-to-close operations support.

Included work may include:

- Timeline setup
- Deadline tracking
- Document checklist support
- Lender/title/client communication support
- Milestone tracking
- Closing preparation
- File wrap-up

Not included unless separately agreed:

- Legal interpretation
- Brokerage compliance decisions
- Negotiation decisions
- Client-advisory decisions
- Work outside the agreed file scope

---

## 10. Monthly Operations Support Boundaries

Monthly Operations Partnership may include:

- CRM organization
- Pipeline/task tracking
- Client follow-up support
- Workflow and checklist support
- Light transaction prep
- Template/process cleanup
- Recurring business follow-through

Monthly Operations Partnership does not include:

- Unlimited administrative requests
- Personal errands
- Full-time assistant availability
- 24/7 response
- Legal advice
- Brokerage compliance decisions
- Marketing management unless separately scoped
- Work outside the approved monthly support focus

---

## 11. Response Expectations

Recommended public posture:

Koinonia typically responds within one business day.

Monthly retained clients receive clearer support rhythm based on tier.

### Suggested Internal Expectations

Starter:

- Monthly support rhythm
- Not priority response
- Best for light organization and follow-through

Growth:

- Biweekly support rhythm
- Priority over one-off requests
- Best for active Realtors needing consistency

Partner:

- Weekly support rhythm
- Highest priority among retained clients
- Best for producing Realtors or small teams needing deeper structure

---

## 12. Partner Tier Contract Support

Recommended default:

Partner tier should include discounted or priority contract support, but not unlimited contract writing at launch.

Reason:

Unlimited contract support could create unclear workload and capacity issues.

Open decision:

- Whether Partner includes a set amount of contract/document support
- Whether Partner receives a discount instead
- Whether urgent contract work is always separate

Recommended starting rule:

> Partner clients receive priority scheduling and preferred pricing for contract/document support, but contract work remains subject to scope and availability.

## 14. Finalized Working Defaults v1

The following decisions are approved as working defaults for launch planning and service operations.

### Monthly Rollover

Monthly support hours do not roll over by default.

Unused monthly capacity expires at the end of the month unless separately agreed in writing.

### Additional Monthly Hours

Additional monthly support is billed at $85/hour when accepted.

Additional work must be approved before it begins and remains subject to availability.

### Rush Requests

Rush requests are subject to availability and are never guaranteed.

When accepted, rush work may include a 50% premium with a minimum rush premium of $50.

### Showing Coverage Radius

Standard local showing coverage starts at $50 within a 20-mile operating radius.

The operating base should be treated as an internal business reference and does not need to be published as a precise address.

### Extended Showing Coverage

Showing coverage outside the standard local radius should be custom quoted based on:

- Drive time
- Mileage
- Timing
- Urgency
- Access complexity
- Expected showing duration

### Minimum Showing Notice

Preferred minimum notice for showing coverage is 24 hours.

Same-day requests may be accepted when capacity allows and may be treated as rush requests.

### Partner Tier Contract Support

Partner clients receive priority scheduling and preferred pricing for contract/document support.

Partner tier does not include unlimited contract writing.

Working default:

- Partner clients receive 20% preferred pricing on standard contract/document support when capacity allows
- Contract/document work remains subject to scope, timing, and availability
- Urgent contract/document work may still be treated as rush work

### Public Website Posture

The public website should continue to show starting prices and monthly tier pricing.

Detailed exception rules, rush pricing, distance adjustments, and mileage formulas should remain consultation-based for now.

# Koinonia Services & Pricing Blueprint

Status: Draft v1  
Owner: Koinonia Transactions  
Purpose: Define Koinonia’s service model, pricing structure, monthly support offer, boundaries, and culture before making additional website copy or pricing changes.

---

## 1. Business Positioning

Koinonia provides real estate operations support for Realtors who need dependable help behind the client relationship.

Koinonia is not positioned as a generic virtual assistant service. It is positioned as a real estate operations partner focused on transactions, contracts, showing coverage, workflow structure, communication support, and follow-through.

Core promise:

> Koinonia helps Realtors protect client service by organizing the operational work that competes for their time.

---

## 2. Primary Buying Paths

Koinonia should be organized around four clear buying paths:

1. Transaction Support
2. Contract & Document Support
3. Licensed Showing Coverage
4. Monthly Operations Partnership

These paths should remain clear and easy to understand. Avoid creating too many service categories that make the business feel scattered.

---

## 3. Transaction Support

### Positioning

Contract-to-close coordination for active files.

This is the clearest entry offer and should remain one of Koinonia’s core services.

### Included Support

- Contract-to-close timeline setup
- Deadline tracking
- Document checklist support
- Lender, title, client, and partner communication support
- Inspection, appraisal, and closing milestone tracking
- Brokerage file organization support
- Closing preparation and file wrap-up

### Suggested Pricing

- Prepaid Transaction Coordination: $389 per transaction
- Pay-at-Close Transaction Coordination: $599 per transaction

### Notes

This offer should be easy for Realtors to understand quickly. It can be sold as a standalone service or paired with contract/document support.

---

## 4. Contract & Document Support

### Positioning

Licensed document preparation support based on the Realtor’s instructions.

This is a premium differentiator because Koinonia is built with licensed real estate perspective.

### Included Support

- Offer preparation from Realtor instructions
- Amendments
- Addenda
- Notices
- Counterproposal document support
- Missing-information review
- Signature-ready document organization

### Suggested Pricing

- Single document support: $75–$125
- Offer package support: $150–$250
- Monthly add-on or included/discounted in higher monthly support tiers

### Boundary

Koinonia prepares documents based on the Realtor’s instructions. Koinonia does not replace the Realtor’s brokerage, legal, client-advisory, or decision-making responsibilities.

---

## 5. Licensed Showing Coverage

### Positioning

Licensed support when schedules, distance, or conflicts make it difficult for a Realtor to personally cover a showing.

### Included Support

- Licensed property access
- Showing appointment coordination
- Buyer showing support when Realtor is unavailable
- Feedback after showing
- Distance or schedule conflict coverage

### Suggested Pricing

- Local showing coverage: $50–$75 per showing
- Extended distance showing: custom / mileage-based
- Urgent showing request: premium add-on

### Notes

Showing coverage should remain practical and flexible. It should not be positioned as a large showing-platform replacement.

---

## 6. Monthly Operations Partnership

### Positioning

Ongoing real estate operations support for Realtors who need more structure, consistency, and follow-through without hiring a full-time assistant.

This should be a major Koinonia offer.

Monthly support should feel like:

> A steady operations partner for real estate business follow-through.

It should not feel like:

> Unlimited assistant work for anything an agent asks.

### Why Monthly Support Matters

Many Realtors do not only need help during active transactions. They also need support between transactions with organization, follow-up, systems, client communication, task tracking, and workflow consistency.

Monthly services create recurring value and help Koinonia become a long-term operations partner rather than only a transaction-by-transaction support provider.

---

## 7. Monthly Tier Structure

Recommended tier names:

- Starter
- Growth
- Partner

These names are clear and easy for Realtors to understand.

---

## 8. Starter Operations Support — $299/month

### Best For

Solo agents who need light structure and follow-through but do not need heavy weekly support.

### Included Support

- Up to 3 hours/month
- Monthly operations check-in
- CRM/task cleanup
- Client follow-up list support
- Basic checklist/template help
- Email/task organization support

### Positioning

Light operational support to help Realtors get organized and create more visible follow-through.

---

## 9. Growth Operations Support — $599/month

### Best For

Busy agents with active business who need consistent operational support without hiring staff.

### Included Support

- Up to 7 hours/month
- Biweekly operations support
- CRM and pipeline organization
- Client follow-up tracking
- Workflow/checklist support
- Light transaction prep
- Priority response over one-off requests
- Discounted contract support add-ons

### Positioning

Consistent operations support for Realtors who need help keeping tasks, clients, and business details moving.

### Notes

This may become the most popular monthly offer.

---

## 10. Partner Operations Support — $999/month

### Best For

Producing Realtors or small teams who need ongoing structure, accountability, and execution support.

### Included Support

- Up to 12 hours/month
- Weekly operations support
- CRM/pipeline management support
- Client follow-up systems
- Workflow documentation
- Listing/transaction admin support
- Priority scheduling
- Discounted or included contract preparation support
- Monthly business operations review

### Positioning

A deeper operations partnership for Realtors or teams who need dependable recurring support behind the business.

---

---

## 12. Exclusions and Boundaries

Koinonia should clearly avoid becoming unlimited general assistant work.

### Not Included Unless Separately Agreed

- Legal advice
- Brokerage compliance decisions
- Client decision-making
- Negotiation strategy decisions
- Unlimited administrative requests
- Personal errands
- Marketing management unless specifically scoped
- Full-time assistant availability
- 24/7 emergency response
- Work outside licensed or professional boundaries

### Professional Boundary Statement

Koinonia supports Realtors with operational execution, organization, communication support, and document preparation based on Realtor instructions. Realtors remain responsible for client advice, brokerage compliance, negotiation decisions, legal questions, and final approval.

---

## 13. Ideal Customer Profiles

### Busy Solo Realtor

Needs structure, transaction support, and help staying organized without hiring an employee.

### Growing Realtor

Has enough business that follow-up, paperwork, and systems are starting to slip.

### Producing Realtor or Small Team

Needs recurring operations support, defined systems, and a dependable partner behind the scenes.

### Realtor With Schedule Conflicts

Needs licensed showing coverage or support during busy client seasons.

## 14. Culture Standards

Koinonia should feel different from a generic transaction coordination or admin company.

### Culture Words

- Calm
- Clear
- Organized
- Faithful
- Professional
- Dependable
- Service-minded
- Stewardship-focused
- Partnership-driven
- Detail-oriented

### Culture Statement

Koinonia is built on partnership, service, and stewardship. We believe Realtors should be able to serve clients with confidence while knowing the details behind the transaction and business are being organized with care, clarity, and follow-through.

### Service Standard

Koinonia should communicate clearly, protect trust, respect boundaries, and keep the work moving with calm professionalism.

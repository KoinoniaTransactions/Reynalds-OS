## Verification Principle

Do not rush video verification with weak proof.

The video should truthfully show:

1. The business exists.
2. The user manages the business.
3. Koinonia operates as a real estate operations support business.
4. The workspace and materials match the public Google Business Profile.
5. No private client, tax, banking, family, or sensitive information is exposed.
